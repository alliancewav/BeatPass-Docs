## Still need help?

[Contact support](/help/contact-support/getting-help) or email [support@beatpass.ca](mailto:support@beatpass.ca) with the task you were trying to finish and what happened.

<Accordion title="What should I include?">
  Include your account email, relevant BeatPass link, exact error text, and browser or device. Add the purchase or certificate reference when it helps identify the record. A screenshot can help if it shows the problem clearly.
</Accordion>

Hide unrelated personal details, full card or bank numbers, passwords, tokens, authentication codes, and recovery codes before sharing an image. Explore [Help home](/help) if you want to find another guide while you wait.
