BeatPass administrators can review reported conversations and the context needed to investigate them. Use this guide to understand the review process as a participant; the review tools are restricted to authorized staff.

## What a report shares

A conversation report includes up to 50 recent messages and the details you submit. Authorized staff can inspect additional conversation history when needed. Your report itself is not shown to the other participants.

See [Report a conversation](/help/messaging/reporting-conversations) for the steps and the available report fields.

## What review can change

Staff can pause further messages in a conversation or reopen it when appropriate. Pausing does not delete its history. A report can be resolved or dismissed after review; submitting one does not automatically block a person or pause the chat.

## Manage your own inbox

Use **Archive conversation** if you want the thread out of your inbox. Archiving affects your own list and does not block another participant. Find it again in **Archive** and choose **Move to inbox**.

Keep passwords, payment credentials, and sensitive identity documents out of ordinary chats. Contact [BeatPass support](/help/contact-support) when an account or licensing issue needs a private support workflow.
