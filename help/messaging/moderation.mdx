Full administrators can use [Messages](https://open.beatpass.ca/admin/messages) to inspect conversation history, and [Reports](https://open.beatpass.ca/admin/moderation/reports) to review reported comments and conversations. These pages are restricted administrative tools.

## Review a Report

1. Open **Reports** and choose the comment or message view.
2. Filter by report status and open the record.
3. Review the reported content, surrounding context, and any additional details.
4. Choose the appropriate action and confirm it.

**Resolve report** records that a report has been handled. **Dismiss report** closes a report without that finding. Neither action alone is a substitute for changing the content or conversation's state.

## Take Action

| Content | Actions |
| --- | --- |
| **Conversation** | **Pause conversation** prevents further messages; **Reopen conversation** allows messaging again, subject to normal access rules |
| **Comment** | **Hide comment** removes it from public display; **Restore comment** makes it visible again |

Use **Messages** to find and inspect conversations even when they have not been reported. Review actions retain their moderation record; pausing a conversation does not delete its history.

User reports include up to 50 recent messages. The conversation history view lets an authorized administrator inspect additional context. Treat these exchanges as private user information.

See [Reporting conversations](/help/messaging/reporting-conversations) for the participant's workflow.
