Open the message icon in the top navigation, or visit [Messages](https://open.beatpass.ca/messages) for a larger workspace. Your conversation list and the open chat scroll separately, keeping the typing area within reach.

## Find a Conversation or Message

Use **Search conversations** in **Inbox** or **Archive** to find a conversation in that view. Inside a chat, use **Search in conversation** to find text in its history. Clear the search to return to the full thread.

## Conversation Actions

Open a chat's **More options** menu. On desktop, you can also hover a conversation in the list and select **Conversation options**.

| Action | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Pin conversation** | Keeps the conversation near the top of its list; **Unpin conversation** removes the pin |
| **Mark as read** | Clears your unread state |
| **Mark as unread** | Leaves a reminder and returns you to the conversation list |
| **Follow user / Unfollow user** | Changes your follow relationship in a direct chat; sending requires following |
| **Archive conversation** | Moves the chat out of your inbox without deleting its history |
| **Move to inbox** | Restores a chat from **Archive** |
| **Report conversation** | Sends a report to administrators for review |

## Archive and Restore

1. Choose **Archive conversation** from the conversation menu.
2. Open **Archive** above the conversation list when you want to find it again.
3. Choose **Move to inbox** to restore it.

Archiving affects your own list. Other participants keep their conversation, and a new message can return an archived chat to your inbox. Archiving does not block the other person.

## Read Reminders

Opening a chat marks its messages read. Use **Mark as unread** when you want to return later. Opening the chat again clears that reminder.
