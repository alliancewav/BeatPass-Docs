Bring your collaborators into one conversation for beat shortlists, playlist plans, and release ideas.

## Start a group

1. Select the **message icon** in the top navigation, then **New Message**.
2. Follow and select **2–10 other people**. BeatPass includes you in the group automatically.
3. Write your first message and select **Send Message**. On desktop, **Enter** also sends; on mobile, Return adds a line.

The group appears when you send your first message. Its title comes from the participants' names.

## Read and reply

Open the group from your conversation list. New replies appear while it is open, with typing indicators, unread badges and message status to help you keep up. If a message shows **Failed**, use its retry control.

Use [message notification preferences](/help/notifications/notification-settings) to choose the channels available for message alerts.

## Change the group

You cannot add or remove participants or rename a group from the messaging screen. Start a new conversation with the people you want included. A one-on-one side conversation is useful when the whole group does not need an update.

## Keep your inbox useful

[Pin important conversations or archive a quiet thread](/help/messaging/managing-conversations). Archiving changes your own inbox; it does not block participants or remove the conversation for everyone.
