## Open your inbox

Select the **message icon** in the top navigation. Open a recent chat, or choose **New Message** to find someone and start a conversation.

Use the panel's expand control for the full [Messages workspace](https://open.beatpass.ca/messages), with more room for longer conversations. On desktop, the conversation and inbox list scroll separately.

## Stay in the loop

The message icon shows an unread badge as replies arrive. Open **Notification Preferences → Messages** to review **Direct Messages** and **Playlist Invites**. [Notification settings](/help/notifications/notification-settings) explains the available channels.

## Keep conversations comfortable

Messages are not public posts. Authorized administrators can review conversations and reported exchanges for moderation. Keep passwords, payment credentials and sensitive identity documents out of ordinary chats.

To report abuse, open the conversation menu and choose **Report conversation**. Choose a reason, add relevant details and submit. You can archive the thread to move it out of your inbox. See [Report a conversation](/help/messaging/reporting-conversations) for what a report includes and what happens next.
