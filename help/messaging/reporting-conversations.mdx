Use **Report conversation** when a chat violates the [Content Guidelines](/help/legal/content-guidelines). Archiving alone does not send a report.

## Send a Report

1. Open the conversation's **More options** menu, or its desktop **Conversation options** menu.
2. Select **Report conversation**.
3. Choose a **Reason** and add **Additional details (optional)** when they help explain the issue.
4. Select **Submit report** and wait for confirmation.

Your report includes up to 50 recent messages for administrators to review. The report itself is not shared with the other participants. If submission fails, the dialog keeps your details so you can try again.

## After Reporting

Administrators can review the report and conversation, pause further messages, or reopen a conversation when appropriate. Submitting a report does not automatically pause the chat or block a participant.

You can use **Archive conversation** to move it out of your inbox. See [Managing conversations](/help/messaging/managing-conversations) to restore it later.
