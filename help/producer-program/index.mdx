Bring your beats to BeatPass with free producer access. Apply for a new producer profile or request access to an existing page that represents your music. Once approved, you can upload, choose licensing options, manage your catalog, and prepare payouts.

## Why Join?

<Columns cols={3}>
  <Card title="Build your catalog" icon="music" href="/help/uploading">Publish beats and organize collections with the allowance shown to your account.</Card>
  <Card title="Set your offer" icon="file-contract" href="/help/licensing-for-producers">Choose the available licensing model and the terms of an exclusive offer.</Card>
  <Card title="Keep your identity" icon="user" href="/help/profiles/producer-profiles">Give your music a public producer page with your branding and credits.</Card>
</Columns>

You keep ownership of your music while granting BeatPass the permissions in the [Producer Upload and Seller Agreement](/help/legal/producer-upload-seller-agreement). For an exclusive sale, the listed producer price is separate from a buyer platform fee or confirmed plan waiver. Read the offer and governing terms rather than treating a benefit headline as a payout guarantee.

## How It Works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the program page">
    Open [Producer Program](https://open.beatpass.ca/producer-program) and select **Apply to join**. Sign in or create an account if needed. An existing application shows **View application**; approved producers see **Open dashboard**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the right request">
    Use **Become a Producer** for a new profile or **Claim Existing Artist** for an existing page. **Get Verified** is a separate Discord review for people who already have producer access.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Introduce your music">
    Provide your real name, a public link to your beats, and the supporting material requested. Read the application agreement before submitting.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Follow the decision">
    Keep your existing application while it is **Pending**. After approval, open Producer Dashboard and use [After approval](/help/producer-program/after-approval) for your first tasks.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What You'll Need

Prepare your BeatPass account, real first and last name, and a public link to your beats. You need rights to the music and any samples, vocals, artwork, or AI-assisted elements you upload. Keep Proof of Rights available if requested.

Beats must be human-made, not fully AI-generated. [Requirements](/help/producer-program/requirements) explains the music and application checklist.

## Review the commitment

The application agreement asks for at least **2 beats per month**. Review that commitment before submitting. Producer access is free; your upload page shows any account allowance.

## Submission Rules

You can have one request under review at a time. If it is **Pending**, wait for a decision. If producer access was **Revoked**, [contact support](/help/contact-support) about an appeal.
