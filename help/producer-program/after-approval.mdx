Once your request is approved, you gain access to the Producer Dashboard and upload tools. This page covers the practical next steps so your account, catalog, and payouts are set up correctly.

<DashboardFigure />

## What Changes After Approval

Once your request is approved:

- Your account gains **producer permissions**
- You can upload beats and collections
- You can configure licensing for your tracks
- You become eligible for producer earnings
- You can set up payouts from **Finances**


## First Steps

### 1. Access Your Producer Dashboard

Open [Producer Dashboard](https://open.beatpass.ca/producer-dashboard) from your profile menu. Your workspace includes catalog, audience, and business tools such as **My Beats**, **My Collections**, **Audio Recon**, **Pro Page**, **Progress**, and **Finances**.

<Tip>
  The **Producer Dashboard** menu item only appears after approval when you have producer permissions.
</Tip>

### 2. Complete Your Producer Profile

Make your profile more trustworthy and easier to discover:

- Upload a high-quality profile picture
- Add a bio describing your style and sound
- Link your social accounts
- Add photos to your about section if you use them

### 3. Set Up Payouts

To receive earnings, complete the payout setup shown in **Finances**:

1. Open your producer **Finances** area
2. Choose **Set up BEATPAY** or **Upgrade to BEATPAY** when offered
3. Choose a bank account currency your bank can receive and enter the bank details requested for that currency

<Note>
  Approval opens the upload tools. Publishing a standard-licensed beat needs subscription payout readiness for every credited producer; exclusive offers need their own payout and offer checks. Follow the choices shown in the editor.
</Note>


## Upload Your First Beat

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the upload page">
    Start from your producer workspace and go to the upload tools.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add your beat file">
    Drag and drop or choose your audio file. The current producer upload flow only accepts **WAV** files.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the required fields">
    Add cover art, at least one genre, track name, artists, BPM, key, and scale. Add a Sample-Safe status and AI use declaration under **Protection & Verification**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a licensing model">
    Choose a licensing model and read any payout notice. You can save catalog settings before payout setup is complete; new customer downloads and purchases still need the relevant payout route. Exclusive offers also require a contact email, terms, WAV and stems delivery guarantee, and a current producer signature.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Wait for the fingerprint check to finish, then click **Save Track**.
  </Step>
</Steps>


## Build Your Catalog

### Cover Art Is Required

New tracks and new collections both need cover art before they can be saved. BeatPass supports custom uploads and genre-matched cover art presets.

### Complete the Core Metadata

For each beat, make sure you fill in:

- **Track Name**
- **Artists**
- **Key**
- **Scale**
- **BPM** (whole number, 60 to 200)

Descriptions, tags, and collection assignments are optional. Cover art, at least one genre, Sample-Safe status, AI declaration, and a completed fingerprint check are required for a new track.

### Use Collections Where It Helps

If you release beat packs, tapes, or themed drops, use **Create Collection** on the upload page. Collection tracks inherit the collection's artists, genres, and tags when they are first uploaded.


## Understand Your Earnings

As an approved producer, you can earn from:

- **Subscription contribution** when standard licensing is active for every credited producer
- **Exclusive sales** when buyers purchase your configured exclusive option

For exclusive sales, the buyer pays the platform fee on top of your listed price when it applies. Eligible active Pro buyers have it waived. Your producer finances area still records the full listed producer amount, sale, payout readiness, and payout setup status.


## XP You Start Earning Automatically

BeatPass currently awards:

| Action | XP |
| --- | --- |
| Upload an eligible track | +100, once per track |
| First counted play on an eligible track | +1, once per track |
| Complete an eligible track's details | +50, once per track |
| Credit an eligible collaboration | +25, once per track |

BeatPass tracks this automatically. You do not need to claim XP manually.


## Get Verified

If you want the verified badge and its benefits:

1. Go to the Producer Program page
2. Open [application options](https://open.beatpass.ca/backstage/requests)
3. On the request selector, choose **Get Verified**
4. Follow the Discord community verification process

Verification adds the profile badge and can make your credited beats eligible for Audio Recon. Eligible verified producers can receive alerts about new beat requests; review each brief yourself to decide whether it fits your music.

See [Get Verified](/help/producer-program/application-types/get-verified) for the complete process.


## Promote Your Beats

Once your beats are live:

- Share links to your profile and tracks on social media
- Add your BeatPass profile link to your bios
- Respond to beat requests that match your style
- Keep older tracks updated if you improve metadata or artwork
