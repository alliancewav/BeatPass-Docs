Apply for producer access with a public link to your best work, then follow the application status while the team reviews it. Producer access is free; earning also depends on your catalog and payout readiness.


## Overview

<Steps>
  <Step title="Apply">
    Choose the right request type and complete the required information.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review">
    The BeatPass team reviews your request for quality, legitimacy, and ownership.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start Earning">
    Once approved, use your producer tools to upload beats, manage your catalog, and connect payouts.
  </Step>
</Steps>


## Step 1: Start Your Application

1. Open [Producer Program](https://open.beatpass.ca/producer-program)
2. Choose **Apply to join** and sign in if needed
3. You arrive at the request selector

### Choose Your Application Type

| If you... | Choose... |
| --- | --- |
| Don't have a producer profile yet | **Start Application** |
| Have music on BeatPass but no account access | **Claim Profile** |
| Already have a producer profile and want verification | **Get Verified** |

<Warning>
The **Producer Dashboard** menu item in your profile dropdown requires existing producer access. It does not lead to the application page. Always use the Producer Program page to start applications.
</Warning>


## Step 2: Fill Out the Application

<ProducerApplicationFigure />

### Profile Setup

Depending on your request type:

- **Start Application**: Enter your **Producer Name**
- **Claim Profile**: Search for and select the producer profile you want to claim
- **Get Verified**: The screen shows **Meet us in the producer community** with an **Open Discord** button (no submission form)

For claim requests, use **Select Producer Profile** and wait for **This profile is available to claim.** If the profile is unavailable, choose another or contact support. If the check fails, choose **Try again** before submitting.

### Personal Details

- **First name** (required)
- **Last name** (required)

For **Claim Profile** requests, you also select your relationship to the profile (**Producer** or **Manager**).

### Supporting Materials

- **Link to your beats** (required for Become / Claim): a public URL where your music can be reviewed
- **Instagram handle** (optional)

**Add context (optional)** contains **Record label or company (optional)**, Instagram, and any enabled account-connection options. You can apply without connecting an account. Applications do not collect verification documents.

### Discord Community

Discord is a separate community handoff for **Get Verified**, not a step in the standard application. Joining does not automatically verify your profile; the team guides the review. Contact support if you need another way to reach the team.

### Agreement

Before submitting, you must confirm:

1. Agreement to the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy
2. Ownership of all beats you will upload
3. Authorizing BeatPass to host, distribute, issue licenses for, and verify your music under BeatPass terms
4. Commitment to uploading at least 2 beats per month

Your uploaded beats must be human-made and not fully AI-generated. Any AI-assisted material or AI samples must be commercially licensable through BeatPass and backed by Proof of Rights if requested.


## Step 3: Submit Your Application

Click **Submit Application** after completing the required fields and both agreement boxes. **Your beats are in review** confirms receipt. Return through **View application** on the Producer Program page to check its status. Your confirmation also shows the **Submitted listening link**.

<Note>
You can only have one active application under review at a time. If your current request is **Pending**, new submissions are blocked until a decision is made.
</Note>


## Step 4: Wait for Review

The BeatPass team reviews applications to check:

- Beat quality meets platform standards
- Information provided is accurate
- You are a legitimate producer or authorized representative

The submitted page shows an estimated review time of **1 to 3 business days**, but timing can vary.


## Step 5: Receive Your Decision

### If Approved

For a new producer profile or an approved **Producer** claim, the page shows **Welcome to the producer community** and offers **Open dashboard**. An approved **Manager** claim offers **Edit profile**. The action refreshes your account access before continuing. The decision notification explains your next steps. With producer access, you can:

- Start uploading beats and collections
- Complete payout setup from **Finances**
- Edit your profile and catalog
- Configure licensing for your tracks

See [After Approval](/help/producer-program/after-approval) for next steps.

### If Denied

Check your decision email for feedback. Choose **Back to application options**, address the feedback, and apply again. You can keep the same producer name.

### If Access Is Revoked Later

If your producer access is revoked after approval, you cannot submit a new request directly. Contact support so the team can review your appeal.


## Application Status

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Pending** | Request submitted and waiting for review |
| **Approved** | Open the dashboard or profile action shown for your approved request |
| **Denied** | Request was not approved |
| **Revoked** | Producer access was removed: requires an appeal |

## Submission Blocks

Before showing request options, the selector checks whether you are allowed to submit:

- **Application Already Submitted**: Use **View application** to return to your review, then **Refresh status** to check for a decision. You do not need to send another application
- **Producer Access Revoked**: Use **Contact Support** to discuss an appeal


### If Something Will Not Load

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Application options or profile availability cannot be checked">
    Choose **Try again**. Wait for the check to finish before submitting; it prevents an application for the wrong or unavailable profile.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="An optional account connection fails">
    You can try the connection again or continue with your application. Account connections are optional. Applications do not accept verification-document uploads.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Your application is approved but the dashboard will not open">
    Try **Open dashboard** again so BeatPass can refresh your account access. If it keeps failing, contact support rather than sending another application.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Tips for a Strong Application

- **Use a quality beats link**: Share a link that showcases your best work and loads properly
- **Use your real name**: Accurate details help the review process and simplify payout setup
- **Add your Instagram**: Social presence can help confirm your identity
- **Join Discord**: Required for verification, and useful for staying informed about producer community updates
