Choose **Start Application** when you do not already have a producer profile on BeatPass. Approval creates a new profile linked to your account.


## Who Should Use This

Choose this path if:

- You are new to BeatPass as a producer
- You do not see a BeatPass producer profile that should belong to you
- You want a fresh producer profile built around your current account

If your music is already on BeatPass under an existing profile, use [Claim Existing Artist](/help/producer-program/application-types/claim-existing-artist) instead.


## What You'll Need

| Requirement | Details |
| --- | --- |
| **Producer name** | How you want to appear publicly on BeatPass |
| **Link to your beats** | Public URL to your music for review |
| **First and last name** | Your real name for identity review |
| **Instagram handle** (optional) | Can help with identity verification |
| **Company and available account connections** (optional) | Add context without uploading documents |


## Step-by-Step Request

<ProducerApplicationFigure />

### 1. Start the Request

1. Open [Producer Program](https://open.beatpass.ca/producer-program)
2. Choose **Apply to join** and sign in if needed
3. Choose **Start Application** from the available options

<Note>
If your account already has a connected producer profile, application options shows that profile with **Dashboard** or **Edit profile**, plus **Get Verified**. Use the existing profile rather than applying for another one.
</Note>

### 2. Profile Setup

Enter your **Producer Name**. This is the public name BeatPass will use for your producer profile if approved.

### 3. Personal Details

Fill in:

- **First name**
- **Last name**

### 4. Supporting Materials

Provide:

- **Link to your beats** (required)
- **Instagram handle** (optional)
- **Record label or company (optional)** and available account connections under **Add context (optional)**

### 5. Optional Community

Discord is separate from this application. You can explore the community or ask about verification after connecting your producer profile. No document upload or social connection is needed to submit.

### 6. Agreement

Check both boxes to confirm:

1. You agree to the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy
2. You own all rights to the beats you will upload, authorize BeatPass to host and issue licenses for them under BeatPass terms, and commit to uploading at least 2 beats per month

Your future uploads must be human-made and not fully AI-generated. If you use AI-assisted elements or AI samples, you must be able to verify the source and commercial-use rights. See [AI Audio and Human-Made Content](/help/legal/ai-audio-human-made-content).

### 7. Submit

Click **Submit Application** to send the request for review. **Your beats are in review** confirms submission. The page also shows your **Submitted listening link** so you can check the music you shared.


## What Happens Next

### Review

BeatPass reviews the quality of your beats, the accuracy of your information, and whether the request appears legitimate. The submitted page shows an estimated review time of **1 to 3 business days**, but timing can vary.

Choose **View application** on the Producer Program page to return to your review. Use **Refresh status** to check for an update without submitting again.

### Decision

You receive an email when a decision is made:

- **Approved**: Your new producer profile is created and activated
- **Denied**: BeatPass explains why so you can improve and reapply

### After Approval

Once approved, you gain access to producer tools and can start uploading beats, completing payout setup, and managing your catalog. See [After Approval](/help/producer-program/after-approval) for next steps.


## Common Questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What if my request is denied?">
    You receive an email explaining why. Fix the issue, strengthen your materials, and apply again when ready.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How long does review take?">
    The submitted page shows an estimated 1 to 3 business days, but actual review time can vary.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Do I need a paid subscription to be a producer?">
    No. Producer access is free and separate from customer subscriptions. Current producer accounts cannot acquire new standard or exclusive catalog licenses, including with a paid plan. Previously acquired valid licenses remain subject to their original terms.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
