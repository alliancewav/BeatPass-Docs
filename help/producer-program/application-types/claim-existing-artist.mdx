The **Claim Existing Artist** request is for producers whose music or producer page is already on BeatPass, but whose current account does not control that profile yet.


## Who Should Use This

Choose this path if:

- You already see your music on BeatPass
- The producer profile is not yet connected to your account
- You need BeatPass to verify ownership or authority before linking it to you

If you are starting from scratch with no existing profile, use [Become a Producer](/help/producer-program/application-types/become-producer) instead.


## How It Works

1. You search for and select the existing producer profile
2. BeatPass checks whether it is currently available
3. The team reviews your ownership evidence
4. If approved, the profile is linked to your account


## What You'll Need

| Requirement | Details |
| --- | --- |
| **The profile to claim** | You search for and select it during the request |
| **Link to your beats** | Public URL showing your music and identity |
| **First and last name** | Your real name for review |
| **Your role** | How you relate to the profile (e.g. Producer, Manager) |
| **Instagram handle** (optional) | Can help with identity review |
| **Company and available account connections** (optional) | Add context without uploading private documents |


## Step-by-Step Request

### 1. Start the Request

1. Open [Producer Program](https://open.beatpass.ca/producer-program)
2. Choose **Apply to join** and sign in if needed
3. Choose **Claim Profile** from the available options

<Note>
Accounts with a connected primary producer profile see their existing profile options. Contact support for ownership help instead of creating a second primary profile.
</Note>

### 2. Select the Profile

Use **Select Producer Profile** to search for the producer you want to claim. Wait for the availability check:

- **This profile is available to claim.**: You can continue
- **This profile is unavailable. Please choose another or contact support.**: Choose another profile or ask support to review ownership

If the check cannot finish, choose **Try again** before sending your application.

### 3. Confirm the Profile

Check that the selected profile is the right one. BeatPass checks availability before you can submit. If the check fails, use **Try again**. Claiming keeps the profile’s existing artwork; there is no picture or verification-document upload.

### 4. Personal Details

Fill in:

- **First name**
- **Last name**
- **Role** (**Producer** or **Manager**)

<Note>
Choose **Producer** or **Manager**, whichever accurately describes your relationship. Contact support if neither describes the access you need.
</Note>

### 5. Supporting Materials

Provide:

- **Link to your beats** (required)
- **Instagram handle** (optional)
- **Record label or company (optional)** and available account connections under **Add context (optional)**

### 6. Agreement

Check both boxes to confirm:

1. You agree to the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy
2. You own the rights to the beats you will upload, authorize BeatPass to host and issue licenses for them under BeatPass terms, and commit to uploading at least 2 beats per month

Your future uploads must be human-made and not fully AI-generated. If you use AI-assisted elements or AI samples, you must be able to verify the source and commercial-use rights. See [AI Audio and Human-Made Content](/help/legal/ai-audio-human-made-content).

### 7. Submit

Click **Submit Application** to send the claim for review. **Your beats are in review** confirms receipt. Use **View application** from Producer Program to return, then **Refresh status** to check for a decision.


## What Happens Next

BeatPass verifies that the profile is available, that you are the rightful owner or authorized representative, and that your supporting materials make sense for the claim. You receive an email when the claim is approved or denied.

Once approved, the profile is linked to your account. An approved **Producer** claim offers **Open dashboard**; an approved **Manager** claim offers **Edit profile**. The action refreshes your account access before continuing. See [After Approval](/help/producer-program/after-approval) for producer setup.


## What If the Profile Is Already Claimed?

If the form says the profile is unavailable:

1. Check that you selected the correct profile
2. The normal claim flow stops until the profile is available
3. If you believe ownership needs correction, [contact support](/help/contact-support/getting-help)


## Common Questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What if I cannot find my profile?">
    Try alternate spellings or shorter versions of the name. If you still cannot find it, the profile may not exist yet: use **Become a Producer** instead.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I claim multiple profiles?">
    The producer workspace supports one owned producer profile per account. Contact support about a more complex ownership or management situation before submitting another claim.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What happens to existing tracks when I claim?">
    The existing profile stays in place. Approval gives your account the ability to manage it.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I prove ownership?">
    Share a public listening link that clearly represents your music. Company, Instagram, and enabled account connections can add context. The application does not accept identity documents or screenshot attachments. Contact support if the team needs more information.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
