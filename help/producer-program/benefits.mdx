BeatPass producer access is more than an upload permission. It unlocks the tools needed to publish your catalog, offer licenses, and earn from activity on the platform.


## Revenue & Earnings

### Keep 100% of Your Price

For exclusive sales, you keep the listed price you set for the beat. The buyer sees any applicable platform fee separately at checkout. A waiver depends on the buyer's active plan; it does not reduce your listed price. Refunds, adjustments, and payout readiness still follow the applicable terms.

<Note>
  Payouts still depend on an active payout setup, and final bank deposit timing depends on the active payout route and bank processing.
</Note>

### Multiple Income Streams

As a producer, you can earn from:

- **Subscription contribution** when standard licensing and the subscription payout stream are active
- **Exclusive licenses** when buyers purchase your configured exclusive option

A catalog that listeners return to can support both paths.


## Upload & Catalog

### Build Your Catalog

Producer access is free. Keep building your beats and collections, and follow any upload allowance shown on the upload page. If a limit looks wrong, contact support before buying a customer plan; a customer subscription does not unlock new standard or exclusive catalog licenses for producer accounts.

### Catalog Management

Your producer workspace lets you:

- Upload individual beats and collections
- Edit metadata, cover art, and licensing
- Reorder collection tracks
- Replace audio before an exclusive sale
- Review finances and producer activity


## Rights & Ownership

### Keep Your Rights

You retain full ownership of your music. By joining the program, you authorize BeatPass to host, distribute, issue licenses for, and verify your beats under BeatPass terms.

This means:

- You can still sell your beats elsewhere
- You control your track pricing and exclusive terms
- Existing non-exclusive BeatPass licenses stay valid if a beat later sells exclusively
- Your catalog must remain human-made, not fully AI-generated, and any AI samples or generated elements must be commercially licensable through BeatPass

<Note>
  BeatPass may promote your beats using short snippets or full audio, together with cover art and credits, on BeatPass-owned channels under the [promotion terms](/help/legal/terms-of-service). To opt out, use the [contact form](https://open.beatpass.ca/contact) with your account email and put **Promo Opt-Out** at the start of **Message**. New promotional posts stop within **72 hours**; you can also request removal of earlier posts within **10 business days**, where technically feasible.
</Note>

## Verified Producer Benefits

Verification adds the profile badge and can make your credited beats eligible for Audio Recon and new-request alerts. Notification delivery follows the relevant activity and your enabled channels:

<Columns cols={2}>
  <Card title="Verified Badge" icon="circle-check">
    A blue check appears on your producer profile, listing cards, and linked user profile.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Audio Recon" icon="wave-square">
    Eligible tracks can be scanned when at least one credited producer is verified. A match needs listening and review.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Producer Dashboard Notifications" icon="bell">
    Review the producer notifications available in your settings. Payout notices are not a guarantee of bank arrival.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Beat Request Alerts" icon="bullseye">
    Notifications about new beat requests for eligible verified producers. Browse the brief yourself to decide whether your music fits.
  </Card>
</Columns>

See [Get Verified](/help/producer-program/application-types/get-verified) for the verification process.


## No Upfront Costs

There is no fee to join the Producer Program. You pay nothing to get started. On exclusive sales, BeatPass charges the buyer platform fee when applicable; an eligible buyer plan can waive it without reducing your listed price.
