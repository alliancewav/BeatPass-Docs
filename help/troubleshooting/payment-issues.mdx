Check BeatPass **Billing** before retrying a payment. A pending checkout, an unpaid renewal, and an exclusive purchase can need different next steps.

## Start with the recorded status

Open [Billing](https://open.beatpass.ca/billing) to check the current plan and **Payment history**. For an exclusive purchase, also check its confirmation page and [Your licenses](https://open.beatpass.ca/library/licenses).

For a subscription, **Your BeatPass is ready.** confirms activation. Use **Go to Billing** to review the recorded plan and receipt.

If **Confirmation is still pending** appears, use **Check again** or open Billing. Do not make another payment while confirmation is pending. **Payment needs your attention** means you should check Billing and the displayed message before using **Back to checkout** when offered.

## Your sign-in session ended

If checkout says **Your session has ended. Sign in again to continue.**, choose **Sign in**. Review the plan, dates, and full total again before paying. If the charge changes, checkout asks you to review the updated total and submit again.

## A card or renewal failed

Check the card expiry, provider error, and any bank authentication request. In **Billing → Payment method**, select **Update** to change the subscription method. Use only payment options offered for the current checkout.

A bank decline may need the card issuer's help. Updating a card does not itself confirm a retry succeeded: check the plan and payment status afterwards. Overdue payment can block new standard licenses; valid earlier licenses are checked separately.

## The amount or plan looks wrong

Compare the invoice with the checkout total, billing interval, platform fee, and any promo code or plan benefit. An exclusive-purchase benefit does not automatically change subscription fees.

For a plan change, check its effective date. A scheduled change keeps the current plan until renewal; an immediate upgrade can include a provider-calculated prorated charge.

## A charge is duplicated or unauthorized

For two completed charges or failed delivery, [contact support](/help/contact-support) with the date, amount, and purchase or invoice reference. Redact unrelated information from evidence. For an unauthorized charge, contact your card issuer as well as BeatPass.

Do not send full card numbers, security codes, bank details, passwords, or sign-in links.

## A refund or cancellation is needed

[Cancel a subscription](/help/billing/managing-subscription/cancelling-subscription) to stop future renewals. This does not automatically refund an earlier charge.

For an accidental exclusive purchase, the [24-hour exception](/help/billing/refund-policy) requires a request within 24 hours and unused files and certificate. Other problems, including verified rights issues, have separate review grounds. Approval is not guaranteed.
