Start with the message shown beside the beat or in your license library. A new standard license, an existing license, and your own producer file have different access rules.

## Check which download you need

| Your situation | Next step |
| --- | --- |
| You want a new standard catalog license | Sign in with an eligible customer account and an active paid plan that includes downloads |
| You are a producer | Download your own original files, or retrieve beats covered by valid earlier licenses; a paid plan does not unlock new standard catalog licenses |
| You already licensed the beat | Open [Library → My Licenses](https://open.beatpass.ca/library/licenses) and check that license's status |
| The beat sold exclusively | New standard licenses stop; earlier valid licenses remain subject to their issued terms |
| Your payment is overdue | Resolve the payment in [Billing](https://open.beatpass.ca/billing) before requesting a new standard license |
| The beat is not ready for standard licensing | Choose another eligible beat or check back later; subscribing does not override the track's availability |

## You see Download requested but no file

**Download requested** means BeatPass handed the request to your browser. It cannot confirm that the file was saved.

1. Open your browser's downloads and check for a blocked, paused, or failed request.
2. Allow the download when your browser asks, and check your device's storage space.
3. Retry from the beat or from your valid license entry. A retry does not grant a second project.

## Your certificate needs attention

Open **Your licenses** and follow any name-confirmation or review action shown for the entry. Completing a browser download does not resolve a certificate identity problem. See [license certificates](/help/downloads-and-licensing/license-certificates) and [account details](/help/account-settings/account-details).

Downloading your own producer file does not issue a license certificate to yourself.

## Exclusive files or persistent failures

For an exclusive purchase, check its purchase record, certificate, and agreed deliverables. Follow [exclusive purchase help](/help/downloads-and-licensing/exclusive-licenses/purchasing-exclusive).

If you still cannot retrieve a file, [contact support](/help/contact-support) with the beat link, the time of the attempt, and the exact error. Do not send passwords, payment-card details, or session information.
