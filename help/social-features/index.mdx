Follow a producer whose sound you love, leave a thoughtful comment, or share a beat with someone who should hear it. Public activity connects to your BeatPass profile; [messages](/help/messaging) are separate conversations.

## Included features

<Columns cols={2}>
  <Card title="Follow people" icon="user-plus" href="/help/social-features/following-system">
    Keep producers and user profiles close in your library and following lists.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Leave a comment" icon="comment-dots" href="/help/social-features/comments">
    Comment on tracks and collections, or point to a moment on a track waveform.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Repost a favorite" icon="repeat" href="/help/social-features/reposts">
    Put a track or collection on your public profile when reposting is available.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Share a link" icon="share-nodes" href="/help/social-features/sharing-and-embeds">
    Send a beat, collection, producer page, or playlist to someone else.
  </Card>
</Columns>

## Where these features appear

<Tabs sync={false}>
  <Tab title="People" icon="users">
    On a user profile, follow the person, browse public followers and following, or start a message when you are signed in. On a producer page, follow, share, or message the claimed owner when that option is available.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Music" icon="music">
    Tracks can have waveform-linked comments, replies, reposts, share links, and embeds. Collections have standard comments, reposts, sharing, and embeds. Playlists have share links.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## What social features do not include

BeatPass does not have a separate follower-activity feed. Embeds are available for tracks and collections, rather than producer pages or playlists. If a repost control is absent, that feature may not be available.

## Community guidelines

Keep feedback constructive and respect the person behind the music. Harassment, spam, and inappropriate content can lead to removal or account restrictions. Report concerns through the [Content Guidelines](/help/legal/content-guidelines) and respect others' rights when sharing their work.
