Send someone a beat, share a collection, or put a BeatPass player on your own website. Beats, collections, producer pages and playlists can be shared by link.

## Share a link

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Share">
    Select **Share** on the page, or open its **More** menu and choose **Share**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Copy the link">
    Select **Copy**, then paste the link into your message, social post, or website.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Share from your device">
    When **Share via…** is available, use it to choose an app from your device's share sheet. Use **Copy** if your browser does not offer that option.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Add a player to your website

You can embed a **beat or collection** player. Producer pages and playlists use share links instead.

1. Open **Share** on the beat or collection, then choose **Embed**.
2. Review the player preview when shown on desktop.
3. Copy the generated code.
4. Paste it into your website builder's **Embed** or **HTML** block, then preview your page.

The player adjusts to the space on your page. Visitors may need to press Play before audio starts.

## Recommend music on BeatPass

Use [Repost](/help/social-features/reposts) when you want a recommendation to appear in your BeatPass profile's **Reposts** tab. Use **Share** when you want to send a link somewhere else.

## When something needs attention

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="The player does not appear on my website">
    Copy the complete code again and check that your website accepts embedded external players. Use an Embed or HTML block rather than a normal text field. If the site blocks the player, ask your website editor or administrator to allow the BeatPass embed.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The embedded player has no sound">
    Press Play first. Check that the beat or collection is still available on BeatPass, then review your browser's audio permissions.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="My device's share sheet did not open">
    Use **Copy** in the Share dialog, then paste the link into the app you want to use.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
