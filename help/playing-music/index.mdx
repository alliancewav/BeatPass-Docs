## Where playback starts

You can start playback from most places you browse music, including:

- Track rows and track cards
- Producer pages and collection pages
- Playlists and radio pages
- Channel pages, search results, and library tables

## Desktop and mobile differences

| Surface | Desktop | Mobile |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Player shell | Fixed bottom bar | Mini player plus full-screen overlay |
| Track info | Artwork, track link, artist links, and a like button | Artwork, title, and artist names |
| Primary controls | Shuffle, previous, play/pause, next, repeat, and seekbar | The mini player keeps compact playback actions; the expanded view adds listening tools |
| Extra actions | Queue toggle, volume, download, lyrics, and expand depending on settings and screen width | Queue and lyrics live inside the expanded overlay |

## Common actions while listening

- Open the track page from the player to see full track details.
- Save the current track to your library.
- Add tracks, collections, or playlists to the queue from context menus.
- Choose **Lyrics** when that action is available.
- Download the current track when downloads are enabled and your account has access.

## More about a beat

A track page can also show:

- **BeatPass ID** with licensing, Sample-Safe, available AI disclosure, promised deliverables, and fingerprint details
- The exclusive license preview when an exclusive offer is available
- **About the producer** below the discovery carousel, with a link to the producer profile

<Tip>
  Use the queue for the current listening session and playlists for saved, shareable collections. The two systems are separate.
</Tip>
