Keep BeatPass playing while you browse. The player gives you quick listening controls and opens the queue, lyrics, or download choices when those actions are available for the current beat.

<PlayerFigure />

## Control your listening

| Control | What you can do |
| --- | --- |
| **Play / Pause** | Start or pause the cued beat |
| **Previous** and **Next** | Move through the current queue |
| **Shuffle** | Change the listening order |
| **Repeat** | Cycle through repeat modes |
| Progress bar | Choose another point in the beat |
| **Volume** | Adjust playback volume where the control is shown |

## Use the compact or expanded player

On desktop, the bottom player bar shows track information, playback controls, and available secondary actions. On smaller screens, tap the mini player to open **Now playing**. Open **Queue** or **Lyrics** there; use the close/back control or the supported downward gesture to return to browsing.

The compact layout keeps the most useful controls first. Some secondary actions move into **More** or the expanded player as space changes. Producer workspaces can show different navigation beneath the player.

## Download audio

Select **Download** to review the available action for that beat and your account. The flow may offer your own original file, licensed audio, an existing purchase, or plan/exclusive-license guidance. A **Download requested** message means the browser has been asked to save audio; find any issued certificate separately in **My licenses**.

See [How downloads work](/help/downloads-and-licensing/how-downloads-work). The browser handoff does not prove that a file finished saving or that a certificate is ready.

## Keyboard shortcuts

| Shortcut | Action |
| --- | --- |
| <kbd>Space</kbd> or <kbd>K</kbd> | Play or pause |
| <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> or <kbd>Shift</kbd> + <kbd>→</kbd> | Next beat |
| <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> or <kbd>Shift</kbd> + <kbd>←</kbd> | Previous beat |

Focused buttons, links, sliders, and editing fields keep their own keyboard behavior. Use the progress or volume control directly to seek or adjust volume.
