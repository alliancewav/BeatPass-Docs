Give listeners a place to hear your sound and get to know your work. Your producer profile brings your beats, collections, biography, credits, photos, and linked videos together.

You need approved [producer access](/help/producer-program) to manage a producer profile. Your account uses one owned producer profile; collaborative credits do not create extra workspaces.

## Explore a producer profile

| Section | What you can find |
| --- | --- |
| **Overview** | Producer pick, popular and new beats, collections, About, credits, and available videos |
| **Beats** | Individual beats from the catalog |
| **Collections** | Grouped releases |
| **Gallery** | Public photos and biography |
| **Videos** | Recent YouTube videos and Shorts when available |
| **Similar artists** | Related producers when available |
| **Followers** | People following the producer |
| **Achievements** | The owner's or administrator's producer view |

Sections depend on available content. A verified badge identifies a verified producer; it does not clear rights for a particular beat.

## Make it yours

1. Open your producer profile and choose **More** → **Edit**.
2. Use **Details**, **Images**, **Biography**, **Links**, or **Credits** to reach the section you want.
3. Review your changes and select **Save changes**. Wait for any image upload to finish first.
4. Use **Preview** to check the public result. You stay in the editor after saving.

If saving fails, keep the editor open, read the field message, and retry after fixing it. **Cancel** returns to the producer profile.
