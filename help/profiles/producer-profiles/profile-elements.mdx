# Profile Elements

Your producer profile combines branding, identity, and discovery metadata. This page covers the profile elements you can edit today.

## Cover Image

Your cover image is the large banner at the top of the producer page.

### Current File Requirements

- **Formats**: JPEG, JPG, PNG, GIF, or WebP
- **Max size**: 2MB

### How to Update It

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your producer profile">
    Navigate to your producer profile page on BeatPass.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the editor">
    Click the **More** button on your profile, then select **Edit**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Update the image fields">
    Use the producer editor to replace the cover image and related profile artwork.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the profile">
    Save once the preview looks right.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
Use artwork that stays readable behind your producer name and action buttons.
</Tip>

## Producer Avatar

Your avatar appears in the header, around your catalog, and in other producer surfaces.

### Current File Requirements

- **Formats**: JPEG, JPG, PNG, GIF, or WebP
- **Max size**: 2MB

Use the same producer editor entry point to replace the avatar.

<Tip>
Square images work best because the public page renders the avatar in a circular frame.
</Tip>

## Location

Location is part of your public producer biography.

### Where to Edit It

Open **Biography** in the producer editor to change:

- **City**
- **Country**

## Genres

Genres help BeatPass present your sound in discovery and profile contexts.

### Where to Edit Them

1. Open your producer editor
2. Go to **Details**
3. Update the **Genres** field
4. Save the profile

## Biography and Links

Use **Biography** in the producer editor for a short public introduction. Keep the first lines specific: describe your sound, relevant work, or what a customer can license.

Use **Links** for the destinations that help a visitor continue the relationship, such as your main social account, portfolio, or artist channel. Remove outdated links instead of filling every available slot.

## Featured Beat

Choose the beat that leads your public music surfaces from **Backstage** → **My Beats**:

1. Confirm the correct producer profile
2. Find an eligible beat in **Beat catalog**
3. Select **Feature**

Featuring another beat replaces the current choice. Select **Remove featured beat** to let BeatPass choose an available beat. A featured beat must have playable audio, primary ownership for the selected producer, and an active licensing or purchase option.

When BeatPass chooses automatically, it balances plays, saves, how much listeners hear, release recency, complete track details, and the buying choices that are currently live. Your usable featured choice always takes priority; if it becomes unavailable, BeatPass falls back instead of leading visitors to a dead end.

## Credits and Gallery

Use **Credits** in the producer editor to add real professional work. Use **Images** for gallery photos that strengthen the producer story. The Pro Page omits either section when it has no usable content.
