Your producer profile combines artwork, your public story, and your music. This page covers the profile elements you can edit today.

## Cover image

Your cover image is the large banner at the top of the producer page. It also provides the backdrop for About content on your profile and beat pages.

### Current file requirements

- **Formats**: JPEG, JPG, PNG, GIF, or WebP
- **Recommended shape**: 1920 × 1080, or 16:9. Follow the file limit shown by the image picker.

### How to update it

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your producer profile">
    Navigate to your producer profile page on BeatPass.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the editor">
    Click the **More** button on your profile, then select **Edit**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Update the image fields">
    Open **Images** and replace **Cover Image**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the profile">
    Select **Save changes** once the preview looks right and any image upload has finished.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
Use artwork that stays readable behind your producer name and action buttons.
</Tip>

## Producer avatar

Your avatar appears in the header, around your catalog, and in other places listeners discover you.

### Current file requirements

- **Formats**: JPEG, JPG, PNG, GIF, or WebP
- **Max size**: 2MB

Open **More** → **Edit**, then use the avatar field in **Details**. Review the crop and select **Save changes** after the upload finishes.

<Tip>
Square images work best because the public page renders the avatar in a circular frame.
</Tip>

## Location

Location is part of your public producer biography.

### Where to edit it

Open **Biography** in the producer editor to change:

- **City**
- **Country**

## Genres

Genres help BeatPass present your sound in discovery and profile contexts.

### Where to edit them

1. Open your producer editor
2. Go to **Details**
3. Update the **Genres** field
4. Select **Save changes**

## Biography and links

Use **Biography** in the producer editor for a short public introduction. Keep the first lines specific: describe your sound, relevant work, or what a customer can license.

Use **Links** for the destinations that help a visitor continue the relationship, such as your main social account, portfolio, or artist channel. Remove outdated links instead of filling every available slot.

## YouTube videos

A YouTube channel URL in **Links** adds a **Videos** tab and a video carousel below **Credits**. The Videos tab keeps regular videos and Shorts separate. Visitors can watch inside BeatPass without manually adding individual uploads.

See [YouTube videos on your profile](/help/profiles/producer-profiles/youtube-videos) for setup, refresh timing, and unavailable videos.

## Featured beat

Choose the beat that leads your public music surfaces from **Producer Dashboard** → **My Beats**:

1. Open **My Beats** for your linked producer profile.
2. Find an eligible beat in **Your catalog**.
3. Choose its feature action, or use **Change** on the featured-beat card.

Featuring another beat replaces the current choice. Use the remove-from-featured action to let BeatPass choose an available beat. A featured beat must have playable audio, primary ownership for the selected producer, and an active licensing or purchase option.

Your featured beat takes priority while it remains available. Otherwise, BeatPass balances listener response, freshness, complete track details, and current buying choices. Learn more in [Producer Pro Page](/help/producer-dashboard/pro-page#how-the-automatic-choice-works).

## Credits and gallery

Use **Credits** in the producer editor to add real professional work. Use **Images** for gallery photos that strengthen the producer story. The Pro Page omits either section when it has no usable content.
