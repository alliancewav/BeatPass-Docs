Your BeatPass profile gives people a place to find you. Every account has a user profile; approved producers also have a producer profile for their music.

## Profile types

<Columns cols={2}>
  <Card title="Your user profile" icon="user" href="/help/profiles/user-profiles">
    Your avatar, handle, saved music, playlists, reposts, and followers.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Your producer profile" icon="music" href="/help/profiles/producer-profiles">
    Your artist page with a catalog, credits, images, and producer identity.
  </Card>
</Columns>

## Which profile do I have?

You already have a user profile if you have a BeatPass account. Use it to follow people, save beats, share playlists, and keep conversations connected to your handle.

Producer access is free and needs [Producer Program approval](/help/producer-program). It adds your public artist page and tools for uploads, analytics, and finances. Your user and producer profiles stay connected, with separate editing controls.
