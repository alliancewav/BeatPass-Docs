## Ownership

### Producers Keep Their Rights

When you upload a beat to BeatPass:

- You **retain full ownership** of your beat
- Nothing transfers ownership to BeatPass or to consumers
- You authorize BeatPass to operate the platform, make the beat available for licensing, issue license certificates, deliver files, and verify licenses under the applicable BeatPass terms

### What Consumers Get

When you download a beat with a subscription:

- You receive a **non-exclusive, perpetual, worldwide license**
- You can use the beat in **one project**
- A valid license continues after subscription cancellation, subject to its terms, breach provisions, and the producer having the rights to grant it
- You **do not** gain ownership of the beat itself

## Who can get a new license?

New standard catalog licenses are available to eligible consumer accounts with an active paid plan that includes downloads. In the current app, producer accounts cannot get new standard catalog licenses or make new exclusive catalog purchases for other producers' beats, even with a paid subscription.

Producers can download their own source files. Valid licenses issued before a later role change remain usable and can still be retrieved under their original terms. Re-downloading does not grant another project.

## License Restrictions

Check the specific exclusive offer or obtain separate written permission before using a beat for:

- Television, film, or streaming service productions
- Commercials and advertising campaigns
- Video games
- Major brand campaigns
- Using one beat in multiple projects

An exclusive purchase does not automatically cover every use. AI training, datasets, model development, sample packs, and competing libraries require separate written permission from BeatPass and the relevant rights holder.

## AI Audio and Human-Made Beats

BeatPass is for human-made beats. Producers must make a meaningful human creative contribution to every beat they upload.

- Fully AI-generated beats are not permitted.
- AI may be used for assistive production tasks or limited source elements only when the final beat remains primarily human-made.
- AI samples and generated elements must be commercially licensable through BeatPass.
- Producers must disclose AI use when required and provide Proof of Rights on request.
- BeatPass may review, delist, pause downloads, hold unpaid payouts, or restrict accounts for AI policy violations.

See [AI Audio and Human-Made Content](/help/legal/ai-audio-human-made-content) for the practical policy guide.

## Producer Payout Services

Producers must provide accurate payee and bank information and complete the verification required by the payout provider shown in **Finances**. Existing eligible producers may continue receiving payouts through legacy Stripe Connect until their BEATPAY setup becomes active. New and migrated bank payouts use Wise-powered BEATPAY when assigned by BeatPass.

Payout timing depends on the active provider, receiving bank, bank account currency, conversion, and any compliance review. See [BEATPAY Bank Payouts](/help/earnings/beatpay), [Stripe Connect (Legacy)](/help/earnings/stripe-connect), and the [official Privacy Policy](https://open.beatpass.ca/pages/privacy-policy).

## Producer Promotion

BeatPass may promote producers and their beats on its own channels (website and social media). This includes:

- Short clips or full audio from beats
- Cover art, titles, and producer names
- Links to producer profiles

To opt out, use the [BeatPass contact form](https://open.beatpass.ca/contact) with your account email and put **Promo Opt-Out** at the start of **Message**. BeatPass will stop new promotional posts within **72 hours**. You can also request removal of earlier posts within **10 business days**, where technically feasible.

<Note>
  BeatPass will not use your beats in third-party paid advertisements without your written consent.
</Note>

## Content Rules

All content uploaded to BeatPass must:

- Be original work you have the right to upload
- Not infringe on anyone else's copyright
- Not contain illegal, harmful, or offensive material
- Be human-made and not fully AI-generated
- Have verifiable commercial-use rights for any AI samples or AI-generated elements

## DMCA & Copyright

BeatPass respects intellectual property rights and follows the DMCA notice-and-takedown process.

### If Your Work Is Used Without Permission

Send a DMCA notice to **contact@beatpass.ca** including:

- Identification of the copyrighted work
- Location of the infringing material on BeatPass
- Your contact information
- A statement of good faith
- A statement that the information is accurate and, under penalty of perjury, that you own the copyright or are authorized to act for its owner
- Your signature

### If Your Content Was Removed

If you believe your content was removed by mistake, use the [Copyright & DMCA guide](/help/legal/copyright-and-dmca) for the counter-notice checklist and template. The [official DMCA policy](https://open.beatpass.ca/pages/dmca-notice-and-takedown-policy) describes the full process.

### Repeat Infringers

Accounts with repeated copyright violations may be suspended or terminated.

## Account Termination

BeatPass may terminate accounts that:

- Violate the Terms of Service
- Receive repeated copyright complaints
- Engage in fraudulent activity
