This guide explains the [official Cookie and Tracking Policy](https://open.beatpass.ca/pages/cookie-and-tracking-policy). BeatPass uses cookies and similar technologies to run the platform, secure accounts, remember preferences, support checkout, and measure usage or communications where permitted.

## Categories

- **Strictly necessary:** login, authentication, security, checkout, fraud prevention, account settings, and core platform operation.
- **Preferences:** language, display, playback, accessibility, and saved settings.
- **Analytics and performance:** usage, errors, traffic, feature adoption, and reliability.
- **Marketing and communications:** campaign measurement, email preferences, and promotional communications where permitted.

## Choose optional tracking

When BeatPass asks about optional analytics and marketing cookies, choose **Refuse** or **Accept**. You can refuse optional tracking and keep using BeatPass.

## Change your choice

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Privacy preferences">
    Open the [BeatPass contact page](https://open.beatpass.ca/contact) and scroll to the footer. Select **Privacy preferences**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Refuse or Accept">
    Select **Refuse** to keep optional analytics and marketing tracking off, or **Accept** to allow it. You can return here to change your choice.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check that your choice saved">
    The notice closes when your choice saves. If BeatPass says it could not save the choice, optional tracking remains off. Try again when your browser allows the preference to be saved.
  </Step>
</Steps>



<Note>
  Refusing optional tracking is different from blocking every cookie in your browser. Necessary cookies support login, security, and checkout. Browser-level blocking can affect those features.
</Note>

Marketing email choices are separate. Use the unsubscribe link in a marketing email or the available [notification preferences](/help/notifications/notification-settings).
