Use a BeatPass standard non-exclusive license to make one finished song with a beat. Other artists can license the same beat too. Keep your certificate with the song's release files, and follow the terms issued for your license.

Read this guide with the [official Non-Exclusive License Agreement](https://open.beatpass.ca/pages/non-exclusive-license-agreement), [Terms of Service](https://open.beatpass.ca/pages/terms-of-service), certificate, and any transaction-specific or signed terms. This guide does not change an earlier grant.

## Who can get a new standard license

You need an eligible consumer account and an active paid plan that includes downloads. Current producer accounts cannot get new standard licenses to other producers' beats, even with a paid plan.

Producers can retrieve their own source files and audio covered by valid earlier licenses. An earlier valid license can continue after cancellation, producer approval, or a later exclusive sale, subject to its terms and valid underlying rights.

## Check the current standard limits

A new standard license covers **one project** with:

- Up to **100,000 physical or digital units**.
- Up to **500,000 audio streams**.
- **One music video**, no longer than **five minutes** in total. A lyric video counts towards that same video limit.
- Live events of up to **50 attendees per event** under the current standard certificate.

You can record vocals and other original contributions, make musical edits within the project, and release or promote that finished song within the licensed limits. Check your own issued terms for an older license. Downloading the same beat again does not license a second song.

## Credit and income sharing

Credit the producer as **Produced by [Producer Name]** where commercially reasonable and technically available, including release metadata or video descriptions when those fields exist.

Performance royalties and synchronization income default to **50% producer / 50% licensee**, unless the transaction, certificate, or a separate accepted agreement states otherwise. Register the producer's applicable rights accurately. An income split does not itself grant permission for a sync placement.

## Ask before using rights outside the grant

The standard license does not include Content ID registration for the beat, film, TV, broadcast, advertising, games, larger live events, or another project. Contact support or the producer about separate permission before proceeding.

Do not redistribute the raw beat or stems, claim ownership of the original instrumental, resell or transfer the license, or extract reusable samples outside your project. AI training, datasets, model development, sample packs, and competing libraries require separate written permission from BeatPass and the relevant rights holder.

## Keep the file and proof together

A standard download provides the beat's attached downloadable audio and an issued or reused certificate. It does not promise a WAV/MP3 bundle or stems. Open [Your licenses](https://open.beatpass.ca/library/licenses) to retrieve the certificate and public verification link.

If the beat is later sold exclusively, future standard licenses stop while earlier valid grants continue. If a rights issue or certificate problem appears, [contact support](/help/contact-support). BeatPass cannot grant rights the producer did not own, and a certificate does not guarantee third-party clearance. See [Refund Policy](/help/billing/refund-policy) for remedy review.
