An eligible BeatPass subscription download gives you access to a **non-exclusive license** for one project. Re-downloading the same beat may retrieve your existing license; it does not itself grant rights for another song.

<Info>
  **Non-exclusive** means multiple artists can legally use the same beat. Your certificate records the licensed beat and the BeatPass account that holds the license.
</Info>

## Check the terms for your license

This page is a guide. Read the [Terms of Service](https://open.beatpass.ca/pages/terms-of-service) and the applicable [Non-Exclusive License Agreement](https://open.beatpass.ca/pages/non-exclusive-license-agreement) or [Exclusive License Agreement](https://open.beatpass.ca/pages/exclusive-license-agreement), together with your transaction terms and certificate. The Terms control conflicts unless a signed written license says otherwise.

The standard non-exclusive agreement covers one project, with limits of **100,000 physical or digital units**, **500,000 streams**, and **one music video no longer than five minutes**. Follow the caps and parameters attached to your download. Performance royalties and synchronization income default to **50% for the producer and 50% for the licensee**, unless the transaction terms, certificate, or a separate agreement provide otherwise. An income split does not itself grant permission for a sync use.

Use the terms that apply to your issued license. This guide does not change earlier grants. Exclusive permissions and deliverables depend on the specific purchase.

## Quick answer

A standard BeatPass non-exclusive license covers one original musical work within the applicable limits.

You can release and monetise that finished song within the applicable unit, stream and video limits. Your perpetual license has no automatic time limit and can survive subscription cancellation, subject to the agreement, breach provisions and valid underlying rights.

You cannot register the beat in Content ID, resell the beat, release the beat by itself, claim authorship of the beat, transfer the license, or use the beat for AI training or competing datasets.

## What This License Covers Today

- Create **one original musical work** with the downloaded beat.
- Release and monetize that finished song within the applicable unit and stream limits.
- Create one music video for the Project, up to five minutes in total. A lyric video counts as that music video, not as an additional video. Promote the Project through related artist social media, release trailers and DSP promotion under the agreement.
- Perform the finished work live at events with up to **50 attendees**.
- Keep the perpetual one-project license after cancellation, subject to the agreement, breach provisions and valid underlying rights.

## Project Limit

Each standard non-exclusive license covers **one project**. Re-downloading the beat does not itself grant rights for a different song. Contact support to confirm the separate permission needed before using it in another project.

## What You Must Do

- Credit the producer on releases and public descriptions.
- Keep the use tied to the one project covered by your license.
- Keep your certificate in **Library** -> **Licenses** in case you need proof later.

### Producer Credit

The current standard license requires producer credit:

> **"Produced by [Producer Name]"**

Use that credit in places such as metadata, streaming credits, and video descriptions when those fields are available.

## What This License Does Not Allow

| Restriction | What it means in practice |
|-------------|---------------------------|
| **Content ID registration** | You cannot register the beat in Content ID or similar systems |
| **Film, TV, broadcast, commercials, or games** | These uses are outside the current standard terms |
| **Large live events** | Non-exclusive live use is capped at **50 attendees** per event |
| **Beat-only releases** | You cannot upload, sell, or redistribute the beat by itself |
| **Transfer or resale** | You cannot sell the beat, the license, or stems to someone else |
| **Claiming authorship of the beat** | The producer remains the creator of the beat |
| **AI training or datasets** | You cannot use the beat, preview, files, artwork, metadata, or platform content to train, fine-tune, prompt, benchmark, build, or improve AI systems, datasets, sample packs, or competing libraries |

## Why non-exclusive still needs proof

Multiple artists may legally use the same beat. Your certificate records the licensed beat, your account, when the license was issued, and the applicable terms. A repeat file download may use the existing certificate.

## Files And Proof

BeatPass gives you:

- The beat's downloadable audio file.
- A non-exclusive license certificate tied to your account.

<Note>
  BeatPass does **not** guarantee a bundled WAV plus MP3 pair on every standard download. The file you receive depends on the actual downloadable source attached to that beat. Stems are not part of the standard non-exclusive terms.
</Note>

## AI-Origin and Rights Issues

BeatPass is a platform for human-made beats and relies on producer warranties, disclosures, and Proof of Rights when issuing licenses. BeatPass does not guarantee that a beat is AI-free, infringement-free, or accepted by every distributor, label, publisher, PRO, or third party.

If BeatPass later removes or restricts a beat because of a verified rights issue, AI misrepresentation, failure of Proof of Rights, or policy breach, BeatPass may provide support, replacement credit, account credit, refund review, documentation support, or other remedies under the Refund Policy. BeatPass cannot grant rights the producer did not own or control.

## If the beat sells exclusively later

If another artist purchases exclusive rights to a beat you've already downloaded:

- Your earlier valid non-exclusive license continues for its licensed project, subject to the agreement, breach provisions and valid underlying rights.
- **Future non-exclusive downloads stop** once the beat is sold exclusively.

## Common Questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can multiple people release songs with the same beat?">
    Yes. Non-exclusive licenses allow multiple artists to use the same beat. Each artist has their own valid certificate.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I use the beat on YouTube and streaming platforms?">
    Yes, within your applicable unit, stream and video limits. The standard agreement allows one music video no longer than five minutes. Standard non-exclusive licensing does not allow Content ID registration for the beat.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What counts as one project?">
    One project means one finished song. Re-downloading the same beat does not itself grant rights for another song. Contact support to confirm the separate permission needed for a different project.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Where can I find proof of my license later?">
    Open **Library** -> **Licenses**. BeatPass stores your standard certificate there so you can verify what was issued for that download.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I use the beat to train an AI model?">
    No. BeatPass licenses do not allow AI training, dataset creation, model development, sample-pack creation, or competing library use without separate written permission from BeatPass and the relevant rights holder.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
