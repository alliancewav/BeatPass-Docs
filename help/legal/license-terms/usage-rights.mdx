Check whether your planned release fits your BeatPass license before distributing, performing, or registering it. This comparison describes the current standard grant; for exclusive rights, read the actual offer and purchase documents.

Read the [Terms of Service](https://open.beatpass.ca/pages/terms-of-service), [Non-Exclusive License Agreement](https://open.beatpass.ca/pages/non-exclusive-license-agreement) or [Exclusive License Agreement](https://open.beatpass.ca/pages/exclusive-license-agreement), and your certificate together. Earlier grants keep their own applicable terms.

## Compare your next use

| Your plan | Current standard non-exclusive | Exclusive purchase |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Release and monetize one finished song | Up to 100,000 units and 500,000 streams, with credit and applicable income sharing | Check the purchase terms |
| Make a music or lyric video | One video total, up to five minutes | Check the purchase terms |
| Promote that song on artist social media or in release trailers | Allowed within the project's grant; video limits still apply | Check the purchase terms |
| Perform the song live | Up to 50 attendees per event under the current standard certificate | Check the purchase terms |
| Use the beat in a second song | Separate permission needed; repeating a download does not add a project | Check the purchase terms |
| Register the beat in Content ID | Not included | Only if expressly allowed |
| Use in film, TV, broadcast, advertising, or a game | Separate rights needed outside the standard grant | Only if expressly allowed |
| Resell the raw beat or reusable samples | Not included | Exclusive does not automatically allow it |
| Train AI, build a dataset, sample pack, or competing library | Separate written permission required | Separate written permission required |

## Check credit and shares

Standard terms require **Produced by [Producer Name]** where commercially reasonable and technically available. Performance royalties and synchronization income default to **50% producer / 50% licensee**, unless transaction, certificate, or a separate accepted agreement states otherwise. That income split does not grant sync permission on its own.

Exclusive purchases can also include producer/buyer shares or royalty obligations. Read the amounts and rights in the preview; do not assume that **Exclusive** means royalty-free or unlimited.

## Keep proof of the rights you received

A standard download supplies the attached source file and a certificate, not a promised WAV/MP3/stems bundle. For an exclusive purchase, the offer lists the files and producer delivery responsibility.

Open [Your licenses](https://open.beatpass.ca/library/licenses) for the document; choose **Details**, then **Verify**, to check its record. A certificate records the issued grant; it does not guarantee underlying clearance or third-party acceptance. If your use is unclear, [contact support](/help/contact-support) before proceeding.
