Use this page to compare common release scenarios against the current standard non-exclusive license and the current default exclusive terms shown in BeatPass. For an exclusive purchase, check the rights recorded in your purchase documents together with the governing Terms and agreement.

<Warning>
  Exclusive rights are configurable per track. Use the **Exclusive License Terms** preview and your generated certificate whenever you need the exact answer for a specific purchase.
</Warning>

<Warning>
  AI training, dataset creation, model development, sample-pack creation, and competing library use are not included in BeatPass standard or exclusive license paths unless separately agreed in writing by all required parties.
</Warning>

## Check the terms for your license

This page is a guide. Read the [Terms of Service](https://open.beatpass.ca/pages/terms-of-service) and the applicable [Non-Exclusive License Agreement](https://open.beatpass.ca/pages/non-exclusive-license-agreement) or [Exclusive License Agreement](https://open.beatpass.ca/pages/exclusive-license-agreement), together with your transaction terms and certificate. The Terms control conflicts unless a signed written license says otherwise.

The standard non-exclusive agreement covers one project, with limits of **100,000 physical or digital units**, **500,000 streams**, and **one music video no longer than five minutes**. Follow the caps and parameters attached to your download. Performance royalties and synchronization income default to **50% for the producer and 50% for the licensee**, unless the transaction terms, certificate, or a separate agreement provide otherwise. An income split does not itself grant permission for a sync use.

Use the terms that apply to your issued license. This guide does not change earlier grants. Exclusive permissions and deliverables depend on the specific purchase.

## Streaming And Digital Releases

| Use case | Standard non-exclusive | Exclusive purchase |
|----------|------------------------|-------------------|
| Release one finished song on Spotify, Apple Music, YouTube Music, and similar platforms | Within the applicable limits; standard agreement: 100,000 physical or digital units and 500,000 streams | Check the purchase terms |
| Monetize that finished song | Within the applicable grant and income-sharing terms | Check the purchase terms |
| Use the same beat for multiple separate finished songs | One project only; re-downloading does not itself grant another project | Check the purchase terms |

## Video, Sync, And Promotion

| Use case | Standard non-exclusive | Exclusive purchase |
|----------|------------------------|-------------------|
| Music video for your finished song | One music video no longer than five minutes under the standard agreement | Check the purchase terms |
| Artist social media, release trailers and DSP promotion related to the Project | Allowed under the agreement; the music-video limit still applies | Check the purchase terms |
| Lyric video for your finished song | Counts as the Project’s one music video: one video total, up to five minutes, including a lyric video | Check the purchase terms |
| Film, TV, commercials, video games, or other sync-style use | Separate permission required outside the standard grant | Check the purchase terms |
| Content ID registration | No | Only if the purchase terms allow it |

## AI, Datasets, And Reusable Assets

| Use case | Standard non-exclusive | Exclusive purchase |
|----------|------------------------|-------------------|
| Train, fine-tune, prompt, benchmark, or improve an AI model | No | No, unless separately agreed in writing |
| Build an AI dataset, audio-generation system, or sample pack from the beat | No | No, unless separately agreed in writing |
| Extract loops, stems, samples, or reusable assets outside the licensed project | No | Check the certificate, but AI/dataset use still requires separate permission |

## Live Use And Broadcasting

| Use case | Standard non-exclusive | Exclusive purchase |
|----------|------------------------|-------------------|
| Live performance up to **50 attendees** | Yes | Check the purchase terms |
| Larger live events | No | Check the exclusive terms |
| Broadcast radio or TV use | No | Check the exclusive terms |

## Files, Certificates, And Proof

| Item | Standard non-exclusive | Exclusive purchase |
|------|------------------------|-------------------|
| Audio file delivery | The beat's downloadable source file | The files listed in the exclusive preview |
| Stems or extra formats | Not part of the standard terms | Only if the preview includes them |
| License proof | Certificate stored in **Library** -> **Licenses** | Certificate generated for the purchase |

## Credit, Ownership, And Restrictions

- Standard non-exclusive downloads require producer credit.
- Standard non-exclusive use is tied to **one finished song per valid license**. Re-downloading does not itself add another project.
- Both paths prohibit reselling the beat itself as a standalone product.
- Both paths require you to follow the governing Terms, applicable agreement and transaction-specific terms recorded for your license.
- Exclusive purchases can still include producer publishing or royalty obligations, so review them before checkout.
- BeatPass relies on producer warranties, disclosures, and Proof of Rights when issuing certificates; verified rights or AI-policy issues may lead to support, credit, refund review, or certificate-status context.

## Quick Summary

### Standard Non-Exclusive

- Covers one finished song within the applicable unit, stream and video limits and income-sharing terms.
- Includes public release and small live use.
- Does not include Content ID, sync use, or large-event rights.

### Exclusive Purchase

- Best when you need the specific expanded rights listed in the track's preview.
- Often includes a broader file package and broader commercial permissions.
- Still requires you to read the actual offer because exclusive terms are not one-size-fits-all.
