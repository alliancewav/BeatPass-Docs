BeatPass currently has two licensing paths: the standard **non-exclusive** license created when an eligible subscriber downloads a beat, and the optional **exclusive** purchase offered on eligible tracks.

<Warning>
  Keep your certificate in **Library** -> **Licenses** as proof of the issued license. Read it with the governing Terms, applicable agreement and transaction terms. For an exclusive purchase, also review the **Exclusive License Terms** preview. A certificate does not create rights beyond the applicable grant.
</Warning>

<Warning>
  BeatPass licenses do not allow AI training, dataset creation, model development, sample-pack creation, or competing library use unless all required parties give separate written permission.
</Warning>

## Check the terms for your license

This page is a guide. Read the [Terms of Service](https://open.beatpass.ca/pages/terms-of-service) and the applicable [Non-Exclusive License Agreement](https://open.beatpass.ca/pages/non-exclusive-license-agreement) or [Exclusive License Agreement](https://open.beatpass.ca/pages/exclusive-license-agreement), together with your transaction terms and certificate. The Terms control conflicts unless a signed written license says otherwise.

The standard non-exclusive agreement covers one project, with limits of **100,000 physical or digital units**, **500,000 streams**, and **one music video no longer than five minutes**. Follow the caps and parameters attached to your download. Performance royalties and synchronization income default to **50% for the producer and 50% for the licensee**, unless the transaction terms, certificate, or a separate agreement provide otherwise. An income split does not itself grant permission for a sync use.

Use the terms that apply to your issued license. This guide does not change earlier grants. Exclusive permissions and deliverables depend on the specific purchase.

## Quick Comparison

| Question | Non-Exclusive | Exclusive |
|----------|---------------|-----------|
| **How you get it** | Download an eligible beat while your paid plan is active | Buy the exclusive option on a track that offers one |
| **Who else can use the beat** | Multiple artists can hold valid licenses | No new buyers after purchase, but older valid non-exclusive licenses remain valid |
| **Scope of use** | One original musical work per valid license; re-downloading does not itself add a project | Check the applicable exclusive purchase terms |
| **Files delivered** | BeatPass delivers the track's downloadable audio file and a certificate | Check the offer preview for the exact file list |
| **Content ID** | Not allowed | Allowed only if the exclusive terms say so |
| **Live performances** | Up to 50 attendees per event | Check the exclusive terms; current default terms do not add a fixed cap |
| **Film, TV, broadcast, commercials, games** | Not allowed | Check the exclusive terms; current default terms can allow them |
| **AI training or datasets** | Not allowed | Not allowed unless separately agreed in writing |

## What Both Paths Have In Common

- The license is issued to the BeatPass account and email used for the download or purchase.
- BeatPass stores verification details so you can prove the license later.
- You cannot resell or redistribute the beat itself as a standalone product.
- You are licensing use rights from the producer, not buying the right to relist the beat however you want.
- BeatPass relies on producer warranties, disclosures, and Proof of Rights when issuing licenses.
- If a beat is later restricted because of a verified rights issue, AI misrepresentation, or Proof of Rights failure, BeatPass may provide support, credit, refund review, or certificate-status context under the applicable policies.
