Read an exclusive BeatPass offer before paying so its rights and files fit your release. An exclusive purchase stops future licensing to new buyers; earlier valid non-exclusive licenses continue under their own terms.

The [official Exclusive License Agreement](https://open.beatpass.ca/pages/exclusive-license-agreement), [AI and Rights Addendum](/help/legal/exclusive-license-ai-and-rights-addendum), transaction terms, certificate, and any applicable signed agreement define your purchase. This guide does not change an earlier grant.

## Review the specific offer

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the exact terms">
    On the beat page, select **Preview exact license terms**, or select **View License Terms** inside checkout. The preview is titled **Review exclusive license**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the rights and shares">
    Read the streaming, distribution, video, broadcast, live-performance, sync, and Content ID permissions. Check territory, governing law, producer/buyer shares, royalty obligations, and transfer restrictions.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the files">
    Read **Files included** and any delivery guarantee. BeatPass prepares the original WAV; the producer is responsible for other promised files. Do not pay for an offer with missing file information.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Keep the accepted terms">
    Review the final checkout total and terms again before paying. After success, keep the certificate in [Your licenses](https://open.beatpass.ca/library/licenses).
  </Step>
</Steps>

There is no universal exclusive file bundle, producer/buyer share, or royalty percentage. A starting preset is not the accepted offer for every beat. Current newly published or edited offers require stems and a delivery guarantee, but older offers can differ.

## Ownership and earlier licenses

The producer keeps ownership except for the rights expressly granted. You do not gain permission to relist the raw beat as your own product. A valid earlier licensee is not an infringer merely because you later bought exclusive rights.

## AI and rights concerns

Fully AI-generated beats are not permitted. The producer must control the rights in AI-assisted elements and samples. Exclusive does not include AI training, datasets, model development, sample packs, or competing libraries without separate written permission from all required parties.

A later verified rights issue can lead to support, documentation, credit, or refund review under the [Refund Policy](/help/billing/refund-policy). BeatPass cannot grant rights the producer did not own or promise every distributor will accept your release.
