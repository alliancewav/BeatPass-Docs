Use this guide to report a copyright issue or respond when your upload is removed. It explains BeatPass's process under the [official DMCA Notice-and-Takedown Policy](https://open.beatpass.ca/pages/dmca-notice-and-takedown-policy) and the [U.S. DMCA statute](https://uscode.house.gov/us/usc/119-124/t17/s512/).

<Info>
  The official policy identifies the designated agent and contact information. Send copyright notices to **contact@beatpass.ca**. Use the checklist below to include the information needed for review.
</Info>

<Note>
  DMCA is for copyright claims. Reports about fully AI-generated beats, undisclosed AI use, unverifiable AI samples, or AI rights-verification issues are handled under the Terms of Service and AI Audio Policy unless the report also includes a copyright claim.
</Note>


## For Rights Holders: Reporting Infringement

If you believe someone has uploaded your copyrighted work to BeatPass without permission, you can file a DMCA takedown notice.

### What to Include

Your notice must include **all** of the following:

1. **Your signature**: Physical or electronic signature of the copyright owner or authorized agent
2. **Identify the original work**: Title, link, or description of the copyrighted work being infringed
3. **Identify the infringing material**: The exact BeatPass page/track title, producer name, and URL if available
4. **Your contact information**: Name, mailing address, telephone number, and email, if available
5. **Good-faith statement**: That the use is not authorized by the copyright owner, its agent, or the law
6. **Accuracy statement**: That the information is accurate and, under penalty of perjury, that you are the owner or authorized to act for the owner

### Where to Send

Email [contact@beatpass.ca](mailto:contact@beatpass.ca?subject=DMCA%20Takedown%20Notice) with **DMCA Takedown Notice** as the subject. For a follow-up by phone, call **514-397-9912**.

<Tip>
  Attach any supporting evidence you have: release links, purchase/license receipts, screenshots, ISRC/UPC codes, PRO registration, or distributor/DSP links.
</Tip>

<Accordion title="Copy a takedown notice template">

Copy and paste this template into an email to [contact@beatpass.ca](mailto:contact@beatpass.ca?subject=DMCA%20Takedown%20Notice):

```
Subject: DMCA Takedown Notice – [Your Work/Title]

I, [full name], am the copyright owner (or authorized agent) of the
work(s) identified below.

1) Work(s) claimed infringed:
   [title/description; if multiple, provide a representative list]

2) Material claimed infringing and location on BeatPass:
   [BeatPass page/track title, producer name, and URL if available]
   [Any additional identifiers or context]

3) Contact information:
   Name:
   Company (if any):
   Mailing address:
   Phone:
   Email:

4) Good-faith statement:
   I have a good-faith belief that the use of the material described
   above is not authorized by the copyright owner, its agent, or the law.

5) Accuracy and authority:
   I state that the information in this notification is accurate and,
   under penalty of perjury, that I am the owner of the copyright(s) or
   authorized to act on behalf of the owner.

Signature: [type full name to e-sign or provide physical signature]
Date:
```

</Accordion>

## What Happens After a Takedown Notice

When BeatPass receives a valid DMCA takedown notice:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Content Removal">
    BeatPass reviews the notice and may remove or disable access to the identified material expeditiously.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Uploader Notification">
    BeatPass takes reasonable steps to notify the uploader about removal and the counter-notice process.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Record Keeping">
    BeatPass may keep the complaint and action records for compliance, repeat-infringer review, and disputes.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  If a notice is incomplete, BeatPass may request missing elements before acting, or act on what can be verified and request clarification for the rest.
</Note>


## For Producers: Responding to a Takedown

If your content was removed and you believe it was a **mistake or misidentification**, you can submit a counter-notice.

### What to Include in a Counter-Notice

1. **Your signature**: Physical or electronic
2. **Identify the removed material**: What was removed and the URL where it appeared before removal
3. **Good-faith statement**: Under penalty of perjury, that the material was removed as a result of mistake or misidentification
4. **Contact information and jurisdiction consent**: Your name, mailing address, and telephone number, plus consent to the appropriate U.S. Federal District Court and acceptance of service from the original complainant or their agent

<Accordion title="Copy a counter-notice template">

```
Subject: DMCA Counter-Notice – [Your Track/Upload]

I, [full name], am the uploader of the material described below.

1) Material removed and its former location on BeatPass:
   [BeatPass page/track title and URL if available]

2) Good-faith statement:
   I state, under penalty of perjury, that I have a good-faith belief
   that the material was removed or disabled as a result of mistake or
   misidentification.

3) Contact information, consent to jurisdiction, and service:
   Name:
   Mailing address:
   Phone:
   Email:
   I consent to the jurisdiction of the Federal District Court for my
   judicial district (or, if outside the United States, any district in
   which BeatPass may be found) and I will accept service of process
   from the original complainant or their agent.

Signature: [type full name to e-sign or provide physical signature]
Date:
```

</Accordion>

### Restoration Timeline

Under [17 U.S.C. §512(g)](https://uscode.house.gov/us/usc/119-124/t17/s512/), the restoration window is **10 to 14 business days after BeatPass receives a compliant counter-notice**. BeatPass forwards the counter-notice to the original complainant.

If BeatPass receives notice that the complainant has filed an action seeking a court order against the allegedly infringing activity, restoration may be prevented. Contact support about your case rather than assuming a specific restoration date.


## Repeat Infringer Policy

Repeated uncontested notices or a pattern of infringement may lead to upload restrictions, monetization limits, suspension, or termination. BeatPass considers the circumstances under its policy. The policy does not promise a fixed number of warnings or a set sequence of actions.

<Warning>
  Accounts that receive repeated, uncontested takedown notices may face **permanent termination**. Always ensure your uploads are original works that you own or have rights to license.
</Warning>


## Protecting Your Work as a Producer

### Proactive Steps

- **Register your beats** with a Performing Rights Organization (PRO) like ASCAP, BMI, or SOCAN
- **Keep records** of your original project files (DAW sessions, stems, creation dates)
- **Use BeatPassID**: BeatPass's audio fingerprinting system helps identify your tracks. See [BeatPassID](/help/downloads-and-licensing/beatpass-id)
- **Check Content ID compatibility**: Before registering a beat, make sure your rights and provider rules allow it and that you can resolve claims affecting valid licensees. See [Content ID Claims](/help/downloads-and-licensing/content-id-claims)

### If You Find Your Beat Stolen

1. **Document the infringement**: Screenshot the infringing page, note the URL, producer name, and upload date
2. **File a DMCA takedown notice** using the template above
3. **Contact BeatPass support** at [support@beatpass.ca](mailto:support@beatpass.ca) for additional help


## Important Warnings

<Warning>
  **Misrepresentation liability**: Any person who knowingly and materially misrepresents that material is infringing, or that material was removed by mistake, may be liable for damages including costs and attorneys' fees. Ensure your submissions are accurate and made in good faith.
</Warning>

<Note>
  This guide explains the process and includes a template for convenience. Read the [official DMCA policy](https://open.beatpass.ca/pages/dmca-notice-and-takedown-policy) and seek legal advice for a disputed claim or counter-notice.
</Note>


## Content ID vs. DMCA

These are different processes:

| | Content ID Claims | DMCA Takedown |
|---|---|---|
| **Where** | YouTube, Spotify, other DSPs | BeatPass platform |
| **What** | A platform identifies audio in a release | A rights holder alleges copyright infringement on BeatPass |
| **How to resolve** | Submit your license as evidence through the platform's review process; it does not guarantee clearance | Send a copyright notice with the required information |
| **More info** | [Content ID Claims](/help/downloads-and-licensing/content-id-claims) | This page |

## AI-Origin Reports

If your concern is that a beat is fully AI-generated, uses undisclosed AI, includes unverifiable AI samples, lacks commercial-use rights, or violates BeatPass's human-made content standard, contact BeatPass support instead of using the DMCA template unless you are also making a copyright claim.

Include the beat title, producer name, URL if available, and a clear explanation of the AI-origin or rights-verification concern. See [AI Audio and Human-Made Content](/help/legal/ai-audio-human-made-content) for the platform standard and [Non-DMCA Rights & Trust Report Policy](/help/legal/non-dmca-rights-trust-report-policy) for the broader non-copyright report path.
