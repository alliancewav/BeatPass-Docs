This guide summarizes the [official Exclusive License AI and Rights Addendum](https://open.beatpass.ca/pages/exclusive-license-ai-and-rights-addendum). Read it with the [Exclusive License Agreement](/help/legal/exclusive-license-agreement), your offer, and your purchase documents.

## Before you buy or list a beat

- Exclusive beats must remain human-made. Fully AI-generated beats are not permitted.
- The producer must have the rights needed to license the beat, including any AI-assisted elements or samples.
- Producers must disclose AI use and provide Proof of Rights when requested.
- An exclusive purchase does not include AI training, datasets, sample packs, or competing-library use unless all required parties separately agree in writing.

## What your certificate records

Your certificate records the purchase and the rights issued from the producer's offer. Verification checks the BeatPass record. It does not guarantee that no third-party claim or rights issue exists, or that a distributor will accept a release.

Keep your certificate and any support correspondence. They can help explain your license if a distributor, label, or rights team asks questions.

## If a rights issue comes up

Contact [BeatPass support](https://open.beatpass.ca/contact) with the beat, purchase details, and relevant claim or notice. BeatPass may review the issue, request proof, explain the certificate record, or consider support, credits, or a refund under the [Refund Policy](/help/billing/refund-policy). A monetary refund is not guaranteed.

A request to review your certificate does not by itself mean that you did anything wrong. BeatPass cannot grant rights the producer did not own or control.

## Which terms apply

The official addendum addresses AI and rights issues where generic certificate wording differs. A signed written agreement between all required parties controls where the addendum says it does. This guide does not change an earlier license or purchase.
