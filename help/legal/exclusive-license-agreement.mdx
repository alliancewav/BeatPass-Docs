This page summarizes the [official Exclusive License Agreement](https://open.beatpass.ca/pages/exclusive-license-agreement) and the [Exclusive AI and Rights Addendum](/help/legal/exclusive-license-ai-and-rights-addendum). It applies when a buyer purchases exclusive rights to a beat through BeatPass.

<Warning>
  Exclusive rights depend on the exact preview, checkout terms, certificate, and any signed agreement for that purchase.
</Warning>

## Names and acceptance

Buyer and producer accounts must contain their complete legal first and last names. The producer signs the current exclusive offer and confirms authority to license for all rights holders. The buyer accepts the displayed terms and electronic signature before payment. BeatPass records the accepted identities and terms with the purchase and certificate.

Missing account names or incomplete signed identities lock the certificate and make public verification report an action required. This technical check does not itself revoke an existing grant. Completing an older certificate preserves its original purchase, terms, identifier and issue date.

## What Exclusive Means

An exclusive purchase stops future BeatPass licensing of that beat to new buyers. It does not cancel non-exclusive licenses that were validly issued before the exclusive purchase.

The producer keeps ownership except for the rights granted to the exclusive buyer under the applicable exclusive terms.

## Files and Rights

The buyer receives the files listed in the exclusive preview, checkout, or certificate. Rights such as Content ID, broadcast, sync, producer/buyer shares, royalty obligations, territory, and stems depend on the terms shown for that specific purchase.

## AI and Dataset Restrictions

Exclusive licensing does not allow AI training, dataset creation, model development, sample-pack creation, competing-library use, or reusable generative assets unless all required parties separately agree in writing.

## Rights Issues

BeatPass relies on producer warranties, AI disclosures, Proof of Rights, and license configuration when issuing exclusive certificates. If BeatPass later restricts a beat because of verified rights issues, AI misrepresentation, or Proof of Rights failure, BeatPass may provide support, documentation, credit, refund review, or certificate-status context under applicable policies.
