Build a shortlist of beats to return to. Open your **profile avatar** → **My library**, then **Liked Beats**, or open [your saved beats](https://open.beatpass.ca/library/songs).

The heading shows **My songs** or your liked-song count, with **Your favorite tracks in one place** underneath.

<LibraryFigure />

## Save, find, and play

1. Save a beat from the player, its page, or its card.
2. Return here and use **Search within tracks** to find it.
3. Play a beat from its row, or open the beat or producer page to explore further.
4. Use the table's sort and filter controls to narrow your shortlist.

Saving a beat keeps it handy. Before you download or release music, [check your licensing access](/help/downloads-and-licensing).

## Can't find a saved beat?

Clear search and filters, then check that you are using the account that saved it. **No beats yet** appears when the list has no saved beats or matching results. Open the beat again to check its saved state.
