Keep the producers you want to hear again in one place. Open your **profile avatar** → **My library**, then **Following**, or go straight to [your saved producers](https://open.beatpass.ca/library/artists).

The heading shows **My artists** or your liked-artist count, with **Your favorite producers** underneath.

## Save a producer

Open a producer page and select **Follow**. **Following** confirms the producer is saved. Select it again to remove that producer from your saved list.

## Find someone you saved

Use **Search within artists**, then choose **Recently added** or **A-Z** to arrange the results. Select a producer to open their page and explore their beats and collections.

## Can't find a producer?

Clear the search first and check that you are signed into the account that followed them. **No producers found** appears when there are no saved producers or no results for your search. Visit the producer's page to check the **Follow** state.

For new-upload alerts, review [Notification Preferences](/help/notifications/notification-settings). To send a message, follow the person's user account when prompted; that is separate from following their producer page.
