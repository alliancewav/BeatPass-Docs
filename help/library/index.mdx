## Find your next stop

**Your library** opens the overview. Choose a section along the top; on a narrow screen, swipe the section chips sideways to find more.

## Add something worth keeping

Like a beat, save a collection, follow a producer, or create a playlist. Return to its library section to find it again. History appears as you listen; licenses appear after a qualifying download or completed purchase.

<Accordion title="Something is missing from my library">
  Check that you are signed in to the same account, then clear any search or filter that could hide the item. A followed or collaborative playlist can appear when it is attached to your account. For certificates, follow [license recovery](/help/downloads-and-licensing/license-certificates).
</Accordion>

Saving or playing music does not grant release rights. Check [Downloads and licensing](/help/downloads-and-licensing) before using a beat in your project.
