The **Licenses** page is the central place to review BeatPass license records tied to your account.

## How to get here

Open your **profile avatar** → **My library**, then choose **My Licenses**. The page title is **Your licenses** with the subtitle **View and manage all your track licenses**.

<LicenseWalletFigure />

## What you can do

- **Search** by track, artist, licensee, or license ID
- **Download** a printable certificate when that license is ready
- Open **Details**, then **Verify**, for the public verification page in a new tab
- Review licenses granted to other users if you own tracks on BeatPass

## What appears on each license card

Each card shows:

- Track name (linked to the track page) and credited artists
- License type and licensed party
- Status and any required identity review or waiting state
- **Details**, where you can find the issued date and certificate ID

Read the status label rather than relying on colour: an incomplete or unavailable certificate can use a warning colour.

## Empty state

If you have no licenses yet, the page shows:

**No licenses yet** means there are no records to show. An eligible licensed download or completed purchase can create one; downloading your own producer source file does not.

## Important notes

- The **Download** action on a license card opens the printable certificate page — it does not re-download the audio file.
- License records reflect the terms at the time of issuance. Certificates include the available BeatPass ID and fingerprint details used for verification.
- Open **My Licenses** from the library, or **My licenses** from the download confirmation. Both lead to your license wallet.
