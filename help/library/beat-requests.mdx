Open your **profile avatar** → **My library** → **My Requests**, or **Library** on your phone, to keep your briefs and the music sent back together. The page is titled **Your requests**. Producer accounts can also revisit the tracks they sent to other people.

## Manage requests you created

Use **Your requests** for your own briefs. Choose **Find a request** to reveal search, **All requests**, **Open**, or **Closed**, and **Newest first** or **Ending soon** sorting. **Load more requests** opens the next set when offered.

Each brief shows its description, musical details, reference, timing and received-beat count. Choose **Review beats** to open **Beats for your brief**. Listen with a row's play button, then select the track title or open arrow to review licensing. **Done** returns to the workspace.

## Renewing an expired request

An eligible closed request offers **Reopen · 1 token** or **Reopen request**, with its duration shown. Reopening costs **one token** and uses the original request's **24**, **48**, or **72-hour** window. The renewal window is **30 days** after expiry.

If the action is absent, create a new brief when you have enough available tokens. [Managing requests](/help/beat-requests/managing-requests) explains the lifecycle and recovery paths.

## Deleting a request

Read **Delete request?** before confirming. Deleting removes the brief and its submission history from your workspace; this flow has no restore action. Deleting an open or closed request returns its original token cost only up to your current plan cap. After **Request deleted.**, choose **New request** to check the refreshed available tokens.

## If you are a producer

Choose **Sent beats** to revisit the music you sent with its original brief. Use **Find a brief** to return to the main board and discover another match. **Your requests** remains the separate view of briefs you posted yourself. On an eligible open brief, **Send beats** opens your hosted catalog. The **Review & send** step identifies the result for each selected track; **Retry** covers eligible failures. [Producer request help](/help/beat-requests/for-producers) explains the five-track limit, upload-and-send flow and restrictions.

## Create a new request

Choose **New request** when offered. Write your brief, add the required reference, choose a duration, and review the token cost before **Post request**. [Create a request](/help/beat-requests/creating-requests) explains the fields.

## If a view does not load

Choose **Try again** and check your filters. A failed load does not mean your requests have been deleted. If a returned beat is no longer available, its row says so; a submission does not itself grant release rights.
