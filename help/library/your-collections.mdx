Keep collections you want to revisit in your library. Open your **profile avatar** → **My library**, then **Liked Collections**, or open [your saved collections](https://open.beatpass.ca/library/albums).

The heading shows **My albums** or your liked-album count, with **Your favorite collections** underneath. BeatPass uses both albums and collections for this saved selection.

## Find a collection

1. Save a collection from its page or card.
2. Return to your saved collections and use **Search within albums** to find it.
3. Choose **Recently added**, **A-Z**, or **Release date** to arrange the results.
4. Select the collection to open it and listen.

## Nothing showing?

Clear your search and confirm you are signed into the account that saved the collection. **No collections yet** appears when there are no saved collections or matching results. [Discover collections](/help/discovering-music/albums-and-collections) to find your next set.
