## Check recent alerts

Select the **bell icon** in the top navigation. Its badge shows your unread count. Alerts can become read as they appear in the open list, so the badge may change while you review them. Select an alert to open its linked destination when one is available.

Use **Mark all** to mark every unread notification as read. The gear shortcut opens **Notification Preferences**.

## See more notifications

Open the full [Notifications page](https://open.beatpass.ca/notifications) from the bell menu for a longer list. Use the page controls at the bottom for older alerts and **Settings** to change your preferences. Some alerts have actions you can use directly, such as **Accept** and **Decline** for playlist invitations.

<Note>
  **Mark all as read** on the full page and **Mark all** in the bell menu clear unread notifications across your account. The full-page action is disabled when there are no unread notifications left.
</Note>

## No alerts yet?

**Hang tight!** means there are no notifications to show. Use the settings shortcut to review what you want to receive. **Settings** remains available on the full page.
