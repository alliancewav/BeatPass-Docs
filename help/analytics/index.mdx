## Choose what to explore

- **Overview** shows listening over your selected dates. Collection and producer reports also show **Most Played Tracks**.
- **Audience** groups **Top Devices**, **Top Platforms**, **Top Listeners**, and **Top Locations**.
- A beat's **Overview** includes **Avg Listen Duration** and **Completion Breakdown** when duration-tracked plays are available.
- Your primary producer report adds **Pro Page** for measured storefront activity.

A fresh report starts at **This year**. Saved report links keep their chosen dates and view. Counts marked **Lifetime** remain separate from the period charts.

## Keep the result in context

Use [Finances](/help/producer-dashboard/finances) for actual earnings and payout status, and [Progress](/help/producer-dashboard/progress) for XP and achievements. Listening and visitor activity do not themselves establish a payment or a license.

If a section fails, choose **Try again**. If it is empty, check the period and whether the music has listening activity. [Save a link or capture a chart](/help/analytics/backstage-insights/exporting-data) when you need a record.
