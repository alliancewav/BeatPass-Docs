Open your first **Insights** report, choose a period, and switch between listening activity and the people behind it.

## Before You Begin

Sign in to the producer account that can manage the beat, collection, or producer profile. If an **Insights** action is missing or access is refused, check the account first.

## Open Your First Insights Page

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose your music">
    Open **Insights** from a beat in **My Beats**, a release in **My Collections**, or your producer tools. On desktop, the top **Insights** shortcut opens your primary producer profile.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the report">
    Read the name and artwork in the summary. **Details** opens more information about the beat, collection, or profile. Counts marked **Lifetime** are separate from the chosen reporting period.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose your dates">
    Use the date selector above the report. A fresh report starts at **This year**. Saved links can reopen their chosen dates and view.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose what to explore">
    Start in **Overview** for listening activity. Open **Audience** for devices, platforms, listeners, and locations. Your primary producer report also offers **Pro Page** for activity connected to your shared storefront.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Important Expectations

- A play, a unique person, a Pro Page visit, and a completed license measure different things.
- Check each chart's total and subtitle. A percentage can describe the rows shown or plays with recorded information, rather than every play.
- Recent activity can take time to appear. Scroll to a chart and allow it to load. Choose **Try again** on a failed section; an error is different from zero activity.
- Use [Finances](/help/producer-dashboard/finances) for earnings and [Progress](/help/producer-dashboard/progress) for XP.
- [Save a report link or capture a chart](/help/analytics/backstage-insights/exporting-data) when you need a record.
