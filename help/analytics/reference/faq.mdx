**Insights** separates listening, audience, and storefront activity so you can answer one question at a time. Use these answers when a report is missing or its totals look different.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Who can open Insights?">
    Sign in to the account that can manage the beat, collection, or producer profile. If access is refused, check that account first. Contact support if the result still looks wrong.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Where did the audience charts go?">
    Choose **Audience** for devices, platforms, listeners, and locations. **Overview** holds the listening trend, most-played tracks where available, and a beat's listening-depth detail.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Where is Pro Page Traffic?">
    Open your primary producer's Insights and choose **Pro Page**. The tab follows activity connected to the shared storefront; marketplace listening stays in **Overview**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why do the totals or percentages differ?">
    Lifetime summary counts, period plays, duration-tracked plays, and Pro Page activity use different measures. Audience charts state whether their shares use recorded plays or the rows shown. Read each subtitle and total before comparing.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why is my page empty or partially empty?">
    Check the dates and whether the music has activity. Scroll to a chart and allow it to load. A failed section offers **Try again**; that error is different from no activity.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do analytics update instantly?">
    Recent activity can take time to appear, and sections can refresh separately. Check the date range and try again before concluding that activity is missing.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I export a report?">
    The report has no built-in export button. [Save the report link or capture a chart](/help/analytics/backstage-insights/exporting-data), including its period and capture date.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I compare periods or several beats side by side?">
    Open reports separately and choose matching dates. The date selector sets one reporting period; it does not add a built-in comparison view.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
