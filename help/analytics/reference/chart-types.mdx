Use **Insights** to answer one question at a time: when listening changed, which beats led a release, or where your audience came from.

## Shared Charts

| View | Chart | What to look for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Overview** | **Plays Over Time** | Listening activity across the selected dates |
| **Audience** | **Top Devices** | Share of plays with device information |
| **Audience** | **Top Platforms** | Share of plays with platform information |
| **Audience** | **Top Listeners** | Share of plays across the listeners shown, including grouped guest listening |
| **Audience** | **Top Locations** | Share of plays across the locations shown |

Read the total and subtitle on each chart. A percentage of **plays shown** can differ from a share of all plays. **Other** and **Unknown** categories keep missing or less common information in context when offered.

## Listening detail for one beat

A beat's **Overview** adds **Avg Listen Duration** and **Completion Breakdown** when duration-tracked listening is available. The tracked-play total is separate from plays without duration information. [Read listening detail](/help/analytics/backstage-insights/engagement-metrics).

## Album And Artist Extras

**Most Played Tracks** appears in collection and producer **Overview** reports. Choose a beat in that list to open its own Insights.

Your own primary producer report also has **Pro Page** for storefront activity. It is separate from marketplace listening in Overview. [Read Pro Page Traffic](/help/analytics/backstage-insights/artist-insights#pro-page-traffic).

## Read the result in context

If a chart is empty, check the selected dates and whether activity exists. If a section fails, choose **Try again** there; a failure is not a zero result.
