Use this glossary while reading **Insights** to tell period listening, lifetime summaries, audience shares, and completion apart.

## Core Listening Metrics

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Plays">
    Plays Over Time describes recorded listening in the selected period. Counts marked Lifetime in the summary describe a different time span.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Likes and followers">
    Available summary counts identify the music or producer profile. A Lifetime label means they are separate from the dates selected for the charts.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Duration">
    The length of a beat or combined length of a collection, shown in its details. Total Listen Time measures listening activity instead.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Track Engagement Metrics

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Avg Listen Duration">
    Average listening time for plays with duration tracking in the selected period. Compare it with the track length and the tracked-play total shown.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Average portion heard">
    The average share of the beat heard in tracked listening. It appears with Completion Breakdown.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Total Listen Time">
    Combined listening time represented by the report's duration-tracked plays.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Completed">
    A tracked listen in which more than 80% of the beat was heard. The other completion bands describe shorter portions of the recording.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Audience Breakdown Metrics

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Top Devices and Top Platforms">
    Shares of plays with the corresponding device or platform information. Read recorded plays and any Other or Unknown category shown.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Top Listeners">
    Listening activity across the accounts and guest group shown. Guest Listeners combines guest activity; it is not a count of identified people.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Top Locations">
    Shares across the locations shown. The displayed chart total gives the basis for those percentages.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Most Played Tracks">
    Ranked beats in the selected collection or producer report. Choose a beat to open its own Insights.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Pro Page outcomes

**Engaged visits**, **30-second listens**, **License actions started**, and **Completed licenses and sales** measure activity connected to your shared storefront. Use **What counts?** in the **Pro Page** tab for its current definitions. Started actions can repeat; a completed license or sale requires confirmation by BeatPass.
