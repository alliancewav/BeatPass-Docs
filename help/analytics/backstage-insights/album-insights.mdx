Use **Album Insights** to review a collection as a whole, find its most-played beats, and explore the audience behind the release.

## Opening Album Insights

Open **My Collections**, find the release, and choose **Insights** from its actions. Check its name, then select dates above the report. A fresh report starts at **This year**; saved links can keep other dates and views.

## Header Information

The summary identifies the release and its available lifetime **Plays**, **Likes**, and **Tracks**. Open **Details** for the release or creation date, duration, genres, and other available context. Counts marked **Lifetime** stay separate from period charts.

## Explore the charts

<Tabs sync={false}>
  <Tab title="Overview" icon="chart-line">
    Read **Plays Over Time** for the selected dates. **Most Played Tracks** ranks beats within the collection; select a track to open its own Insights.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Audience" icon="users">
    Review **Top Devices**, **Top Platforms**, **Top Listeners**, and **Top Locations**. Read each chart's subtitle and total before comparing shares.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Look closer at one beat

Open [Track Insights](/help/analytics/backstage-insights/track-insights) for **Avg Listen Duration** and **Completion Breakdown**. Duration and completion detail is available for individual beats.

If a report is empty, check the period and whether the collection's beats have listening activity. If a chart failed, choose **Try again** in that section before drawing a conclusion.
