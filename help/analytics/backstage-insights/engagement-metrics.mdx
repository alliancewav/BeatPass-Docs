A beat's listening detail shows how long people stay and how much of the recording they hear. Open **Track Insights** and choose **Overview** to read it alongside the play trend.

## Track-Only Sections

**Avg Listen Duration** and **Completion Breakdown** are available for individual beats. Collection and producer reports show their listening trend and most-played tracks; open one beat for listening depth.

Completion bands describe the share of a beat heard:

| Band | Portion heard |
| --- | --- |
| **Completed** | More than 80% |
| **Mostly** | More than 60%, up to 80% |
| **Half** | More than 40%, up to 60% |
| **Partial** | More than 20%, up to 40% |
| **Skipped** | 20% or less |

## Use the tracked-play total

Duration and completion use plays with listening-duration information. The report identifies its tracked-play count and, where available, how many plays have no duration data. Those plays can remain in the overall play total without supplying a duration or completion result.

Compare like with like: use the same beat, dates, and chart total. An audience percentage, play total, and completion percentage describe different groups.

## Analytics plays and payout-counted plays

When shown, **Plays in analytics** and **Plays counted toward payouts** are different measures. Ordinary guest listening can appear in analytics. Reviewed artificial activity and plays above the repeat limit are excluded. Historical plays keep their original payout rules. Use the explanation shown on the report rather than treating every play as an earning.

## If the numbers are missing

- **No engagement data yet** means there is not enough duration-tracked listening to show the detail.
- **No tracked listens in this period** can appear when the chosen dates have no usable listening-duration information.
- On an error, choose **Try again** in the failed section. An unavailable report is different from zero listening.
