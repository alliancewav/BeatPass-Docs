Use a beat's **Track Insights** to see when people listen and how much of the beat they hear. Switch to **Audience** when you want to explore the listeners behind that activity.

## Opening Track Insights

Open **My Beats**, find the beat, and choose **Insights** from its actions. Check the name and artwork, then use the date selector. A fresh report starts at **This year**; saved links can keep other dates and views.

## What You See On This Page

<Tabs sync={false}>
  <Tab title="Overview" icon="chart-line">
    Start with **Plays Over Time**. When duration-tracked plays are available, the listening reports show:

    - **Avg Listen Duration**, with average listening time, track length, total listening time, and completion context.
    - **Completion Breakdown**, with the average portion heard and the completed, mostly, half, partial, and skipped bands.

    <TrackOverviewFigure />

    Inspect a point on the time chart to read its value. [Engagement help](/help/analytics/backstage-insights/engagement-metrics) explains why tracked-play totals can differ from the full play count.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Audience" icon="users">
    Review **Top Devices**, **Top Platforms**, **Top Listeners**, and **Top Locations** for the same dates. Their subtitles explain the plays included in each share.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

The summary identifies the beat and its available lifetime counts. Open **Details** for information such as its upload date, duration, BPM, and key. Counts marked **Lifetime** do not become period totals when you change the date selector.

## Empty States You May See

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="No engagement data yet">
    Listening detail appears when the beat has plays with duration tracking. A play count can exist before that detail is available.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="No tracked listens in this period">
    Check the period. Plays without listening-duration information can still appear in play totals but do not supply duration or completion detail.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="A chart could not load">
    Choose **Try again** in the failed section. A failed request is different from a period with no activity.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

Use the explanation on the report to distinguish analytics plays from plays counted toward payouts. A play is not itself a guaranteed earning.
