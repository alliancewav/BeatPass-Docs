To keep a record of an **Insights** report, choose your dates and save its link or capture the chart you need. These report pages have no built-in export action.

<Info>
  A report link keeps the chosen dates and view, but the figures can change as activity updates. For a fixed record, include the date range and capture date with your screenshot or notes.
</Info>

## Save or share a report

- Use the date range selector to isolate the period you want to review.
- Share the direct insight page link with teammates who already have access to that content.
- Capture screenshots or manual notes when you need to reference a specific chart view.

## If you need help

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Sharing a report link">
    Share only with someone who already has access to that report. A copied link does not grant access to your account.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="A chart is missing from my capture">
    Scroll to the chart and wait for it to load. If it shows an error, refresh the report before saving a record.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
