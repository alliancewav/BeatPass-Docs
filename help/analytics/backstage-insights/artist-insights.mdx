Use **Artist Insights** to follow listening across your producer catalog, then choose a beat to investigate. **Overview**, **Audience**, and your own **Pro Page** keep the different questions separate.

## Opening Artist Insights

Open your producer **Insights** action from the desktop top navigation or producer tools. Check the profile name, then choose dates above the report. A fresh report starts at **This year**; saved links can keep other dates and views.

## Header Information

The summary identifies your profile and its available lifetime counts, including **Followers**, **Albums**, and **Plays**. **Details** opens more profile information. These summary counts are separate from the selected reporting period.

## Pro Page Traffic

Your primary producer profile has a **Pro Page** tab. Open it to review activity connected to your shareable storefront. If the page is not ready, follow the unlock checklist shown there.

<ProPageInsightsFigure />

| Result | What it describes |
| --- | --- |
| **Engaged visits** | Active sessions on your shared page |
| **30-second listens** | Plays that reached at least 30 seconds |
| **License actions started** | Standard or exclusive licensing actions selected on the Pro Page |
| **Completed licenses and sales** | Matching downloads or purchases confirmed by BeatPass |

Select **What counts?** to read the definitions and dates used by the current report. Started actions are separate from completed licenses; repeated actions can count more than once.

The tab also shows **Where visits came from**, **Licensing activity**, memberships started, and **Exclusive earnings** when available. Earnings stay separate by currency. A similarity in numbers does not make visits, plays, subscriptions, and sales interchangeable.

Detailed Pro Page activity has a retention limit; daily totals remain available for older measured periods. Before tracking began, the report gives guidance instead of estimated activity. A partly measured range is identified. Regular marketplace listening remains in **Overview**.

See [Your Producer Pro Page](/help/producer-dashboard/pro-page) for the unlock, sharing, and campaign guide.

## Open a beat for listening depth

Open [Track Insights](/help/analytics/backstage-insights/track-insights) for **Avg Listen Duration** and **Completion Breakdown**. These charts use the beat's plays with duration tracking, so their total can differ from all recorded plays.

If a section fails, choose **Try again** there. If it is empty, check your dates and the explanation shown before treating a missing report as zero.
