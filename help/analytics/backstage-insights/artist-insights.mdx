Artist Insights is the broadest Backstage Insights view. It combines activity across one artist profile so you can review catalog performance from a single page.

## Opening Artist Insights

Open the **Insights** action on any artist profile you manage from Backstage. The page loads with the **This year** date range by default — adjust it with the floating date-range selector if needed.

## Header Information

The current artist header can include:

- Follower count
- Album count
- Total plays
- Genres

<Note>
  The artist insights header does not display a track count. For track-level detail, open the insights page for a specific track or album instead.
</Note>

## Pro Page Traffic

When Pro Page measurement is available for your artist and date range, **Pro Page Traffic** appears above the regular Artist Insights charts. It focuses on the outcomes created by your shareable storefront:

- **Engaged visits** — visitors who stayed active
- **30-second listens** — plays that reached at least 30 seconds
- **Licenses completed** — licensed downloads completed from the Pro Page journey
- **Exclusive sales** — exclusive purchases completed from the Pro Page journey

The section can also show where measured visitors came from, which share buttons they used, and exclusive earnings separated by currency. Select **How these numbers are counted** to read the definitions used for the current report.

<Note>
  A date range before measurement began shows guidance instead of estimated activity. A partly measured range is marked clearly, and regular marketplace plays stay in the reports below.
</Note>

See [Your Producer Pro Page](/help/producer-dashboard/pro-page) for the unlock, sharing, and campaign guide.

## What Artist Insights Does Not Show

Artist Insights does not include the track-only engagement modules: **Engagement Overview**, **Listen Duration**, or **Completion Breakdown**. Open a specific [Track Insights](/help/analytics/backstage-insights/track-insights) page for that detail.
