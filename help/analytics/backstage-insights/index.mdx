Use **Insights** to follow one beat, collection, or producer profile through its listening history. Choose the report, set your dates, and select the view that answers your question.

## Choose The Right View

  <Card title="Track Insights" href="/help/analytics/backstage-insights/track-insights" icon="music">
    See listening over time, duration, and how much of one beat people hear.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Album Insights" href="/help/analytics/backstage-insights/album-insights" icon="folder">

  </Card>

## Read your report

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check the music and dates">
    Confirm the name and artwork. Open **Details** for more context. A fresh report starts at **This year**; saved links can keep other dates. Counts marked **Lifetime** do not change with the reporting period.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start in Overview">
    Read **Plays Over Time**. Collection and producer reports add **Most Played Tracks**; choose a track there to open its own Insights. A beat adds duration and completion detail when listening has been tracked.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Explore Audience">
    Choose **Audience** or **Explore your audience** to see devices, platforms, listeners, and locations. Check each chart's subtitle and total before comparing percentages.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review your shared storefront">
    Your own primary producer report also has a **Pro Page** tab. This activity is connected to your shareable storefront and is separate from regular marketplace listening.
  </Step>
</Steps>
