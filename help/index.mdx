## Explore at your own pace

Keep a [playlist](/help/playlists), start a [conversation](/help/messaging), post a [beat request](/help/beat-requests), or make your [profile](/help/profiles) yours. Search using the label you see in the app when you need a specific screen.

Before releasing music, check your [license terms](/help/legal/license-terms). If something gets in the way, [find a fix](/help/troubleshooting) or [ask support](/help/contact-support). You do not need to know the technical cause.

[What's new](/release-notes) follows product updates. Building an integration? The separate [API & Developers](/developers/overview) area has the technical reference.
