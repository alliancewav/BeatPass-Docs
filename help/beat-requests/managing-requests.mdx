Return to **Your requests** to review the beats sent back, reopen an eligible brief, or remove a request you no longer need. Read the token and deletion consequences before confirming.

## Viewing Your Requests

Open [Library → My Requests](https://open.beatpass.ca/library/custom-requests). The page is titled **Your requests**. From the main **Beat Requests** board, **Your requests** opens the same workspace.

If you have producer upload access, choose **Your requests** for your own briefs or **Sent beats** for music you submitted to other people.

Use **Find a request** to reveal search and filters. Search your briefs, choose **All requests**, **Open**, or **Closed**, and sort by **Newest first** or **Ending soon**. **Load more requests** opens the next set when available.

### Request Status

- An open brief accepts new producer submissions until its selected time ends.
- **Closed** means new submissions have stopped. Existing received beats can still be reviewed while the record is retained.
- Eligible closed requests offer **Reopen · 1 token** or **Reopen request** with the duration shown.

## Tracking Active Requests

Your brief shows its musical details, reference, remaining time and number of beats received. Counts represent submitted tracks, not distinct producers.

Choose **Review beats** to open **Beats for your brief**. Play a response inline, select its title or open arrow for its track page and licensing, then choose **Done** to return. Receiving a submission does not grant a license.

## Deleting a Request

Open the request's delete action and read **Delete request?** before confirming **Delete request**. The action removes the brief and its submission history from your workspace. Keep the record if you still need it for review or follow-up; there is no restore action in this flow.

Deleting an open or closed request returns the original token cost only up to your current plan cap. Deletion never raises the balance above that cap. After **Request deleted.**, choose **New request** to check the available tokens. If the result does not follow these rules, contact support with the request details.

## Request History

Your briefs and received beats stay in **Your requests** after expiry while the records are retained. The **30-day** reopening window does not itself remove the history. Use the status filter instead of looking for separate active and expired sections. A notification link can reopen a particular brief. If it is no longer available, the page explains that and shows your request history.

### Renewing Expired Requests

Eligible requests can be reopened within **30 days** of expiry for **one token**. The new window matches the original **24**, **48**, or **72-hour** duration.

Read the duration shown, choose **Reopen request** or **Reopen · 1 token**, then check the new status and allowance. You can reopen only your own eligible requests. If the action is absent, create a new brief when you have enough tokens.

## My Requests vs. All Requests

The main [Beat Requests board](https://open.beatpass.ca/beat-requests) is for finding briefs. **All briefs**, **No replies yet**, and **With a budget** narrow that feed; search and genre controls refine it further.

**Your requests** in the Library is for your own records. Producers also have **Sent beats**, which keeps each submitted track with its original brief. Those submissions remain separate from requests you created.

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="I cannot find a brief">
    Check the account, search and status filters. Choose **Clear filters** when offered. A deleted or no-longer-retained brief may no longer open from an older link.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The view could not load">
    Choose **Try again**. A loading failure is different from an empty request list; the error does not mean your saved requests were deleted.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I cannot see or play a submitted beat">
    Open **Review beats** and allow the list to load. An unavailable track is identified in its row. A submitted track still needs the normal license before release use.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="My token result looks wrong">
    Check the balance and current plan cap after the action succeeds. Contact support with the request reference and visible result if they do not match the confirmation.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
