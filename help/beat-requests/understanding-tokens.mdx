Tokens are your allowance for posting **Beat Requests**. The length of a brief decides its cost; receiving or sending a beat does not grant a license.

## How Tokens Work

<Columns cols={2}>
  <Card title="Post a brief" icon="plus">
    **24 hours** costs **1 token**, **48 hours** costs **2**, and **72 hours** costs **3**.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Reopen an eligible brief" icon="rotate-left">
    Reopening costs **1 token** and keeps the original **24**, **48**, or **72-hour** duration.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Monthly allowance" icon="coins">
    Your plan sets the allowance. Unused tokens are replaced when the monthly cycle resets.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Plan changes" icon="arrows-rotate">
    An eligible plan change can refresh the allowance when processed. Check the resulting balance after the change takes effect.
  </Card>
</Columns>

## Token Allowance by Plan

Compare the current request allowances on the [pricing page](https://open.beatpass.ca/pricing). Use the choices offered to your account; producer access stays free, and a paid plan does not bypass producer licensing restrictions.

## Checking Your Token Balance

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open a new request">
    Open [Beat Requests](https://open.beatpass.ca/beat-requests) or **Your requests** in your Library, then choose **New request**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check available tokens and cost">
    In **New beat request**, the footer shows the number of tokens available. Choose **24**, **48**, or **72 hours** under **How long should it stay open?** and compare the cost beside **Post request**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check a changed balance">
    After posting, reopening, deleting, or a plan change, open **New request** again to check the refreshed allowance. If you have no available tokens, **Your next request** shows the reset information offered to your account.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If **We could not load your request allowance.** appears, choose **Try again**. A failed check does not mean the balance is zero.

## Using Tokens

Tokens are spent when a request is successfully posted or an eligible closed request is reopened. Browsing briefs, listening to responses, or preparing an unposted draft does not spend them.

If posting or reopening cannot be confirmed, check **Your requests** before trying again. An interrupted response does not necessarily mean the action failed.

## Getting Tokens Back (Refunds)

Deleting one of your open or closed requests returns its original token cost, limited by your current plan allowance. There is no restore action in this flow, so keep a brief if you still need its submission history.

| Scenario | Token return |
| --- | --- |
| Delete an open or closed request with enough room below your cap | Original cost of **1**, **2**, or **3 tokens** |
| Delete a request with only some room below your cap | Only the amount needed to reach the cap |
| Delete a request while already at the cap | None |
| A request expires naturally | No automatic return |

<Warning>
  Deletion never takes your balance above your plan's current allowance. Token returns are not cash refunds, and tokens are returned to the original request owner.
</Warning>

Read **Delete request?** before confirming **Delete request**. **Request deleted.** confirms removal. Open **New request** to check the resulting available tokens. If the outcome does not match these rules, contact support with the request details.

## Token Reset Cycle

On the monthly reset, your balance is replaced by your plan's current allowance. Unused tokens do not carry over. When no tokens are available, the request form shows **Your request tokens reset on** with the available date.

A successfully processed plan change can also refresh your allowance. Follow the effective date in **Billing**; choosing a future change does not give you the new allowance immediately.

## Running Out of Tokens

Existing open briefs can still receive beats. For another request, wait for the reset, delete a brief you no longer need to recover tokens within your cap, or compare a higher allowance if **View plans** is offered.

Your draft stays saved while you explore plans. Check it again before posting.

## Upgrading for More Tokens

Open **Billing → Change plan**, or **View plans** when offered in the request form. Compare the allowance, price, full charge, and effective date. After the change is processed, return to **New request** to check your balance.

BeatPass has no one-off token-pack purchase in this flow.

## Common Questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can I reopen any closed request?">
    You can reopen your own eligible request within **30 days** of expiry. **Reopen · 1 token** or **Reopen request** shows the action and its original duration. If it is unavailable, create a new brief when you have enough tokens.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why did deletion return fewer tokens than the request cost?">
    Returns stop at your current plan allowance. A request that cost three tokens returns only one if your balance has room for one. At the cap, nothing is added.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does a closed request disappear when it can no longer reopen?">
    Reopening eligibility and your saved history are separate. Closed briefs and received beats remain in **Your requests** while the records are retained; use the status filter to find them.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does a request guarantee a beat or a license?">
    No. Producers send catalog tracks that may fit your brief. Listen and review the track's licensing choices before downloading, buying, or releasing music.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
