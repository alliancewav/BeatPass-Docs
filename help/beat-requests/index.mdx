Tell producers the sound you are looking for, then listen to the beats they send. A Beat Request can lead you to an existing catalog track or a new beat that fits your brief. Receiving a submission does not grant a license.

Read the [Custom Beat and Beat Request Terms](/help/legal/custom-beat-request-terms) for tokens, delivery discussions, and disputes. A reference is creative direction, not permission to copy a protected beat or skip licensing the exact one you want.

## Reference tracks guide fit. They do not replace licensing.

Use a reference to explain mood, tempo, arrangement, or energy. If the exact beat you want is already available, license it from its original producer. Request a similar direction or a different fit without asking someone to recreate protected elements.

## Quick Facts

| Request window | Token cost |
| --- | --- |
| 24 hours | 1 token |
| 48 hours | 2 tokens |
| 72 hours | 3 tokens |

A reference track is required. The description allows up to **500 characters**. Each producer can send up to **five different tracks** to one request. [Request specifications](/help/beat-requests/request-specifications) helps you describe genre, mood, BPM, and key.

## Who Can Use Beat Requests?

### Creating Requests

Signed-in users can create requests when enough tokens are available.

### Sending Beats as a Producer

To send music, you need a producer profile, upload access, and an eligible hosted track. External links cannot be submitted as beats. You cannot submit to your own request, send a track twice, or submit after the window closes.

## Open the submitted beats

Use **Review beats** from your request card or the library. In **Beats for your brief**, play the available responses and open their track pages for licensing. Its count and your notification settings help you follow replies.

If a submitted beat is no longer available, the dialog identifies it. If the list cannot load, choose **Try again**. Discuss fit or a custom arrangement in [messages](/help/messaging); use the track's actual licensing flow before relying on release rights.

## Request Lifecycle

A request consumes its token cost when posted and stays active for the full selected window, even after submissions arrive. New submissions stop at expiry.

Eligible expired requests can be renewed within **30 days** for **1 token**. Renewal uses the original request duration. Follow the current action and time shown in [request management](/help/beat-requests/managing-requests).

Deleting a request removes it from the public feed and current management flow. Deleting an open or closed request returns its original token cost only up to your current plan cap. Read [token guidance](/help/beat-requests/understanding-tokens) before removing a brief and its submission history.

## Best Practices

### For Requesters

Describe what should fit your song, not just a genre. Use a reference for direction, leave space for the producer's own ideas, and review submissions while the request is active.

### For Producers

Choose relevant work and respond when a requester continues the conversation. A submission does not guarantee a sale or a custom delivery agreement.
