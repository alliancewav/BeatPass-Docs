A useful brief gives producers a sound to aim for while leaving room for their own music. Start with the two required fields, then add the optional details that matter to your song.

## Overview of Fields

| Area | What to provide |
| --- | --- |
| **What are you hearing?** | A description of 10–500 characters |
| **Reference track** | A complete public http or https link |
| **Style & mood** | An optional genre and feeling |
| **Tempo** | An optional preset or custom BPM/range |
| **Key & scale** | An optional key and major/minor preference |
| **Budget** | Optional spending guidance and currency |
| **How long should it stay open?** | 24, 48, or 72 hours, using one, two, or three tokens |

## Description (Required)

In **What are you hearing?**, describe the sound, energy, arrangement or space your vocals need. The field shows your progress toward the **500-character** maximum; use at least **10 characters**.

For example: “Warm keys, a laid-back groove, and space for a melodic hook. I like the energy of the reference, but want an original melody and drums.”

## Reference Track (Required)

Use **Reference track** for a complete public link. The form suggests BeatPass, YouTube, Spotify and SoundCloud links. Check that the producer can open and hear it without requesting access from you.

### How to use a reference track responsibly

Explain which elements guide the fit: tempo, spaciousness, hook placement, or mood. A reference does not authorize copying another person's melody, recording, or protected beat. If you want that exact beat, use its original producer's licensing flow.

## Genre (Optional)

Open **Style & mood** and choose **Genre**. Leave **Any genre** if you want producers to suggest a direction. If genres fail to load, **Try again** reloads them; the brief can still be posted without a genre.

## BPM Range (Optional)

Open **Tempo**. Choose **Any tempo**, **Slower**, **Midtempo**, **Faster**, or **Custom**. The preset shows its BPM range. **Custom** opens **Your tempo (BPM)** for a number or range, such as 90 or 90-110.

Use a range when flexibility matters. Explain half-time feel or another important rhythmic detail in the brief rather than assuming a number tells the whole story.

## Key (Optional)

Open **Key & scale** and choose the key and **Scale**. **Any key** and **Any scale** leave room for ideas. A preference is useful when the song already has vocals or another part in a fixed key.

## Mood (Optional)

**Style & mood** also offers **Mood** choices. Use **More moods** for additional options. Explain the important feeling in your description instead of depending on a label alone.

## Budget (Optional)

Open **Budget**. Leave **Open budget**, choose a displayed range, or use **Custom** for **Amount or range**. Choose the currency offered by the form.

Budget is guidance, not a payment, reservation, or promise of delivery. Catalog beats still use their normal standard or exclusive licensing flow. Agree separately with the producer on any extra custom work, delivery and payment.

## Return to your brief

After changing an optional panel, choose **Done** or **Back to brief**. This returns to your request; it does not post it. Check the reference, duration and token cost, then choose **Post request** when ready.
