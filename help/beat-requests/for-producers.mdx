Find an artist looking for the sound you already make. Read their request, choose eligible tracks from your BeatPass catalog, and send a focused selection they can listen to and license.

## Who Can Submit to Requests

You need a producer profile, upload access, and at least one BeatPass-hosted track you own or collaborate on. Both verified and non-verified producers can submit. External links cannot be sent as beat submissions.

Verified producers receive automatic new-request notifications. Other eligible producers can browse the feed and submit when they find a match.

## Finding Beat Requests

### Browsing Active Requests

Open **Beat Requests** and browse **Open briefs**. Read the description, optional genre/BPM/key/mood, reference track, and remaining time. The submission count counts tracks; one producer can send several.

### Filtering and Sorting

Use **All briefs**, **No replies yet**, or **With a budget** to narrow the board. Search by sound, genre, or requester, choose a genre, and sort by **Newest first** or **Ending soon**. **Your requests** opens the requests you posted in your Library. Closed briefs do not accept new submissions.

## Submitting a Beat

### Step-by-Step

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check the fit">
    Read the brief and listen to the reference. Match its direction without copying protected parts of another person's music.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Send beats">
    Choose **Send beats** on a request. The dialog lists eligible tracks you own or collaborate on, and marks tracks already submitted.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a short selection">
    Select the beats that fit. You can submit up to **five different tracks per request**, each once. Use **Upload & send** if you need to add a new hosted beat before sending it through the flow.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review and confirm">
    Choose **Send** to review, then **Send** to submit. Check every beat's result before closing the dialog. If only some succeed, keep those results and use **Retry** for the remaining eligible beats.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### What Gets Submitted

The requester receives the existing BeatPass track, rather than an external file or a promised new custom beat. A new upload becomes a hosted track before it can be submitted. **Upload & send** publishes the beat to your catalog first, then sends it to the request. If the upload is saved but sending fails, choose **Try sending again** or **View catalog**; avoid uploading the same file again just to retry the send.

### Restrictions

You cannot submit to your own request, send the same track twice, exceed five tracks from your account, or submit after expiry. BeatPass can also reject obviously duplicate audio. A request stays open to other producers until its selected time ends.

## After You Submit


### What Happens

The submission count increases and the requester can receive an alert according to their notification settings. They can listen, open the track and its license choices, or message you. A submission does not guarantee a reply or a purchase.

### Success Messages

The **Review & send** step shows a result for each beat. Later, open [Library → My Requests](https://open.beatpass.ca/library/custom-requests), then **Sent beats** to revisit the music and its original brief. Return to an open brief and choose **Send beats** when your remaining slots allow another submission.

### Error Messages

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="I already sent this track">
    Choose a different eligible beat. **You have already submitted this track to this request** means that track cannot be sent to the same request again.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I reached the five-track limit">
    **Submission limit reached (5 tracks max)** means your producer account has used its slots for that request. The cap applies to you, not to every producer combined.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The request expired">
    The **24**, **48**, or **72-hour** window has closed. New submissions are unavailable unless the requester renews it.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="My track or account is not eligible">
    Check that you own or collaborate on the track, that it is hosted on BeatPass, and that your account has upload access. If **Send beats** is unavailable, use the producer account that can upload and submit your hosted music.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Best Practices

### Choosing What to Submit

Choose the strongest matches for the requested mood and direction. Read the whole brief, including its reference, rather than sending every style in your catalog.

### Building Connections

If the requester continues the conversation, use [messages](/help/messaging) to clarify fit or a custom arrangement. An exclusive purchase still uses the normal track-licensing flow; a request or a chat does not replace a license.

### Timing

Send while the request is active so the artist has time to listen. Check the displayed time remaining before preparing another submission.

## Notifications for Producers

### Getting Notified

Automatic new-request notifications are for verified producers. Available **Email**, **Push**, and **In-App** channels still depend on your preferences and, for push, device permission.

### Configuring Notifications

Open the **bell icon** → **gear icon** → **Notification Preferences**. Under **Beat Requests** → **Matching Requests**, choose the channels offered to your account.

## Common Questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Do I need to make a new beat?">
    No. Send a suitable beat already in your catalog, or use **Upload & send** to add one before submitting it. A request is not automatically a custom-production agreement.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I withdraw a submission?">
    Submissions cannot currently be withdrawn. Check your selection before confirming.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How do I become verified?">
    Follow [Get Verified](/help/producer-program/application-types/get-verified) for the current Discord identity-review steps.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
