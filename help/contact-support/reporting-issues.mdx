Report a BeatPass issue where the team can find the affected content. A short description, exact link, and what you saw are more useful than guessing why it happened.

## Choose the right route

| Concern | Where to start |
| --- | --- |
| Comment abuse or spam | Open the comment's **More** menu and select **Report comment** |
| Conversation abuse or spam | Open the conversation actions and choose its report option |
| Playback, upload, billing, or account problem | [Contact BeatPass](https://open.beatpass.ca/contact) or email [support@beatpass.ca](mailto:support@beatpass.ca) |
| Copyright takedown notice | Follow [Copyright & DMCA](/help/legal/copyright-and-dmca) |
| AI origin, impersonation, fraud, or other rights concern | Follow [Rights & trust reports](/help/legal/non-dmca-rights-trust-report-policy) |

Reporting content does not guarantee removal. Include enough detail for a review and avoid sending the same report repeatedly.

## Describe a broken feature

<Steps>
  <Step title="Name the task">
    Start with a first line such as **Playback issue:** or **Billing problem:**. The contact form has no subject or category field.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Show where it happened">
    Include the exact BeatPass link, track, playlist, producer, request, or invoice involved. Say what you expected and what happened instead.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add useful context">
    Include the browser, device, error text, and whether it happens every time. If you already tried a fix, say what changed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send supporting evidence safely">
    Use email for screenshots or recordings, or put a suitable share link in the message. Hide unrelated personal data and payment details. Never include passwords, reset links, or recovery codes.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## If your account seems at risk

Use [password recovery](/help/account-settings/password/forgot-password) if needed, review [Active sessions](/help/account-settings/active-sessions), and remove access you do not recognize. Contact BeatPass with a short timeline. Do not wait for a reply before securing sign-in details you control.

## If a payment or license is involved

Keep the purchase date, amount, invoice or certificate ID, and related notice. For an accidental exclusive purchase, the [refund review](/help/billing/refund-policy) has a **24-hour** exception with strict unused-file and certificate conditions. A rights issue follows a separate review path.
