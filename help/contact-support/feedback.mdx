Tell BeatPass about a task that could feel easier, a feature you would use, or a part of the app you enjoy. A concrete example helps the team understand the idea.

## Send your idea

Use the [contact form](https://open.beatpass.ca/contact) or email [support@beatpass.ca](mailto:support@beatpass.ca). Start with **Feedback:** or **Feature request:** in the first line of the form's **Message** field. For email, use it in the subject.

Include:

- What you were trying to do.
- What felt confusing, slow, or missing.
- How the change would help your music or workflow.
- A relevant page link or example. Email is useful if you want to attach an image.

For example: "Feedback: finding a saved beat. I remembered the producer but not the title, and I wasn't sure which part of my library to open. A clearer route from the producer page would help me return to a session."

## Need something fixed now?

Use [Report an issue](/help/contact-support/reporting-issues) for a broken feature, account access problem, incorrect charge, abuse report, or copyright concern. Include the relevant error or transaction details.

BeatPass may reply for more information, but a suggestion does not guarantee an individual reply or a feature release. Send one clear message rather than repeating the same request.
