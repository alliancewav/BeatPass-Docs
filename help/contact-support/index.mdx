Get help with BeatPass through the [contact form](https://open.beatpass.ca/contact) or [contact@beatpass.ca](mailto:contact@beatpass.ca). Tell the team what you were trying to do and what happened; you do not need to diagnose it first.

## What to include

Start with your account email, relevant BeatPass link, and a short explanation. Add exact error text, your browser or device, and purchase or certificate references where relevant. Use email if you need to attach a screenshot; the public form has **Name**, **Email**, and **Message** fields.

Never send passwords, reset links, authentication codes, tokens, or full payment details. Crop screenshots to the part that explains the problem.

## Urgent account or rights questions

If you think someone accessed your account, follow [account recovery](/help/troubleshooting/account-access) and contact support. For copyright notices, use the [DMCA checklist](/help/legal/copyright-and-dmca). Other AI-origin, impersonation, licensing, or trust concerns use the [rights and trust report](/help/legal/non-dmca-rights-trust-report-policy).

Replies go to the email address you provide. Account, payment, and ownership reviews can take time; keep the confirmation and any reference numbers, and check your spam or junk folder.
