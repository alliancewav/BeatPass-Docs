Contact BeatPass when you need help with your account, a charge, a license, or a producer workflow. You do not need to know the technical cause to ask for help.

## Send a message

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Contact us">
    Open the [contact form](https://open.beatpass.ca/contact), or email [contact@beatpass.ca](mailto:contact@beatpass.ca) if you prefer a reply thread or need attachments.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Explain the problem">
    Fill in **Name**, **Email**, and **Message**. Use an address where you can receive a reply. Start the message with a label such as **Billing issue:** or **Upload problem:**; the form has no separate subject field.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send and check confirmation">
    Select **Send Message** and complete any verification shown. **Your message is with us.** confirms submission. Choose **Browse beats** to keep exploring or **Send Another Message** for a separate question.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If **Failed to send message. Please try again.** appears, retry or use email. Do not assume the request arrived until you see confirmation.

## Help the team find the right record

Include your account email, the page or beat link, what you expected, what happened, and the exact error text. For billing, include the date, amount, and invoice or purchase reference. For a license, include its certificate ID.

<Accordion title="What does a useful support message look like?">
  **I was trying to:** describe the task, such as opening a license certificate.

  **What happened:** quote the message on screen or explain what stopped.

  **Where:** include the relevant BeatPass page, purchase reference, or certificate ID.

  **My device:** include your browser and device when the problem concerns a page or control.
</Accordion>

The form accepts text. Use email for an image or recording, or provide a suitable share link. Remove unrelated personal information, full card or bank numbers, passwords, tokens, and recovery codes.

## While you wait

Replies go to the email address you entered. Complex reviews can take time. Refund and privacy requests have their own policy response expectations. Keep your references and check spam or junk for replies.

Try the [troubleshooting guides](/help/troubleshooting) for a common fix, or [reporting guide](/help/contact-support/reporting-issues) for comment, message, copyright, or trust concerns.
