Find your next BeatPass task from the navigation, then keep the music playing while you move around. Controls adapt to screen size and your account, so some producer or subscription actions appear only when relevant.

## Find music and return to it

Use **Search** to find beats, producers, collections, or playlists. Open a beat for its details and license choices, or a producer page for more of that sound.

Use **My library** in your account shortcuts or the library navigation to return to liked beats, playlists, saved collections, followed producers, and listening history. **My Licenses** opens **Your licenses**, where you can find earlier valid grants even after cancellation or producer approval.

## Open your account menu

Select your profile avatar. Depending on your account, the menu includes:

- Account settings for profile details, security, and regional preferences. Open [Account Settings](https://open.beatpass.ca/account-settings) directly if the configured menu label differs.
- **Billing** for subscriptions, payment methods, and invoices.
- **My library**, **Notifications**, and **Beat requests** shortcuts.
- **Profile page** for your public user profile.
- **Producer Dashboard** and **Artist profile** for approved producers.
- **Dark mode** or **Light mode**, and **Log out**.

## Use producer tools

Approved producers can open **Producer Dashboard** from the avatar menu or [open it directly](https://open.beatpass.ca/producer-dashboard). The producer workspace is also referred to as Backstage.

Use **Upload beats** for audio uploads and **Insights** for the available producer analytics shortcut. Dashboard navigation groups catalog, audience, and business tools; use **My Beats**, **My Collections**, **Activity**, **Progress**, **Leaderboards**, and **Finances** for the relevant task.

Start with the [producer quickstart](/help/getting-started/quick-start-guides/producer-guide) if your account was recently approved. Producer access is free; subscribing does not unlock new standard catalog licenses for a producer account.

## Control playback

The player shows the cued beat and offers play/pause, previous/next, seek, and available queue or volume controls. Expanded playback exposes more controls on small screens. A cued player does not mean audio is currently playing; select **Play** when ready.

Keyboard shortcuts work when you are not typing or using a focused control:

| Shortcut | Action |
| --- | --- |
| `Space` or `K` | Play or pause |
| `Ctrl` or `Shift` plus `ArrowRight` | Next beat |
| `Ctrl` or `Shift` plus `ArrowLeft` | Previous beat |

## On a phone

Use the compact top navigation for search and account actions. In the bottom navigation, **Home** returns to the main page, **Find** opens search, and **Library** opens your saved music and licenses. Tap the player to expand it and return to the page when finished. If an action is missing, check your avatar menu or the page's available actions before assuming your account lost access.
