Create a free BeatPass account to keep favorites, build playlists, and follow producers. Use an email you can access so you can verify the account and recover it later.

## Create and verify your account

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open registration">
    Open [Create an account](https://open.beatpass.ca/register).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your sign-in details">
    Fill in **Email address**, **Password**, and **Confirm password**. Use at least 8 characters for the password and enter it the same way in both fields.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review your agreement">
    Review the full French documents supplied under **Review your agreement**, then choose **Français** or **English** under **Agreement language**. Choosing **English** also shows the English version.

    The [Terms of Service](https://open.beatpass.ca/pages/terms-of-service) and [Privacy Policy](https://open.beatpass.ca/pages/privacy-policy) are included in the review.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create your account">
    Select **I accept the supplied agreement and related documents.**, then choose **Create account**. Changing your agreement language clears the checkbox so you can confirm your new choice.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Keep your agreement copy">
    In **Your agreement copy**, choose **Download your agreement** and save the file, then choose **Continue**. You can download it here even if the email copy could not be sent.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify your email if asked">
    If **Verify your email** appears, open the BeatPass verification email and follow its link within the time shown. Return to BeatPass and choose **I've verified my email** if the screen has not continued.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If the email is missing or its link expired, check spam or junk, then choose **Resend email** on **Verify your email**. **Verification email sent. Check your inbox and spam folder.** confirms the request. Wait if the resend control asks you to pause.

Check the email address shown on screen. **Use another account** returns you to sign-in if you chose the wrong one; you do not need to create a second account for a delivery problem.

## Create an account with Google

Choose **Continue with google** and use your intended Google account. If **Finish creating your account** appears, check the email shown, review the supplied agreement, choose its language, and select the agreement checkbox before **Create account**. Download your copy and follow any email-verification prompt.

Your agreement language is separate from the [language you use to browse BeatPass](/help/account-settings/localization).

## If signup gets stuck

- If the documents do not load, choose **Try again** and wait for them before creating your account.
- If BeatPass asks you to **Review latest documents**, load them, choose your agreement language again, and confirm the checkbox before retrying.
- If your copy email does not arrive, use **Download your agreement** while the copy is open. For further help, follow [account access recovery](/help/troubleshooting/account-access).

## Start with a free task

Listen to a beat, like one you want to revisit, and add it to a playlist. You can follow a producer from their profile. For Beat Requests, check your displayed token balance and allowance before posting; plans can change.

Listening and saving do not grant release rights. Eligible customer accounts need a plan with downloads for a new standard license. Producer access is free and has different download rules. See [account roles](/help/getting-started/understanding-roles) before subscribing.

Already joined? [Sign in](https://open.beatpass.ca/login), or use [account recovery](/help/troubleshooting/account-access) if you cannot get in.
