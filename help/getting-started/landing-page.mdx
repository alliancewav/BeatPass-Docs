Homepage sections and featured content can rotate. The exact promotion you see is less important than the action you want to complete.

## After signing in

Your library, profile menu, notifications, and account features become available according to your access. Use [Navigation](/help/getting-started/navigating-beatpass) to find your way around. A public preview or saved favorite is not a license to release the beat.
