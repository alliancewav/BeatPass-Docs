## Platform Comparison

| Traditional beat-store pattern | BeatPass approach |
| --- | --- |
| Per-beat nonexclusive checkout every time | Subscription-based access for standard licensed downloads |
| Seller-by-seller license language | Standard BeatPass subscription licensing |
| Limited post-download verification | Library-based certificates and verification flow |
| Producer identity split across platforms | Public producer profiles with credits, gallery, and stats |

## Why proof matters in a beat subscription

A beat subscription is not only about how many beats you can download. The important questions begin after the download:

- Can you prove what you licensed?
- Can a distributor, platform, label, or collaborator verify the certificate?
- Do eligible licenses remain valid after cancellation?
- Are Content ID limits, producer ownership, and payout calculations clear?

BeatPass answers those questions with license certificates, public verification, BeatPassID fingerprint details, producer ownership terms, and a published contribution formula.
