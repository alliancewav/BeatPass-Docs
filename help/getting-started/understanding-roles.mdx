You can listen for free, subscribe as a customer, or apply to upload and sell your own beats. Producer access does not require a paid plan.

## Choose the access you need

| You want to... | What you need |
| --- | --- |
| Listen, save favorites, and create playlists | A free account |
| Get a new standard license for an eligible beat | A paid plan with download access, on a customer account without a producer profile |
| Upload and manage your own beats | Approved producer access; no paid plan required |
| Download your own original beat files | Your producer account |
| Retrieve a beat you already licensed | A valid existing license, subject to file availability |
| Buy an exclusive license | An eligible customer account without a producer profile, plus the track's separate offer and purchase terms |

## Free accounts

Create an account to save favorites, follow producers, make playlists, and use the community features available to you. Check your account's beat-request balance before creating a request; allowances depend on the current plan.

Start with [Listen & discover](/help/getting-started/quick-start-guides/listener-guide).

## Paid customer plans

A plan that includes downloads lets eligible customer accounts get standard licenses for available beats. Each license covers its stated project and usage limits. It does not replace a separate exclusive purchase.

Check the [current pricing page](https://open.beatpass.ca/pricing) for prices, allowances, and benefits. Review the total and renewal terms before you pay.

Start with [Download & release music](/help/getting-started/quick-start-guides/subscriber-guide).

## Approved producers

Producer access gives you a public producer profile and **Backstage** tools for your catalog, activity, licensing, and finances. Apply through the [Producer Program](/help/producer-program). Check your application for its review status.

Producer accounts cannot get new standard catalog licenses or make new exclusive catalog purchases for other producers' beats, even with a paid subscription. You can download your own source files and retrieve other beats covered by valid licenses you acquired earlier. A subscription is not a way around this restriction.

Open your **profile avatar**, then **Producer Dashboard** to manage your producer workspace, also called Backstage. Start with [Produce & get paid](/help/getting-started/quick-start-guides/producer-guide).

## Existing licenses stay separate from your current role

A later subscription cancellation, producer approval, or exclusive sale does not automatically cancel an earlier valid license. Follow the terms of that issued license. Retrieving the same beat does not grant another project.

Open [Library → My Licenses](https://open.beatpass.ca/library/licenses) to check your records. See [how downloads work](/help/downloads-and-licensing/how-downloads-work) if an action is unavailable.
