Use a BeatPass reset email when you have forgotten your password or joined with Google and need to create one. Keep access to the email attached to your account.

## Reset or create your password

<Steps>
  <Step title="Request the email">
    On [Sign in](https://open.beatpass.ca/login), select **Forgot password?**. Enter **Email address** and select **Send reset link**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Use a fresh link">
    Check your inbox and spam or junk folder. Open the link within **60 minutes**; request another if it expired or was already used.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the new password">
    On **Create new password**, enter **Email address**, **New password**, and **Confirm password**. Use at least 8 characters, then select **Reset password**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

**Your password has been reset!** confirms the change and returns you to sign-in. Use the new password. If connected Google sign-in still works, you can use it while resolving access to your email.

If the link is rejected, choose **Send a new reset link** when offered, or return to [password recovery](https://open.beatpass.ca/forgot-password).

Never share a reset link, password, or recovery code. If you cannot access the mailbox or still cannot sign in, use the [account-access guide](/help/troubleshooting/account-access) or [contact support](/help/contact-support).
