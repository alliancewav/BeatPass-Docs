Start with what you want to do: listen for free, license a beat for a song, or publish your own music as an approved producer. You can take one step at a time.

## Start Here

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your account">
    [Sign up and verify your email](/help/getting-started/creating-account). Already have an account? [Sign in](/help/getting-started/logging-in) or [reset your password](/help/getting-started/password-reset).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find your way around">
    Use [navigation help](/help/getting-started/navigating-beatpass) to recognize the player, your library, and the profile menu. On your phone, look for **Home**, **Find**, and **Library**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose your first task">
    Pick one of the paths below. You do not need to learn every part of BeatPass before starting.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Choose Your Path

<Columns cols={3}>
  <Card title="Listen & discover" icon="headphones" href="/help/getting-started/quick-start-guides/listener-guide">
    Find beats, save favorites, and make playlists with a free account.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Download & release" icon="download" href="/help/getting-started/quick-start-guides/subscriber-guide">
    Check customer eligibility, download a beat, and keep its certificate.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Produce & get paid" icon="music" href="/help/getting-started/quick-start-guides/producer-guide">
    Apply for free producer access, upload your work, and prepare payouts.
  </Card>
</Columns>

Listening and saving do not grant release rights. A paid plan does not let producer accounts acquire new standard catalog licenses. [Account roles](/help/getting-started/understanding-roles) explains which path fits your account.

## Need Help Accessing Your Account?

If sign-in fails, follow [account access](/help/troubleshooting/account-access). If the screen looks different from a guide, check the account you are signed in to and scroll the page's navigation sideways on a narrow screen.

[All quick starts](/help/getting-started/quick-start-guides) keep the three paths together.
