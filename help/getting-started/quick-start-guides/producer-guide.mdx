Publish your first BeatPass beat, complete the payout setup offered to you, and make your producer profile ready for visitors. Producer access is free; this guide is for approved producers.

<ProducerOnly />


<DashboardFigure />

## Open your workspace

Select your profile avatar and **Producer Dashboard**, or [open the dashboard](https://open.beatpass.ca/producer-dashboard). Use **My Beats** and **My Collections** for your catalog, **Activity** for performance, and **Finances** for payout setup and earnings.

## Set up payouts

Start with the [BEATPAY guide](/help/earnings/beatpay) if you want each screen explained as you go.

Open **Finances** and follow **Payout setup**. When offered, select **Set up BEATPAY** and choose **Set up bank payouts**. Select a currency your bank account can receive, then enter the fields requested for that bank route and select **Save bank details**.

A submitted destination does not mean every earning type is already available for payout. Read the status in Finances. Existing eligible Stripe routes can remain active until the relevant BEATPAY route is ready.

## Upload your first beat

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose your WAV">
    From the dashboard, select **Upload beat** and choose the original WAV audio. To group files as a collection, select **Create Collection** before choosing them.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete the musical details">
    Add **Track Name**, cover art, a genre, **Key**, **Scale**, and a whole-number **BPM** from 60 to 200. Your producer profile is filled in as the credited producer.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete protection and licensing">
    In **Protection & Verification**, select the sample declaration and **Human-made** or **AI-assisted**. Add required AI-use details and commercial-rights confirmation, then wait for the audio check. Under **Licensing Options**, choose an available model and complete its terms.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish and check">
    Resolve any incomplete section, then select **Save Track**. Wait for completion before leaving or retrying. Check the saved beat in **My Beats**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

For an exclusive offer, check the contact details, price when available, WAV and stems, delivery guarantee, and your signed authorization. Standard catalog availability also depends on credited producers' payout readiness. See [uploading](/help/uploading) for the full workflow.

## Make the profile yours

Open your producer profile's edit action. In the profile editor, use **Details**, **Images**, **Biography**, **Links**, and **Credits** to update the relevant section. Select **Save changes** to publish, then **Preview** to check the public result.

## Keep growing

Use **Activity** for performance, **Progress** for achievements, and **Finances** for recorded earnings and payout status. Figures and milestones can update after processing; a visible balance is not the same as a completed bank payout.

Producer accounts can retrieve their own audio and valid earlier licenses. A paid plan does not unlock new standard licenses or exclusive catalog purchases for other producers' beats.
