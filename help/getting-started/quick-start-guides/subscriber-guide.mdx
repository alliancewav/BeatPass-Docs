Find a beat for your song, review its license, and keep your proof together before release.

## Before you subscribe

Check the [current plans](https://open.beatpass.ca/pricing) and confirm that your chosen plan includes downloads. Prices, request allowances, and benefits are shown there and at checkout.

Producer accounts cannot acquire new standard catalog licenses, even with a paid subscription. Producer access stays free; this guide is for eligible customer accounts. Existing valid licenses remain retrievable under their terms.

## Get your first licensed beat

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find an eligible beat">
    Browse [BeatPass](https://open.beatpass.ca), open a track, and read its standard license terms. New standard licenses are unavailable after an exclusive sale or when the beat is not ready for standard licensing.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Request the download">
    Choose the download action, then **Download audio**. Follow any name-confirmation or certificate instructions shown. **Download requested** means the browser has been asked to save the file; check your browser's downloads for completion.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check your license">
    Open [Library → My Licenses](https://open.beatpass.ca/library/licenses). On **Your licenses**, find the beat and resolve any action shown before relying on the certificate.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Prepare your release">
    Keep the certificate, credit the producer, and follow the limits for your one licensed project. The standard license does not allow Content ID registration of the beat.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What happens next?

A valid issued license can continue after subscription cancellation or a later exclusive sale, subject to its terms. Retrieving the same licensed beat reuses that grant; it does not license a second song.

If a file does not arrive or a certificate needs attention, use [download recovery](/help/troubleshooting/download-issues) and the [certificate guide](/help/downloads-and-licensing/license-certificates).

## Keep your account organized

Open [Billing](https://open.beatpass.ca/billing) to check your plan, payment history, and renewal. Check your displayed token balance before [creating a beat request](/help/beat-requests/creating-requests).
