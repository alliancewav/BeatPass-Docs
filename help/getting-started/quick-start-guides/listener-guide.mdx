A free account is enough to find your sound, save favorites, and make playlists.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your account">
    [Sign up and verify your email](/help/getting-started/creating-account), then open [BeatPass](https://open.beatpass.ca).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find a beat">
    Browse the home page or a channel, or use **Search**. Open a producer page when you find a sound you want more of.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Keep your favorites close">
    Play a beat, like it, and add it to a playlist. Choose a private playlist for your own listening or a public one to share.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Follow the producer">
    Open their profile and choose **Follow**. Return to your library when you want to pick up where you left off.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Ready for something more?

- To request a particular sound, open [Beat Requests](/help/beat-requests) and check your available tokens before posting.
- To use a beat in a release, read [Download & release music](/help/getting-started/quick-start-guides/subscriber-guide). Listening or saving a beat does not grant a license.
- To publish your own beats, apply to the [Producer Program](/help/producer-program). Producer access is free and has different download rules from customer subscriptions.

## If something gets in the way

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="The beat will not play">
    Work through [playback recovery](/help/troubleshooting/playback-issues), then return to the same beat.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I cannot get into my account">
    Start with [account access](/help/troubleshooting/account-access). Keep using your existing account so your saved music stays together.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
