See what your music has earned and what needs to happen before it reaches your bank. Start in [Finances](https://open.beatpass.ca/producer-dashboard/finances) for your own recorded amounts, payout readiness, and individual activity.

Producer access is free. A paid customer plan does not let producer accounts acquire new standard or exclusive catalog licenses.

## Revenue Streams

<Columns cols={2}>
  <Card title="Exclusive sales" icon="bag-shopping" href="/help/licensing-for-producers/when-beat-sells">
    An eligible exclusive purchase creates an earning that follows its recorded payout route.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Subscription earnings" icon="repeat" href="/help/earnings/contribution-system">
    A contribution value based on recency, play activity, and collaborators helps determine your subscription payout share.
  </Card>
</Columns>

If a producer is excluded from a subscription split because payout setup is incomplete, eligible producers can receive redistributed value. The [contribution guides](/help/earnings/contribution-system) explain the calculation separately from bank setup.

## How It Works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check payout readiness">
    Open **Producer Dashboard** → **Finances** → **Payout setup**. Follow **Set up BEATPAY** or **Upgrade to BEATPAY** when offered. Readiness can differ for subscription earnings and exclusive sales.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Keep your catalog ready">
    Upload your music and complete the licensing choices for each beat. Saving a catalog choice does not by itself make every customer licensing route available.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Follow an earning">
    Choose an earnings currency when more than one is available. In **Finance activity**, find the record and read its amount, provider, and current status.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<FinancesFigure />

**Pending** is waiting, **Processing** is moving through the provider, and **Delayed** needs a closer look. **Sent** means the provider sent the money; arrival at your bank can happen later. **Reversed** identifies a reversed customer payment. See [payout timing](/help/earnings/payout-schedule) for the full explanation.

## Key Facts

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How are exclusive-sale fees shown?">
    The platform fee is added at checkout when it applies, rather than deducted from your listed producer price. Only a waiver confirmed for the buyer's eligible active plan applies. Check the transaction's currency and final total.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Which currency should I look at?">
    Finances keeps currencies separate. Use **Earnings currency** when more than one is available. Track purchase pricing supports **USD**, **EUR**, **GBP**, and **CAD**; a bank account currency is a separate payout choice.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Where do I check a customer subscription price?">
    Use the [current pricing page](https://open.beatpass.ca/pricing). Plan prices and billing currencies are separate from your producer payout destination.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
