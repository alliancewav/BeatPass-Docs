**BEATPAY** is BeatPass's bank payout setup for eligible producers. It is powered by Wise and appears inside **Producer Finances** when it is available for an artist.

<ProducerOnly />

## What BEATPAY Does

<Columns cols={2}>
  <Card title="Bank destination" icon="money-bill-transfer">
    Save a bank destination for eligible BeatPass earnings.
  </Card>
  <Card title="One current route" icon="route">
    Once BEATPAY is active, new eligible earnings use the saved bank destination.

## Where BEATPAY Appears

Open **Backstage** → **Finances**, then review **Payout setup**.

You may see:

| Label | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Set up BEATPAY** | No BEATPAY bank destination is active yet |
| **Upgrade to BEATPAY** | An active legacy Stripe account remains the current route and BEATPAY is available |
| **BEATPAY setup is under review** | Submitted details are being reviewed |
| **BEATPAY is ready** | Eligible earnings use the saved destination |
| **Use a different bank account** | Replace the current BEATPAY destination |

New and resumed Stripe setup are not offered from **Finances**.

## Set Up BEATPAY

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Producer Finances">
    Click your **profile avatar**, open **Backstage**, then select **Finances**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start BEATPAY">
    In **Payout setup**, select **Set up BEATPAY**. If legacy Stripe is active, select **Upgrade to BEATPAY**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose bank account currency">
    Select **Bank account currency**. Choose a currency that the receiving bank account can accept.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter bank details">
    Select **Continue to bank details**, then enter the requested information for the bank account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the destination">
    Select **Save bank details**. When setup is complete, select **Done**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  BEATPAY affects how eligible earnings reach you. It does not change your earning share or the amount recorded for a track sale or subscription earning.
</Note>

## If You Currently Use Legacy Stripe

You do not need to remove Stripe before setting up BEATPAY.

- Legacy Stripe remains the current route until BEATPAY is active.
- If BEATPAY details are under review, **Payout setup** explains that Stripe remains current.
- After BEATPAY is active, new eligible earnings use BEATPAY.
- Stripe can remain visible while previously routed payouts and its remaining balance finish.
- Stripe information on **Finances** is read-only.

Retired or incomplete Stripe records do not create a setup action. Historical finance activity can still identify **Legacy Stripe** as the provider for an older earning.

## Choose a Bank Account Currency

BEATPAY lists bank account currencies available for the Wise-powered payout destination. The list may not include every currency used in your country.

Your bank account currency can differ from your local currency, but the receiving account must accept it.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check with your bank">
    Ask which listed currencies the account can receive.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a compatible currency">
    Select only a currency your bank confirms it can accept. The receiving bank may convert the payment or charge a fee.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the matching route">
    For USD, choose **ACH** when you have US bank details. Choose **SWIFT** when a bank outside the US accepts USD and provides SWIFT details.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Info>
  **Jamaica example:** If JMD is not available and your Jamaican bank confirms that the account can receive USD, select **USD** and enter the bank's **SWIFT** details.
</Info>

## Currency Context

The separate **Currency context** section in **Finances** uses the BeatPass payout source currency and your saved bank currency. Before a destination is saved, it uses a comparison currency you can change.

Select **7D**, **30D**, or **90D** to review the recent rate, low, and high. Point to the graph or use the Left and Right Arrow keys to inspect a date.

<Warning>
  Historical exchange-rate context is not a payout quote. The rate used for a payout can differ from the graph.
</Warning>

## How Bank Details Are Handled

BEATPAY sends the bank details entered during setup to Wise, the payout provider. BeatPass does not keep the full bank account number, routing details, IBAN, full payout address, date of birth, or tax or national identifier submitted for setup.

BeatPass keeps the account-holder name, bank account currency and country, setup and verification status, and masked details that help you recognize the destination. Wise keeps the complete recipient details under its own privacy and legal obligations.

See the [official Privacy Policy](https://open.beatpass.ca/pages/privacy-policy) for more information.

## Common Questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Why do I not see BEATPAY?">
    BEATPAY appears when it is available for the selected artist. **Payout setup** shows the current option or state.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do I need to remove Stripe first?">
    No. Select **Upgrade to BEATPAY** when it appears. Stripe remains the current route until BEATPAY becomes active.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does BEATPAY change my earnings share?">
    No. BEATPAY changes the payout destination for eligible earnings, not how BeatPass calculates the earning.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why is my country's currency not listed?">
    The list contains bank account currencies currently available for setup. Ask your bank whether the account can receive another listed currency.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Where do I check a payout problem?">
    Open **Backstage** → **Finances**. In **Finance activity**, select **Needs attention**. Also review **Payout setup** for an action.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
