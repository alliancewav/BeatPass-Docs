Use [Producer Finances](https://open.beatpass.ca/producer-dashboard/finances) to follow your producer earnings and manage the payout destination available to your account.

## Open the Page

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Producer Dashboard">
    Click your **profile avatar**, then select **Producer Dashboard**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Finances">
    Select **Finances** in the producer navigation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review your producer workspace">
    The page uses your own producer profile. Start with **Earnings**, where **Payout setup** appears below the amounts.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Review the Page

<FinancesFigure />

<Steps>
  <Step title="Review Earnings">
    Compare **Total earned**, **Pending**, **Processing**, **Delayed**, and **Sent** in one currency at a time.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review Payout setup">
    Inside **Earnings**, use **Set up BEATPAY**, **Upgrade to BEATPAY**, or **Use a different bank account** when offered. Check readiness for the earning type; bank setup alone does not enable every payout stream.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check Finance activity">
    Start with **Pending** and **Delayed**. Use **Status** and **Earning type** when you need a specific record. Each page shows 20 records.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Use Currency context">
    Expand **Currency context** below Finance activity, then choose a comparison currency and a 7-, 30-, or 90-day range when available.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<PayoutBankArrival />

Legacy Stripe information is read-only. New and resumed Stripe setup are not offered from **Finances**.

For complete metric and state definitions, see [Finances](/help/producer-dashboard/finances).
