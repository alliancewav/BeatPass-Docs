Use **Producer Finances** to follow money earned by one artist and manage the payout destination for eligible earnings.

## Open the Page

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Backstage">
    Click your **profile avatar**, then select **Backstage**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Finances">
    Select **Finances** in the producer navigation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose an artist">
    If **Artist finances** appears, choose the profile you want to review.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Review the Page

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check Finance activity">
    Start with **Waiting** and **Needs attention**. Use **Status** and **Earning type** when you need a specific record. Each page shows 20 records.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review Earnings">
    Compare **Total earned**, **Waiting**, **Sending**, and **Sent** in one currency at a time.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Use Currency context">
    Choose a comparison currency and a 7-, 30-, or 90-day range when you want historical exchange-rate context.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review Payout setup">
    Use **Set up BEATPAY**, **Upgrade to BEATPAY**, or **Use a different bank account** only when that action appears.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  **Sent** and **Completed** do not confirm bank arrival. The payout provider and receiving bank may continue processing the money.
</Note>

Legacy Stripe information is read-only. New and resumed Stripe setup are not offered from **Finances**.

For complete metric and state definitions, see [Finances](/help/producer-dashboard/finances).
