BeatPass supports multiple currencies for producer pricing and payout tracking. The most important rule is simple: your listed track price is your price. The platform fee is added on top when it applies, and an eligible active buyer plan can waive it without changing your amount.

<PlatformFee />

## Supported Pricing Currencies

BeatPass currently supports these currencies for track purchase pricing:

| Currency | Code |
|----------|------|
| US Dollar | USD |
| Euro | EUR |
| British Pound | GBP |
| Canadian Dollar | CAD |

<Note>
  Subscription prices and billing currency are shown on the [current pricing page](https://open.beatpass.ca/pricing) and confirmed before payment.
</Note>

## For Producers

### Setting Track Prices

- Choose the pricing currency that fits your audience and catalog strategy
- Buyers see your listed price plus the platform fee, or a waived fee when their active plan includes that benefit
- BeatPass does not deduct the platform fee from your listed price

### What You See in Finances

**Producer Finances** can show:

- An **Earnings currency** selector when earnings exist in more than one currency
- Currency-specific **Total earned**, **Pending**, **Processing**, **Delayed**, and **Sent** amounts
- Each finance activity record in its original currency

## For Buyers

If the purchase currency does not match the card or bank's currency, conversion can happen outside BeatPass. The exact outcome depends on Stripe and the buyer's bank.

## BEATPAY Bank Account Currencies

<PayoutSetupFigure />

BEATPAY's **Bank account currency** list is intentionally narrower than the world's currency list. It includes currencies currently available for adding a bank recipient through BeatPass's Wise-powered payout account.

The bank account currency does not have to match your country's local currency. It must match a currency that the receiving account accepts.

- If the account is in the US and uses US bank details, select **USD** and choose **ACH**
- If a bank outside the US accepts USD and provides SWIFT details, select **USD** and choose **SWIFT**
- If the local currency is missing, ask the bank whether the account can receive USD, EUR, GBP, or another currency shown in BEATPAY

<Info>
  **Jamaica example:** If JMD is missing from the setup list and a Jamaican bank confirms that the account accepts USD, the producer can select **USD** and use that bank's **SWIFT** details.
</Info>

<Warning>
  Choosing an unsupported currency can cause a payment to fail or be returned. The receiving bank may also convert the payment or charge receiving or conversion fees. Confirm compatibility and possible fees with the bank before saving the account.
</Warning>

## Practical Tips

- Use a pricing currency your buyers already understand
- Use the **Earnings currency** selector in **Producer Finances** to review one currency at a time
- Expect the active payment or payout provider and your bank to control most conversion details after checkout
- If a local currency is not listed in BEATPAY, confirm an alternative account currency with your bank before selecting it
- Review the payout setup shown in **Finances** if you need a different payout arrangement

## Common Questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can I price a track in more than one currency at the same time?">
    Each priced track uses one configured pricing currency at a time.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can my earnings history contain more than one currency?">
    Yes. **Producer Finances** keeps currencies separate and shows an **Earnings currency** selector when more than one is available.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Who controls currency conversion after checkout?">
    The payment or payout provider and the receiving bank determine applicable conversion rates and fees. Review the checkout total or payout details, then check with your bank if a charge differs.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What if my country's local currency is not available in BEATPAY?">
    Ask your bank whether your account can receive another currency shown during setup, such as USD, EUR, or GBP. Select it only after the bank confirms compatibility. For example, a Jamaican producer whose bank account accepts USD can choose **USD** and use the bank's **SWIFT** details.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Are subscriptions shown in the same currency as track pricing?">
    Not necessarily. Check the plan's displayed billing currency. Exclusive track pricing supports USD, EUR, GBP, and CAD.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
