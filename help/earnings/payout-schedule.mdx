BeatPass records a real earning, then follows the payout route shown in **Finances**. A track sale and a subscription earning can use different providers when they were created at different stages of a payout upgrade.

## Finance Activity Statuses

| Status | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Pending** | The earning is recorded but has not entered payout processing |
| **Processing** | The payout provider is processing the money |
| **Delayed** | An earning exists but its payout has an issue; open the earning for the reason and next step |
| **Sent** | The payout provider has sent the payment. Legacy Stripe entries mean sent to the Stripe balance, with bank payout following the Stripe schedule |
| **Reversed** | The customer payment was reversed; this amount is no longer included in total earned |

Failed payment attempts that never became earnings do not appear in **Finance activity**.

## Earnings Amounts

The **Earnings** section groups non-refunded earnings by currency:

| Amount | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Total earned** | Successful, non-refunded track sales and eligible subscription earnings, including money still moving |
| **Pending** | Earned money waiting to enter payout processing |
| **Processing** | Money moving through the payout provider |
| **Delayed** | Earned money with a payout issue; open the earning for details |
| **Sent** | Money sent by the payout provider |

## Legacy Stripe and BEATPAY

If legacy Stripe is active, it remains the current route until BEATPAY becomes active. After the upgrade:

- New eligible earnings use BEATPAY.
- Previously routed Stripe payouts continue through Stripe.
- A read-only Stripe balance may remain visible while that money finishes moving.

This is why older and newer rows can show different providers.

## What Can Change the Timeline

Arrival time can change because of:

- Payout setup that needs attention
- A provider or bank account review
- A refund or dispute hold
- Provider processing time
- Bank processing time
- Currency conversion or receiving-bank checks

<Warning>
  BeatPass does not promise a fixed arrival time because the payout provider and receiving bank control part of the process.
</Warning>

## If Money Looks Stuck

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Finance activity">
    Go to **Backstage** → **Finances**. **Finance activity** is the first section.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the status">
    Select **Pending**, **Processing**, or **Delayed**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the earning type">
    Use **Earning type** to narrow the list to **Track sales** or **Subscription earnings**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review Payout setup">
    Follow an action only when **Payout setup** asks for one.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Allow for bank processing">
    If the row or earnings amount is **Sent**, review its provider explanation. BEATPAY bank arrival can happen later; Legacy Stripe bank payouts follow the Stripe schedule. Contact support with the earning reference if you need help.
  </Step>
</Steps>
