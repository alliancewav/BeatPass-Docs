To receive money from BeatPass, you need more than just earnings. Your producer profile and the payout setup available to your account need to be ready.

## Earnings Streams and Payout Routes

Exclusive sales and subscription earnings are separate streams. The same bank destination can serve both, but each stream must be available and ready for your account. Check **Payout setup** in [Finances](https://open.beatpass.ca/producer-dashboard/finances) before relying on a sale or subscription payout. An active bank destination alone does not promise that both streams are enabled.

When BEATPAY is active for an eligible stream, new earnings use that route. Previously routed Stripe earnings keep their original provider. A legacy Stripe account can remain visible while its balance and outstanding payouts finish; account retirement depends on those checks.

New customer **Standard Licensing** requires every credited producer to have an active subscription payout route. Exclusive checkout requires the primary producer's exclusive-sale route and offer checks to be ready. You can still save catalog settings before payout setup is complete; follow the notices in the beat editor.

## Readiness Signals

An existing Stripe route depends on the provider confirming these capabilities:

| Signal | What It Means |
|--------|----------------|
| **Connected** | BeatPass is linked to your Stripe Connect account |
| **Charges Enabled** | Stripe has enabled incoming payment capability for the account |
| **Payouts Enabled** | Stripe has enabled payouts from the account |

If [BEATPAY](/help/earnings/beatpay) is available, **Finances** shows the bank payout setup state instead. The Finances card can show **BEATPAY is ready** or **BEATPAY setup is under review**. Follow the explanation and the action on that card.

<Info>
  Follow the state shown in **Finances**. The provider may ask for more details during a later account review.
</Info>

## What Happens If You Are Not Eligible

If a subscription payout runs while your account is not ready:

1. The excluded split does not appear as an earning in **Finance activity**
2. Your share can be redistributed among eligible producers
3. Future payouts can work normally once your setup is complete

<Warning>
  Excluded subscription payouts are not automatically recreated as back pay later. It is important to finish payout setup before you rely on subscription earnings.
</Warning>

## How to Check Your Status

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Producer Finances">
    Go to **Producer Dashboard** → **Finances**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the dashboard state">
    Read the **Payout setup** state and follow an action only when one appears.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review warnings and statuses">
    Look for signals such as **Payout setup needs attention**, **Delayed**, or any prompt asking for more information.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Complete setup and check the result

1. Open **Finances**
2. Select **Set up BEATPAY** or **Upgrade to BEATPAY** when offered
3. Choose a bank account currency that your bank can receive and provide the requested bank details
4. Return to BeatPass and finish any remaining prompts if the payout provider still needs more information
5. Keep payout setup active so future splits can process normally

## Common Questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Do I need Stripe Connect for both track sales and subscription payouts?">
    Follow the route shown in **Finances** for each earning type. BEATPAY is offered to eligible accounts; an existing eligible Stripe route can remain in use. New or resumed Stripe onboarding is not offered.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I still earn before setup is finished?">
    You can upload and organize your catalog before setup is finished. New exclusive sales need an active exclusive-sale payout route; new standard customer licenses need an active subscription payout route for every credited producer.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Why does a subscription earning need attention?">
    Open **Finance activity** and review the affected subscription earning. **Delayed** means an earning exists but its payout has an issue. Read the earning for the reason and next step. A split excluded before it became an earning does not appear in this list.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I fix eligibility later?">
    Yes for future payouts. Complete your payout setup and keep it active so later splits can process normally.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
