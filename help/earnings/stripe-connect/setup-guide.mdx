---

<Warning>
  New Stripe Connect accounts can no longer be created. All producers now set up **BEATPAY** bank payouts instead.
</Warning>

To receive your earnings, open **Finances**, click **Set up BEATPAY**, choose a bank account currency your bank can receive, and add your bank details once. See [BEATPAY](/help/earnings/beatpay) for the full walkthrough.

If you connected a Stripe account in the past, it keeps working until your BEATPAY setup is active — see [Stripe Connect (Legacy)](/help/earnings/stripe-connect) for what happens next.
