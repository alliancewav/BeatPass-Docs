Looking for payout setup? Start in [Finances](https://open.beatpass.ca/producer-dashboard/finances) and use the bank payout option shown for your account.

<Warning>
  Finances does not offer new or resumed Stripe onboarding. Use the BEATPAY option shown in **Finances** when available to your account.
</Warning>

To receive your earnings, open **Finances**, click **Set up BEATPAY**, choose a bank account currency your bank can receive, and add your bank details once. See [BEATPAY](/help/earnings/beatpay) for the full walkthrough.

If you connected Stripe in the past, its eligibility and any remaining payouts are shown in **Finances**. See [Stripe Connect (Legacy)](/help/earnings/stripe-connect) for the transition.
