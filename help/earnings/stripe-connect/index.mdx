Stripe Connect is a **legacy payout route**. Finances does not offer new or resumed Stripe onboarding. Follow **Payout setup** in [Finances](https://open.beatpass.ca/producer-dashboard/finances) for the BEATPAY option available to your account. This page explains existing Stripe earnings and accounts.

<Info>
  Looking to set up payouts? Open **Finances** and click **Set up BEATPAY**. See [BEATPAY](/help/earnings/beatpay) for the full guide.
</Info>

## How Legacy Stripe Information Is Handled

Stripe continues to hold the identity and bank details already associated with an active legacy account. BeatPass keeps the Stripe account reference and operational information needed to show payout-readiness and remaining balance context; BeatPass does not store the full bank account details entered in Stripe.

Legacy Stripe information in **Payout setup** is read-only. BeatPass does not offer new or resumed Stripe onboarding. Use **Upgrade to BEATPAY** when it appears, or contact BeatPass support if an existing Stripe payout needs review. See the [official Privacy Policy](https://open.beatpass.ca/pages/privacy-policy) for the legally binding data-handling terms.

## If You Still Use Stripe Connect

- An existing eligible Stripe account can remain the route for an earning type until BEATPAY is available and ready for it.
- Subscription earnings and exclusive sales can have different readiness. Check **Finances** rather than assuming that one destination enables both streams.
- You can still sign in to your Stripe Express dashboard: see [Accessing your Stripe dashboard](/help/earnings/stripe-connect/accessing-stripe-dashboard).

## What Happens When You Move to BEATPAY

<Steps>
  <Step title="Set up BEATPAY once">
    Open **Finances** and select **Upgrade to BEATPAY** when offered. Choose a bank account currency your bank can receive and complete the requested details.
  </Step>
  <Step title="New earnings switch to your bank">
    New eligible earnings use BEATPAY once that route is active for the earning type. Previously routed Stripe payouts keep their original provider.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Your Stripe account winds down on its own">
    The existing Stripe account can remain visible while its balance and outstanding payouts finish. BeatPass retires it only after the required checks are complete; contact support if an older payout needs attention.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Your past Stripe transactions stay visible in **Finances** after the move. Your earnings share does not change: only where the money is sent.
</Note>
