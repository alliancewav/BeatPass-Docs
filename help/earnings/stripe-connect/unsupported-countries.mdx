<Warning>
  This page described workarounds for countries Stripe Connect could not support. Stripe onboarding is now closed, and those workarounds are no longer needed.
</Warning>

**BEATPAY** pays producers by direct bank transfer and supports a much wider range of countries and currencies than Stripe Connect did. Open **Finances**, click **Set up BEATPAY**, and choose a bank account currency your receiving account can accept.

If your local currency is not listed, ask your bank whether the account can receive another listed currency, such as USD, EUR, or GBP. Select an alternative only when your bank confirms compatibility; the bank may convert the payment or charge fees. See [BEATPAY](/help/earnings/beatpay) for route guidance.
