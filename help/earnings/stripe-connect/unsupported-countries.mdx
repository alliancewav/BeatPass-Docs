Looking for payout setup? Start in [Finances](https://open.beatpass.ca/producer-dashboard/finances) and use the bank payout option shown for your account.

<Warning>
  Stripe onboarding is no longer offered in Finances. Check the payout setup currently offered by BeatPass rather than using older Stripe setup workarounds.
</Warning>

Open **Finances** and follow **Set up BEATPAY** when offered. Available currencies, destinations, and bank routes depend on your setup. If no compatible route is available, contact support before relying on future payouts.

If your local currency is not listed, ask your bank whether the account can receive another listed currency, such as USD, EUR, or GBP. Select an alternative only when your bank confirms compatibility; the bank may convert the payment or charge fees. See [BEATPAY](/help/earnings/beatpay) for route guidance.
