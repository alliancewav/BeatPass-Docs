<Warning>
  This page described workarounds for countries Stripe Connect could not support. Stripe onboarding is now closed, and those workarounds are no longer needed.
</Warning>

**BEATPAY** pays producers by direct bank transfer and supports a much wider range of countries and currencies than Stripe Connect did. Open **Finances**, click **Set up BEATPAY**, and search for your bank's currency in the setup.

If your currency does not appear in the list, contact support and we will look into adding it.
