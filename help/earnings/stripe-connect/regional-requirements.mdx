Looking for payout setup? Start in [Finances](https://open.beatpass.ca/producer-dashboard/finances) and use the bank payout option shown for your account.

<Warning>
  The Stripe Connect supported-country list only applied to Stripe onboarding, which is no longer offered in Finances.
</Warning>

Open **Finances** and follow **Set up BEATPAY** or **Upgrade to BEATPAY** when offered. The form lists the bank account currencies and destinations currently available to your account. A listed currency alone does not guarantee support for every bank or country. Confirm that your receiving account can accept the chosen route. See [BEATPAY](/help/earnings/beatpay).
