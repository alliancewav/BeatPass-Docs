<Warning>
  The Stripe Connect supported-country list only applied to Stripe onboarding, which is now closed.
</Warning>

**BEATPAY** supports bank payouts in a broad range of currencies worldwide. Open **Finances**, click **Set up BEATPAY**, and search for your bank account's currency — the setup shows every currency currently available. See [BEATPAY](/help/earnings/beatpay).
