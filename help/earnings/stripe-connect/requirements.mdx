<Warning>
  New Stripe Connect accounts can no longer be created, so there is no Stripe onboarding checklist to prepare for.
</Warning>

BEATPAY setup is simpler: you choose a bank account currency your bank can receive and enter the bank details requested for that currency — the form adapts automatically. See [BEATPAY](/help/earnings/beatpay).

If you already have a Stripe account from before, [Stripe Connect (Legacy)](/help/earnings/stripe-connect) explains how it winds down after you move to BEATPAY.
