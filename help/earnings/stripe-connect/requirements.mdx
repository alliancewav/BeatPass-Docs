Looking for payout setup? Start in [Finances](https://open.beatpass.ca/producer-dashboard/finances) and use the bank payout option shown for your account.

<Warning>
  Finances offers bank payout setup rather than a new Stripe Connect account. Follow the BEATPAY form shown for your account.
</Warning>

When BEATPAY is offered in **Finances**, choose a bank account currency your bank can receive, then provide the details requested by that setup form. Required information depends on the destination and provider review. See [BEATPAY](/help/earnings/beatpay).

If you already have a Stripe account from before, [Stripe Connect (Legacy)](/help/earnings/stripe-connect) explains how it winds down after you move to BEATPAY.
