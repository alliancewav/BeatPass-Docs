BeatPass does not provide tax advice. This page is general guidance to help you understand where records live and how payout providers can fit into the payout flow.

<Warning>
  Tax rules vary by country, business structure, and payout setup. Use a qualified tax professional for advice specific to your situation.
</Warning>

## For Producers

### Your Responsibility

If you earn through BeatPass, you are generally responsible for reporting that income and keeping your own records.

### Where Tax-Related Information Can Appear

| Place | What You May Find |
|-------|-------------------|
| **Producer Finances** | Earnings summary, [BEATPAY](/help/earnings/beatpay) or Stripe payout status, transfer history, and transaction records |
| **Stripe Express** | Stripe payout records and any Stripe-generated tax documents, if Stripe is active for your account |
| Your own records | Expenses, accounting notes, and supporting documentation |

### During Payout Setup

Stripe or another payout provider can ask for tax, identity, business, or bank information during onboarding or later account review. What they ask for depends on your country and account setup.

<Note>
  BeatPass does not guarantee a specific tax form or threshold in help docs because those rules can change and may be administered by a payout provider or tax authority.
</Note>

## For Buyers

Taxes, if required, are shown during checkout and on your payment records. Your invoices and billing history remain the best reference for what you were charged.

## Record-Keeping Tips

- Save payout and transaction records regularly
- Keep copies of invoices, receipts, and any Stripe-generated tax documents
- Track earnings by date and currency if you receive money across multiple currencies
- Keep separate records for business expenses related to your music work

## Common Questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Does BeatPass give tax advice?">
    No. BeatPass can show your earnings and billing records, but it does not replace professional tax advice.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Where would I find provider tax documents if they are issued for my account?">
    Stripe-generated tax documents are typically managed in Stripe Express when Stripe is your active payout route. Other payout records should be saved from **Producer Finances** and any provider account you use.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Do I need to keep my own records even if BeatPass and a provider show history?">
    Yes. Product history is useful, but your own organized records are still important.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What if I earn or pay in more than one currency?">
    Keep clear records by date and currency, then confirm with your tax advisor how you should report them.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
