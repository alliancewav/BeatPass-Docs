BeatPass uses a <Tooltip tip="A score calculated from track age, plays, and credited producers. It determines how much subscription revenue a track contributes." cta="See the formula" href="/help/earnings/contribution-system/formula">contribution value</Tooltip> system to share subscription revenue among producers.

## Why BeatPass publishes the formula

Subscription payout systems can be confusing if a platform only says "fair revenue share." BeatPass publishes the contribution logic so producers can understand what affects their share: recency, plays, and collaborator count.

Contribution points are not money or XP, and the formula does not promise a fixed payout. Open [My Beats](https://open.beatpass.ca/producer-dashboard/tracks) → **Impact** for the explanation, then [Finances](https://open.beatpass.ca/producer-dashboard/finances) for actual earnings.

## How Subscription Payout Share Works

1. BeatPass takes the subscription base amount as the producer pool.
2. All eligible track contribution values are summed.
3. Your producer share is calculated from your contribution divided by the platform total.
4. Eligible producers receive their share through the payout flow.

## Important Rules

### Eligible Beats

A beat needs to remain available for standard licensing, with every credited producer ready for subscription payouts. **Exclusive-only** beats and beats sold exclusively do not contribute to the subscription pool. An eligible exclusive-only beat can still earn from an exclusive sale.

### Ineligible Producers

If a producer is not payout-ready when a subscription split runs, their share can be excluded and redistributed among eligible producers.

### Periodic Refresh

Contribution values are refreshed periodically. A displayed calculation is a snapshot, rather than a live payout quote. New listening activity and time decay can affect later calculations.
