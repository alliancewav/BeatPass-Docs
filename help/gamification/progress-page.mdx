Use **Progress** to see what you have earned and choose your next producer goal. Open [Progress](https://open.beatpass.ca/producer-dashboard/progress) from **Producer Dashboard**. XP belongs to your account and the workspace uses your own producer profile.

<ProgressFigure />

## Page order

<Steps>
  <Step title="Progress">
    Review your level ring, cumulative XP, and next threshold. Open **Recent XP** for the five latest recorded awards, or use **Earn XP** to jump to earning actions.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Achievements">
    Browse **Unlocked**, then **Explore achievements**. Search or open **Category** to narrow the collection. Select **Choose a goal** when offered, or open a card yourself, then use **Choose this goal** to keep it in focus.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Earn XP">
    See the reward and counting rule for each eligible producer action, then follow its link.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Your choice appears as **Your next goal**. Use **View goal** to reopen it, **Change goal** when offered to review another achievement, or **Remove goal** to clear the choice. Choosing a goal does not award XP.

## New producer accounts

Level 1 with 0 XP is a normal starting point. The page does not require an upload before it can show your level. Use **Earn XP** to review the actions that can add your first XP.

## Progress updates

XP and achievement unlocks update automatically. A short processing delay can occur after eligible activity. If a refresh fails, use **Try again** while your last loaded progress stays visible.

Select **How progress works** for in-app guidance, or read the [full Progress guide](/help/producer-dashboard/progress) for exact labels and states.

## Personal progress and comparison

Progress does not embed a producer ranking. Select **Leaderboards** below the collection when you want to compare activity with other producers. Your level and achievement collection remain personal to your account.
