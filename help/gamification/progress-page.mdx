The Producer Dashboard **Progress** page is your personal action-and-achievement view. XP belongs to your producer account, even when you manage more than one artist profile.

## Page order

<Steps>
  <Step title="Progress">
    Review your current level, cumulative XP, next threshold, and five most recent XP awards.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Earn XP">
    See the current reward for each eligible producer action and where you can take that action.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Achievements">
    Filter available and unlocked achievements, then move through the collection eight at a time.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## New producer accounts

Level 1 with 0 XP is a normal starting point. The page does not require an upload before it can show your level. Use **Earn XP** to review the actions that can add your first XP.

## Progress updates

XP and achievement unlocks update automatically. A short processing delay can occur after eligible activity. There is no manual refresh control.

Select **How progress works** for in-app guidance, or read the [full Progress guide](/help/producer-dashboard/progress) for exact labels and states.

## Personal progress and comparison

Progress does not embed a producer ranking. Select **View Producer Leaderboards** when you want to compare activity with other producers. Your level and achievement collection remain personal to your account.
