The **Leaderboards** page complements your personal **Progress** page. It compares one clear producer measure at a time instead of combining unrelated activity into a single score.

<LeaderboardsFigure />

## Open Leaderboards

Open your **Producer Dashboard**, then select **Leaderboards**. The page heading is **Producer rankings**.

The **Leading producers** cards highlight the first three results. Swipe the cards on your phone, or use the previous/next arrows. Select a card to visit that producer's profile.

## Progress and Competition

**Progress** compares cumulative XP across producer accounts. Your XP comes from the actions and achievements explained in [XP & Producer Levels](/help/gamification/xp-system).

The other ranking choices compare artist-level activity:

- **Earning share** for current contribution share
- **Audience plays** for listener plays outside the artist team
- **Releases** for public tracks
- **Collaborations** for public tracks credited to multiple artists

These measures stay separate so you can understand why a producer holds a position. There is no blended Overall score on this page.

## Fair Ranking Basics

- Producers with equal results share a rank
- Recent activity can be reviewed over **7 days** or **30 days**
- **All time** has no artificial start date
- The top 20 appear first
- Your position and nearby producers appear separately when you are outside the top 20
- Private earnings amounts are never displayed

<Tip>
  Use rankings to choose an area you want to improve. Your [Progress](/help/producer-dashboard/progress), [Activity](/help/producer-dashboard/activity), [Finances](/help/producer-dashboard/finances), and [My Beats](/help/producer-dashboard/tracks) pages provide the detail behind each measure.
</Tip>

For exact interface labels, periods, eligibility, empty states, and update behavior, see the [Producer Dashboard Leaderboards guide](/help/producer-dashboard/leaderboards).
