The **Leaderboards** page in your Producer Dashboard lets you filter rankings by category. Here are the available categories:

## Categories

| Category | What it reflects |
| --- | --- |
| **Overall** | A weighted score combining XP progress (25%), plays (20%), uploads (15%), metadata completeness (15%), collaboration (15%), and contribution (10%) |
| **Plays** | Recorded track-play activity across your catalog |
| **Uploads** | The number of tracks in your BeatPass catalog |
| **Earnings** | Your share of platform contribution, not your private payout total |
| **Collaboration** | A weighted mix of collaborators, collaborative plays, contribution, and achievements |

## How Rankings Work

Each category ranks producers based on the activity described above. **Overall** converts each component position into a score and applies the documented weights. Use **How rankings work** on the leaderboard page for a concise explanation of the current method.

<Note>
The leaderboard page does not currently include genre-specific or time-period filters. All rankings reflect cumulative activity.
</Note>
