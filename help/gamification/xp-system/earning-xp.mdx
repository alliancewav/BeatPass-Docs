The **Earn XP** section on the Producer Dashboard **Progress** page shows the current reward amount and counting rule for each XP source.

## Current XP sources

| Action | Current award | Counting rule |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| **Upload a track** | +100 XP | Once for each eligible track |
| **First counted play** | +1 XP | Once when an eligible track receives its first counted play |
| **Complete track details** | +50 XP | Once when an eligible track has complete metadata |
| **Collaborate on a track** | +25 XP | Once for each eligible collaborative track credited to your account |
| **Unlock achievements** | Varies | Once for each achievement |

<Note>
  The play award is not +1 XP for every play. It is a one-time award for an eligible track after its first counted play.
</Note>

## Useful actions

- Select **Upload** to open the upload flow
- Select **Review My Beats** to review track details and collaboration credits
- Select **View achievements** to move to the achievement collection

Audience plays are informational. Genuine listener activity may trigger the one-time play award, but you should not create artificial plays to pursue XP.

## Achievement rewards

An available achievement shows its potential XP reward. After it unlocks, Progress shows the amount actually added to your account. Some achievements unlock with **No XP bonus** and still count toward your collection.

## Update timing

XP normally appears automatically after eligible activity is processed. A short delay can occur. If Progress cannot refresh, it keeps previously loaded information visible and offers **Try again**.
