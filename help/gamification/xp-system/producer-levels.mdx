Your producer level is calculated from the cumulative XP on your producer account. The **Your progress** section is the primary place to review it.

## What Your progress shows

- **Current level** — Your level number and producer title
- **Cumulative XP** — All XP currently recorded for your account
- **XP to Level** — The amount remaining before the next level
- **Level threshold** — The exact cumulative XP where the next level begins
- **XP progress** — A linear bar showing your position within the current level

A new account begins at Level 1 with 0 XP. This is a valid state, not an error.

## Level titles

BeatPass groups levels under these producer titles:

| Starting level | Title |
| ---: | --- |
| 1 | Novice Producer |
| 3 | Apprentice Producer |
| 5 | Intermediate Producer |
| 7 | Skilled Producer |
| 10 | Experienced Producer |
| 15 | Advanced Producer |
| 20 | Professional Producer |
| 30 | Expert Producer |
| 40 | Master Producer |
| 50 | Legendary Producer |

The XP required between levels increases as your level rises. Trust the exact next-level threshold shown in **Your progress** instead of estimating it.

## Maximum level

Level 100 is the current maximum. At that level, Progress shows **Maximum level reached** and no next-level remainder.
