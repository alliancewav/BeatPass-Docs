BeatPass adds XP to your producer account for specific eligible activity. Cumulative XP determines the level shown on the Producer Dashboard **Progress** page.

## Where XP appears

- In **Your progress** as your cumulative XP and next-level progress
- In **Recent XP** as the five latest recorded awards
- In unlocked achievements as the XP actually awarded
- In the **Progress** ranking on Producer Leaderboards

## Important distinctions

- Track-based XP sources are counted once per eligible track, not on every repeated action
- Achievement rewards vary by achievement
- An achievement can unlock with a real 0 XP bonus
- XP is not money and cannot currently be spent
