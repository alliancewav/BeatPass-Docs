## Pick your next task

<ProgressFigure />

- Check **Progress** for your level, XP total, and how much you need for the next level.
- Explore **Your achievements**, search or filter the collection, and choose a goal to keep in view.
- Open **Leaderboards** to explore **Earning share**, **Progress**, **Audience plays**, **Releases**, and **Collaborations** rankings.

## Getting There

Open **Producer Dashboard** from your profile menu, then choose **Progress** or **Leaderboards** in the producer navigation.
