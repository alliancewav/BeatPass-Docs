The live achievement collection appears in **Achievements** on the Producer Dashboard **Progress** page. BeatPass shows the complete current collection, including achievements you have not unlocked.

## Browse by status

<AchievementGalleryFigure />

Use the buttons below search and category to choose:

- **Not unlocked**: achievements not yet recorded as unlocked for your account
- **Unlocked**: Achievements already recorded for your account
- **All**: The complete collection

**All** is selected on a fresh visit. Your **Unlocked** collection scrolls horizontally; **Explore achievements** shows uncollected goals below it. Saved links can reopen filters or an achievement detail.

## Browse by category

Use **Search achievements** to search names and requirements. Open **Category** and choose a category to narrow the collection. Current categories can cover:

- Uploads and track milestones
- Plays and track performance
- Track details and profile completion
- Genres and collaboration
- Overall producer progress and track quality
- Seasonal activity and beat requests

The exact category labels available depend on the current collection.

An available goal may be seasonal, historical, or subject to account-age requirements. Open it and read its requirement before choosing it as your next goal.

## Read an achievement

Each achievement shows:

- Name and requirement
- **Not unlocked** or **Unlocked** status
- **Common**, **Rare**, **Epic**, or **Legendary** rarity
- Potential XP when an uncollected goal has a reward, or recorded XP when unlocked
- Unlock date when available

<Note>
  Open a card for its full requirement and category. Some goals compare your activity with other producers, so there is no individual percent-complete bar.
</Note>

## Pagination

Uncollected goals appear six per page. Use **Previous** and **Next** below them. Your unlocked collection scrolls separately with **Previous achievement** and **Next achievement** when needed. Use **Clear filters** to return to the full collection.

Open a card to browse details with **Previous achievement** and **Next achievement**. Scroll to read the requirement and producer examples; use **Choose this goal** at the bottom when offered to keep that achievement above the collection.

You can also start with **Choose a goal** when it appears. Your chosen card offers **View goal**, **Change goal** when another is available, and **Remove goal**. Changing opens another achievement to review before you choose it. This choice does not unlock anything or add XP. **Early Adopter** explains its historical requirement instead of offering goal selection.

A recorded 0 XP award says **No XP bonus**. Missing historical reward information stays **Unlocked** without a number.

The live collection can change as BeatPass adds or updates achievements. The **Progress** page is the current reference for names, requirements, rarity, and rewards.
