Achievements recognize producer milestones across uploads, plays, track details, profile setup, genres, collaborations, requests, and other BeatPass activity. They can also award XP.

## Find your achievements

Open your **Producer Dashboard**, select **Progress**, then move to **Achievements**.

## Collection summary

The summary uses one **Collection progress** panel for your unlocked count and percentage. It also shows:

- **Achievement XP** — XP actually added by achievement unlocks
- **Latest unlock** — Your most recently unlocked achievement and its date

## Available and unlocked rewards

An **Available** achievement shows the XP it can award. An **Unlocked** achievement shows the XP actually added when it unlocked. These amounts can differ.

Some achievements unlock with **No XP bonus**. They still count toward **Unlocked** and **Collection**.

## Filter the collection

The default **Status** is **Available**. You can choose **Unlocked** or **All**. Use **Category** to focus on one type of activity.

Eight achievements appear per page. When a filtered result has more than one page, use **Previous** and **Next** to move through it. Changing a filter returns you to the first page.

## Rarity and status

Each achievement displays its status, rarity, and category as plain text. Rarity helps describe how uncommon a milestone is, but it does not replace the XP amount shown on the achievement.
