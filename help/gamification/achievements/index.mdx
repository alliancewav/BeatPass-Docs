Achievements recognize producer milestones across uploads, plays, track details, profile setup, genres, collaborations, requests, and other BeatPass activity. They can also award XP.

## Find your achievements

Open [Progress](https://open.beatpass.ca/producer-dashboard/progress) from **Producer Dashboard**, then choose **Achievements**. Browse your unlocked badges or explore the available goals.

## Collection summary

<AchievementGalleryFigure />

**Your achievements** shows your unlocked count, goals left to explore, collection progress, and XP **from achievements**. The unlocked collection brings your recorded badges together; open one to see its date, reward, and available history.

## Read the reward

A **Not unlocked** achievement shows potential XP when it has an XP reward. An **Unlocked** achievement shows the XP recorded when it unlocked. These amounts can differ.

A recorded 0 XP reward says **No XP bonus** and still counts toward the collection. If the historical award is missing, the card says **Unlocked** without a number.

## Filter the collection

A fresh visit starts with **All**. Use **Search achievements**, open **Category** to choose a category, then select **All**, **Unlocked**, or **Not unlocked**. **Clear filters** returns to the full view.

Unlocked badges scroll horizontally, with **Previous achievement** and **Next achievement** when needed. **Explore achievements** shows six uncollected goals per page; use **Previous** and **Next** below them. **Not unlocked** describes your account status, not a promise that a seasonal or historical goal can be earned now.

## Rarity and status

Cards show a status and rarity pill. Locked artwork is muted and marked with a lock; unlocked artwork is shown in colour. Open a card for its full requirement and category. Rarity is a configured label, separate from the measured number of producers who have unlocked the achievement. It does not determine the XP amount.

## Open a badge

Choose an achievement to read its requirement and reward. Scroll for **Producer community**, public producer examples, and available history. Goal and suggested-task buttons appear at the bottom when offered.

**Producer community** can show a measured unlock count or percentage with its update date. Its cohort is producer accounts linked to at least one track. When a public producer list is available, use **Show more producers** to browse further.

For an unlocked badge, the history shows the saved facts available from that unlock. Missing history or reward information is not the same as a zero. Community information can be unavailable without changing your earned badge.

## Choose your next goal

<AchievementGoalFigure />

1. Select **Choose a goal** when it appears, or open a locked achievement yourself.
2. Read its requirement, then select **Choose this goal** at the bottom when offered.
3. Find its artwork, requirement, and reward in **Your next goal** above the collection. Use **View goal** to reopen it.

Use **Change goal**, when offered, to review another achievement before choosing it. **Remove goal** clears your choice. The choice is remembered for this account on this device when possible; **For this visit** means it will not persist. If you unlock it, **Goal unlocked** celebrates the result without automatically choosing another. Choosing a goal does not award XP.

**Early Adopter** describes the first producer profiles from the BeatPass beta and has no **Choose this goal** action.
