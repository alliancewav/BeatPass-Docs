An achievement's rarity label describes its configured place in the collection. Open the badge to check its requirement and reward, then use any **Producer community** information to see how widely it has actually been unlocked.

## Current rarity labels

| Label | Pill colour |
| --- | --- |
| **Common** | Neutral grey |
| **Rare** | Blue |
| **Epic** | Purple |
| **Legendary** | Orange |

## How rarity appears

The rarity pill keeps its colour in both themes. It is separate from the artwork: locked achievements have muted artwork and a lock, while unlocked artwork appears in colour. Text identifies **Not unlocked** or **Unlocked**, and the detail view shows the category. Rarity is separate from a measured unlock percentage and does not guarantee that a goal is currently attainable.

## Rarity and XP

Rarity and XP are separate. Do not estimate a reward from rarity alone. A locked achievement shows potential XP when it has a reward. An unlocked achievement shows recorded XP; if that historical amount is missing, **Unlocked** appears without a number.

A real 0 XP unlock can exist at any configured rarity and still count toward collection progress.
