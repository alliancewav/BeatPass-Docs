A quick reference for terms and concepts you'll encounter on BeatPass.


## A

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Achievement">
    A milestone badge you unlock by reaching specific goals on BeatPass: such as uploading a certain number of tracks or earning a set amount of plays. Achievements are displayed on your profile. See [Achievements](/help/gamification/achievements/index).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Album (Collection)">
    A group of tracks bundled together under a single title and cover art. Producers create albums to organize related beats. Listeners can browse and like entire albums.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>


## B

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Backstage">
    The producer workspace for managing uploads, catalog, activity, and finances. Open **Producer Dashboard** from your avatar menu. Producer access is available after approval.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Backstage Insights">
    Content-level analytics for individual tracks, albums, or artist profiles. Shows plays, engagement, geographic data, and more. See [Backstage Insights](/help/analytics/backstage-insights/index).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Beat Request">
    A brief describing the sound you need, with eligible producer submissions. Posting consumes tokens and does not guarantee custom work or a match. Check your balance before posting. See [Beat Requests](/help/beat-requests).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="BEATPAY">
    BeatPass bank payout setup powered by Wise. When it appears in **Finances**, it can receive eligible exclusive-sale payouts and any other earnings marked active for BEATPAY. See [BEATPAY Bank Payouts](/help/earnings/beatpay).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="BeatPassID">
    The track identity record that combines an audio fingerprint with licensing, Sample-Safe status, AI disclosure when available, and promised exclusive deliverables. It helps connect licenses to the audio and terms they cover. See [BeatPassID](/help/downloads-and-licensing/beatpass-id).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>


## C

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Channel">
    A curated feed of content on BeatPass, organized by genre, mood, or theme. Channels are updated automatically or by editors. See [Channels](/help/discovering-music/channels/index).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Collaboration">
    When multiple producers are credited on the same track. Collaborators share the track's contribution value equally. Collaborative tracks appear on each credited producer's profile.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Contribution Value">
    A calculated score that determines how much subscription revenue a track earns. It factors in time decay, popularity (plays), and collaboration splits. See [Contribution System](/help/earnings/contribution-system/index).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Cover Art">
    The image displayed alongside a track or album. Producers can upload custom artwork or use BeatPass cover art templates. See [Cover Art](/help/uploading/cover-art/index).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>


## D–E

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Discography">
    The main public producer-profile view that can combine pinned tracks, popular and new beats, albums, about information, and professional credits.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Exclusive License">
    A one-time license defined by the track's purchase preview and certificate. It stops new licensing after purchase, while valid licenses issued earlier remain in effect. See [Exclusive Licenses](/help/downloads-and-licensing/exclusive-licenses/index).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>


## F–G

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Free Account">
    A non-paid BeatPass account for listening, favorites, and playlists. New standard licenses require eligible customer access and a paid plan with downloads; an exclusive purchase is separate. Producer access is free. See [account roles](/help/getting-started/understanding-roles).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Fingerprint">
    Audio analysis that helps identify a recorded track and connect it to a license. A matching hash is not proof of copyright clearance. See **BeatPassID**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Grace Period">
    The time between cancelling a subscription and losing access. During the grace period, you keep all plan features and can resume your subscription without resubscribing.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Genre Channel">
    A channel dedicated to a specific music genre, such as Trap, R&B, or Lo-Fi. See [Genre Channels](/help/discovering-music/channels).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>


## L

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Leaderboard">
    Rankings that compare producers across the current leaderboard categories exposed by BeatPass. See [Leaderboards](/help/gamification/leaderboards/index).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Library">
    Your account-linked collection area for saved tracks, collections, producer pages, listening history, beat requests, licenses, and related personal content. See [Your Library](/help/library/index).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="License Certificate">
    A document recording the beat, licensed party, issued terms, and certificate ID, with a public verification link. It supports a rights review without guaranteeing third-party clearance. See [License Certificates](/help/downloads-and-licensing/license-certificates).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>


## N–P

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Non-Exclusive License">
    A grant for one licensed project that can coexist with other artists' licenses for the same beat. A qualifying standard download issues or reuses a certificate. Re-downloading does not license a second song. See [standard terms](/help/legal/license-terms/non-exclusive).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Perpetual License">
    A license without an automatic time limit, subject to its terms, breach provisions, and valid underlying rights. Cancellation does not itself revoke it. A technical certificate failure is separate from the legal grant.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Pinned Track">
    A track a producer highlights at the top of their producer profile. Only one track can be pinned at a time.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Platform Fee">
    A separate buyer charge shown at eligible checkouts. Its amount and any active-plan waiver are shown before payment. Do not assume a benefit on exclusive purchases applies to subscription charges. See [Platform Fee](/help/billing/platform-fee).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Producer Dashboard">
    The career-level management hub for producers. Includes catalog management, earnings, activity tracking, progress, and leaderboards. See [Producer Dashboard](/help/producer-dashboard/index).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Producer Profile">
    The public profile page for an approved producer on BeatPass. It can show a bio, discography, pinned track, gallery, professional credits, and follower-facing identity. See [Producer Profiles](/help/profiles/producer-profiles/index).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Professional Credits">
    A portfolio feature on producer profiles where producers can showcase Spotify-linked work released outside BeatPass. See [Professional Credits](/help/profiles/producer-profiles/profile-elements).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>


## Q–R

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Queue">
    The list of tracks lined up in the player. BeatPass can restore the saved queue in the same browser when local storage is available. It is not a cross-device saved playlist. See [Queue & Playback](/help/playing-music/queue-and-playback).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Radio">
    Recommendations started from a track, artist, or genre. See [Radio](/help/discovering-music/radio).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Repost">
    Sharing a track or collection on your profile activity feed. See [Reposts](/help/social-features/reposts).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>


## S

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Sample-Safe Warranty">
    A producer-set clearance status that tells BeatPass whether a beat is marked **Cleared** or **Needs Clearance**. It is self-certification, not an automatic legal guarantee from BeatPass. See [Sample-Safe](/help/uploading/sample-safe).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Stripe Connect">
    The legacy producer payout route. Existing eligible routes can remain active until BEATPAY is ready for the relevant earnings. New and resumed Stripe setup are closed. Follow the status in Finances. See [Stripe Connect](/help/earnings/stripe-connect).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Subscriber">
    An account with an active paid BeatPass plan. Features follow the selected plan. Current producer accounts cannot acquire new standard catalog licenses by subscribing. See [account roles](/help/getting-started/understanding-roles).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>


## T–V

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Time Decay">
    The gradual decrease in a track's contribution value over time. Newer tracks contribute more to the revenue pool. The half-life is approximately 6 months. See [Earnings Formula](/help/earnings/contribution-system/formula).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Token">
    A non-cash allowance used to post Beat Requests. Check the current balance, monthly allowance, and expiry in your account. Tokens are not a promise of custom work. See [Understanding Tokens](/help/beat-requests/understanding-tokens).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Verified Badge">
    A badge on a producer profile indicating the producer's identity has been confirmed by BeatPass. See [Verified Badge](/help/profiles/producer-profiles/profile-elements).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>


## X

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="XP (Experience Points)">
    Points earned by producers for actions like uploading tracks, earning plays, and completing metadata. XP determines your producer level and unlocks achievements. See [XP System](/help/gamification/xp-system/index).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
