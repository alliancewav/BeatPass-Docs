Audio Recon helps you find recordings that sound similar to your beats and review them in one workspace. Open [Audio Recon](https://open.beatpass.ca/producer-dashboard/audio-recon), or choose **Producer Dashboard** → **Audio Recon** under Catalog.

<Warning>
  Audio Recon detects **similarity**, not confirmed placements. A match may come from shared royalty-free samples, similar sound design, or coincidence. Always verify by listening before assuming your beat was used.
</Warning>

<AudioReconFigure />

## Start with Matches or Your Beats

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Matches">
    Review beats with similarity findings. Search by title or choose **Platform** to narrow the recordings to Spotify, YouTube, or Deezer.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open a beat">
    Expand the beat to compare your original audio with the findings. **View track** opens its track page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check Your beats">
    Use this tab to see scan status even when no match is listed. Filter **Status** by **Not scanned**, **Processing**, **Scan unavailable**, **Complete**, or **No matches**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review before taking action">
    Listen to both recordings and check your licensing history before contacting support about suspected unauthorized use.
  </Step>
</Steps>



Audio Recon helps you investigate possible uses. A similarity result does not grant permission to copy another recording.

## Who Can Use Audio Recon

Audio Recon scans run for tracks where **at least one credited producer has a verified artist profile**. This means:

- The scan is gated on the artist being verified, not the individual user account.
- For collaborative beats, the scan can run when any one of the credited producers is verified.
- All linked producers on a matching track are notified: not just the verified one.

<Note>
  If you are credited on a track alongside a verified producer, you can still receive Audio Recon match notifications even if your own profile is not yet verified.
</Note>

## How It Works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Your beat is fingerprinted">
    BeatPass fingerprints the track when it is uploaded or when the audio file is replaced.
  </Step>
  <Step title="BeatPass scans eligible audio">
    Scans need uploaded audio, a completed fingerprint, and a verified credited producer. New beats are checked first; scanned beats return after the interval shown in the scan-schedule explanation. The next batch time does not promise a scan time for every beat.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review matching recordings">
    Results ready for your review appear here. A higher score is a stronger similarity signal, rather than proof of use.
  </Step>
  <Step title="You review the result">
    Review results in the dashboard's **Matches** tab or the **Audio Recon** section on the track page. Notifications follow your enabled channels.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Where Matches Appear

**Matches** in the dashboard groups findings by beat. **Your beats** also includes tracks without displayed findings, so you can check what has or has not been scanned. The track page keeps its own **Audio Recon** section for authorized producers.

If there are no matches, open **Your beats** and check the status before drawing a conclusion. **Scan unavailable** is different from **No matches**. Use **Clear filters** when a search hides the results you expected, or **Retry** if the page could not load.

| Readiness message | Your next step |
| --- | --- |
| **Verification required** | Follow **Request Verification** |
| **Upload an audio file to enable scanning.** | Add the beat's audio |
| **Waiting for audio fingerprinting** | Check again after processing |
| **Ready for an upcoming batch** | Wait for the automatic rotation |
| **Next rotation from** | Use the date as eligibility context, rather than a guaranteed completion time |
| **Scan schedule unavailable** | Retry or contact support if it continues |

## Similarity Scores

The score describes how similar the recordings appear to the matching system. Listen before deciding whether it is your beat. Shared samples, common sound design, and coincidental similarities can produce matches. Fingerprint checks on new uploads are a separate process.

## Notifications

When a new match is found, BeatPass notifies all producers linked to the track through your enabled notification channels:

- In-app notifications
- Email notifications

Review your notification settings to control how those alerts reach you.

## How to Review a Match

<Steps>
  <Step title="Listen to both recordings">
    Compare the matched song against your beat closely.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Rule out common false positives">
    Shared sample packs, similar drum kits, and common sound design can all create high similarity.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check BeatPass licensing context">
    If the song uses your beat, compare it against your BeatPass licensing history and existing certificates.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Escalate only after verification">
    If you believe the use is unauthorised, contact BeatPass support with the match details.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Covered Platforms

Audio Recon currently references:

- Spotify
- YouTube
- Deezer

## Common Questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can I trigger a manual scan?">
    Scans run automatically. **Refresh results** reloads the dashboard; it does not start a scan. Check **Your beats** for readiness and the next rotation.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Why do I not see Audio Recon on my track?">
    The track may not be eligible yet, the fingerprint may still be pending, no credited producer is verified, or no qualifying match has been found.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Does a match prove someone used my beat?">
    No. Audio Recon is for investigation, not automatic proof of infringement.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What if I am not verified?">
    You can still receive notifications if a verified co-producer's track matches. For a beat with no verified credited producer, complete the producer verification flow first.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
