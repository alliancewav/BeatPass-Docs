Exclusive licensing lets you offer a beat as a one-time premium purchase. Unlike BeatPass standard licensing, the exclusive certificate uses the terms you configure on the track.

## What an Exclusive Sale Means

When someone buys a beat exclusively:

- BeatPass generates an **exclusive license certificate**
- The beat is marked **Sold**
- New standard downloads stop
- Existing non-exclusive licenses remain valid
- The sale is recorded in your producer finances

## Licensing Models on the Track Form

BeatPass currently shows three licensing models:

| UI Label | What It Means |
|----------|---------------|
| **Standard Licensing** | Standard BeatPass subscription licensing only |
| **Standard + Exclusive** | Offer both standard licensing and exclusive purchase |
| **Exclusive Only** | Offer the beat only as an exclusive opportunity |

<Tip>
  **Standard + Exclusive** is the most flexible option when you want subscription discovery now but still want the beat available for a future exclusive buyer.
</Tip>

## What You Can Configure

For exclusive-capable tracks, BeatPass lets you configure:

- **Exclusive price**
- **Currency** (`USD`, `EUR`, `GBP`, or `CAD`)
- **Price & availability** using **Available** for a priced offer, subject to checkout readiness, or **Private** for buyer inquiries
- **Contact email** for licensing inquiries
- **Producer and buyer revenue share**
- **Territory**
- **Governing law country**
- **Usage rights**
- **Files to deliver**
- **Content ID permission**
- **Publishing royalty**
- **Saved presets**

## Review the starting terms

BeatPass's current default exclusive setup uses:

- **20% producer / 80% buyer** revenue share
- **Worldwide** territory
- A governing-law country shown in the exclusive terms preview
- **3%** publishing royalty
- **WAV (Uncompressed)** as the initial file selection; a publishable exclusive offer also requires **Stems (Separated Tracks)** and your delivery guarantee
- Broad usage rights including distribution, streaming, video, broadcasting, and for-profit performances

Your saved personal default can replace these starting values. Review the actual offer rather than assuming it matches this list. You can change configurable terms before a sale. **Sold** is applied automatically when the buyer's certificate is issued; it is not a status you select yourself. Review governing-law and dispute language carefully because the generated certificate records the terms shown for that purchase.

## Publish and check the offer

1. Open the beat from [My Beats](https://open.beatpass.ca/producer-dashboard/tracks) and choose **Edit**.
2. In **Licensing Options**, choose **Standard + Exclusive** or **Exclusive Only**.
3. Review the price, financial terms, permissions, territory, and promised files.
4. Complete the delivery guarantee and legal-name requirements, then **Sign and publish** the offer.
5. Open the saved beat to review the customer-facing offer. If checkout is unavailable, follow the payout or signature notice rather than repeatedly changing the price.

See [Setting prices](/help/licensing-for-producers/exclusive-licenses/setting-prices) for the signature and payout steps.

## Important Behavior After a Sale

Once a beat is sold exclusively:

- The beat's exclusive terms are locked
- BeatPass blocks new standard licenses
- The buyer keeps the exact terms shown in the issued certificate

<Warning>
  Earlier non-exclusive licenses are still valid after the beat sells exclusively. Exclusive sales stop future licensing; they do not revoke older licenses that were already issued.
</Warning>
