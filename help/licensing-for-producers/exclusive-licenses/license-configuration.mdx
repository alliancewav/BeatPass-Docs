When you enable exclusive licensing on a beat, BeatPass lets you configure the terms that will be written into the buyer's exclusive certificate. Open [My Beats](https://open.beatpass.ca/producer-dashboard/tracks) → **Edit** → **Licensing Options** to review them. Saved personal terms can replace the platform starting values below.

## Where You Configure It

You see exclusive configuration inside the track form when the licensing model is:

- **Standard + Exclusive**
- **Exclusive Only**



## Revenue share

In **Financial terms**, choose a preset or **Customize percentages**. **Producer revenue share** and **Buyer revenue share** describe the commercial revenue split recorded in the offer, separate from the publishing royalty below.

| Field | Starting value |
|-------|-----------------|
| **Producer revenue share** | 20% |
| **Buyer revenue share** | 80% |

BeatPass requires the two values to add up to **100%**.

## Territory

Enter where the exclusive license applies in **Territory**. This is a text field, so use wording you intend to offer. The platform starting value is **Worldwide**.

## Governing Law Country

This controls which country's laws are written into the exclusive certificate for disputes and contract interpretation.

Do not rely on the default without reviewing it. The country shown in the preview and final certificate is what the buyer sees for that purchase, subject to the BeatPass Terms and any non-waivable law.

<Note>
  For a new configuration, BeatPass prefills this from the country saved in **Account Settings**. Review it before publishing. Changing your account country later does not overwrite terms already saved on a beat.
</Note>

## Usage Rights

The **Permissions & files** area separates rights into two groups.

The following core rights are included with every exclusive license and appear as enabled, read-only controls:

- Distribution
- Music and video use
- Non-profit performances

You can separately choose these optional permissions before a sale:

- Broadcasting
- Streaming services
- For-profit performances
- Content ID registration

Review these rights carefully before listing a beat, especially if you want more limited or more custom terms.

## Files to Deliver

The exclusive form lets you choose which files the buyer receives. Current options include:

- WAV
- WAV Tagged
- MP3 320
- MP3 256
- FLAC
- Stems
- MIDI
- Trackout / Multitrack

A new track starts with only **WAV (Uncompressed)** selected because BeatPass can deliver the original uploaded WAV immediately.

Exclusive offers must also include **Stems (Separated Tracks)**. Selecting an exclusive model includes WAV and stems and asks you to guarantee that you can provide the promised files. Complete that confirmation before publishing. Any other checked file is also a promise you must fulfill. BeatPass does not host stems, MIDI, or trackout files. If promised files cannot be delivered, the purchase may be refunded and the issued license may be revoked.

## Content ID Permission

Exclusive configuration includes a toggle for **Content ID** registration. The platform starting configuration allows it, but the setting saved on the beat controls the buyer's actual terms. Earlier standard licensees keep their rights, so review possible conflicts before allowing automated claims.

## Publishing royalty

**Financial terms** also includes a separate **Publishing royalty** percentage. Review it alongside the revenue share; the accepted agreement defines how each applies.

| Field | Starting value |
|-------|-----------------|
| **Publishing royalty** | 3% |

This setting records ongoing publishing participation separately from the upfront price and commercial revenue split.

## Contact Email

If you enable exclusive licensing, BeatPass requires an **exclusive contact email**. Buyers can use it for licensing questions, and BeatPass uses it as part of the exclusive setup.

## What Locks After a Sale

Once a beat is sold exclusively:

- The exclusive terms are locked
- BeatPass keeps the certificate tied to the exact sold terms
- The audio, licensing model, status, price, and license configuration can no longer be changed
- You can still update the cover art
