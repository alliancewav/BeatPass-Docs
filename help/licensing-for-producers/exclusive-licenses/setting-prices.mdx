Setting the right exclusive price matters because it controls both how your beat is positioned and how easy it is for a buyer to move from interest to checkout.

## Current Price Fields

For exclusive-capable beats, BeatPass lets you set:

- **Exclusive price**
- **Currency**
- **Exclusive contact email**

Choose **Available** to show a public price. Checkout also needs a complete, signed offer and an active exclusive-sale payout route. Choose **Private** to keep the price and currency controls visible but disabled; buyers will be directed to chat with you instead.

## Sign the current offer

Complete your producer payout identity in [Finances](https://open.beatpass.ca/producer-dashboard/finances). Your legal name synchronizes with Account details and locks when ready; [contact support](/help/contact-support) if it needs correction. In the track editor, confirm the deliverables and your authority to license for all rights holders, then choose **Sign and publish**. Buyers cannot start checkout with an unsigned or changed offer. Existing offers need current producer authorization before checkout is available.

The buyer accepts the displayed rights and the exact price, fee, total, and currency before payment. A changed quote requires renewed acceptance. The purchase and certificate preserve the accepted record.

## Current Supported Currencies

BeatPass currently supports these exclusive sale currencies:

- **USD**
- **EUR**
- **GBP**
- **CAD**

## Platform Fee Behaviour

BeatPass adds the platform fee on top of your listed exclusive amount when it applies: the buyer pays the fee, not you. An eligible active buyer plan can waive it. This means:

- The BeatPass platform fee is added to the buyer's total rather than deducted from the listed producer amount.
- The buyer's total includes your price plus the BeatPass platform fee, or only your price when an active plan benefit waives the fee.
- The exact fee percentage and flat-rate component are set by the platform and may be shown at checkout.

## Pricing Factors to Consider

### Beat Quality and Demand

Higher prices make more sense when the beat has:

- Strong production quality
- Unique sound or obvious market fit
- Proven play activity or buyer interest
- Premium deliverables such as stems, MIDI, or full trackouts

### Your Catalog Strategy

Think about whether the beat is:

- A broad discovery beat that benefits from **Standard + Exclusive**
- A premium record you want to protect as **Exclusive Only**
- A flexible catalog beat where a moderate exclusive price makes sense

### Your Reputation

Pricing should also reflect:

- Your current credits
- Your catalog depth
- The quality of your profile presentation
- How often buyers already engage with your beats

## Contact Email Matters

BeatPass requires a contact email on exclusive-capable tracks. Buyers may use it when:

- They have licensing questions
- They want to discuss custom terms
- Instant purchase is not the right fit for the situation

## Payment & Payout Notes

When someone completes an exclusive purchase:

1. The buyer pays your listed price plus the BeatPass platform fee when applicable, or your listed price alone when an active plan benefit waives it.
2. BeatPass records the sale in your producer finances.
3. BeatPass queues the producer amount through the payout route shown for exclusive sales in **Finances**.

<Info>
  Exclusive checkout needs an active exclusive-sale payout route. Subscription earnings have a separate readiness check. Follow **Payout setup** in Finances for each earning type; saving bank details alone does not enable every stream.
</Info>

## After the Beat Sells

Once a beat is sold exclusively:

- The beat is marked **Sold**.
- The exclusive terms are locked.
- BeatPass stops new standard licenses for that beat.

Price changes apply to offers before sale. After any price or term change, review and sign the current offer again before expecting checkout to be available.

## Common Questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can I change the price later?">
    Yes, as long as the beat has not already sold exclusively.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Should every beat have the same price?">
    Usually no. It is more practical to price around demand, deliverables, and how premium the beat really is.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What if I want to talk to the buyer first?">
    Choose **Private** under **Price & availability**. The track will not have an instant-purchase price, and interested buyers can use BeatPass chat to discuss the offer with you.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
