## What Changes When You Move Up

- **Explorer** is for free account access and discovery tools.
- All paid plans currently unlock **unlimited non-exclusive downloads**.
- Beat request capacity increases as you move from **Classic** to **Plus** to **Pro**.
- **Plus** is the current recommended paid tier.
- **Pro** raises the monthly request allowance to **10**, adds **Priority Support**, and waives the platform fee on eligible exclusive license purchases while the Pro subscription is active.
- The Pro fee waiver applies only to one-time exclusive licensing checkout. It does not remove the platform fee from Pro subscription checkout or renewal.

## Shared Billing Rules

- All paid plans are billed **monthly**.
- Current paid prices are in **CAD**.
- If you already have a paid plan, open your **profile avatar** -> **Billing** and use **Change plan**.
- If you do not have a paid plan yet, open your **profile avatar** -> **Billing** and use **View Plans**.
- BeatPass adds a platform fee (**12% + $3.00 flat**) on top of the plan price at checkout. The fee is shown before you confirm payment. [Learn more →](/help/billing/platform-fee)
- For the legal summary covering renewals, cancellations, request tokens, platform fees, and plan changes, see [Subscription and Plan Terms](/help/legal/subscription-plan-terms).
