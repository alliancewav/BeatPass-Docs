Choose a plan around the music you want to make and the features you will use. Open the [current pricing page](https://open.beatpass.ca/pricing) for the available plans and prices.

Producer access is free. A paid subscription does not let a producer account acquire new standard licenses for other producers' beats. See [account roles](/help/getting-started/understanding-roles) before upgrading for downloads.

## Before you pay

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check what the plan includes">
    Review download eligibility and the current request allowance. Producer access is separate and free.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the payment details">
    Check the billing interval, currency, renewal terms, and final checkout total, including any platform fee.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm when it matches">
    Continue only when the plan and total are what you expect. Keep your confirmation and use [Billing](/help/billing) to review the subscription afterwards.
  </Step>
</Steps>

A subscription and an exclusive license purchase are separate. A plan benefit may affect an exclusive-purchase fee, but only the benefit shown for your active plan applies.
