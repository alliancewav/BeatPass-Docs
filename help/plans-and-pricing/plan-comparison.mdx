## Downloads and licenses

Check that the plan includes downloads and that your account is eligible. Producer accounts cannot acquire new standard catalog licenses through a subscription. Existing valid licenses and your own producer files are handled separately.

A download license still has project, distribution, video, performance, and attribution conditions. Read [standard license terms](/help/legal/license-terms/non-exclusive) before release.

## Requests and support

Compare the request allowance and support options displayed for each plan. Check your account's current token balance before posting; do not rely on an older screenshot or price list.

## Exclusive purchases

An exclusive beat purchase uses its own offer and checkout. Check the displayed fee or waiver before payment. A subscription does not include the exclusive purchase price.
