## What Stays The Same Across Paid Tiers

- **Classic**, **Plus**, and **Pro** are all monthly paid plans billed in **CAD**.
- All paid tiers currently include **unlimited non-exclusive downloads**.
- All paid tiers use the same base billing flow through **Billing**.
- BeatPass shows the platform fee separately during subscription checkout. Exclusive checkout shows either the calculated fee or the active Pro waiver.

---

## Where The Tiers Actually Differ

- **Explorer** is the free plan and does not unlock paid-plan downloads.
- **Classic** gives you the lowest paid price and **1** request token.
- **Plus** raises that limit to **6** request tokens and is the current recommended plan.
- **Pro** raises the limit to **10**, adds **Priority Support**, and waives platform fees on eligible exclusive license purchases while Pro is active.

## Which Comparison Matters Most

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Requests">
    If request volume is your main decision point, the current split is:

    - **Explorer** - 1
    - **Classic** - 1
    - **Plus** - 6
    - **Pro** - 10
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Downloads">
    If downloads are the main reason you pay:

    - **Explorer** does not unlock the paid-plan download flow.
    - **Classic**, **Plus**, and **Pro** all currently include unlimited non-exclusive downloads.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Support">
    If support matters most:

    - **Explorer** - Standard Support (Email)
    - **Classic** and **Plus** - Enhanced Support (Email, Chat)
    - **Pro** - Priority Support
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Exclusive licensing">
    If you regularly buy exclusive licenses:

    - **Explorer**, **Classic**, and **Plus** use the platform fee shown during exclusive checkout.
    - **Pro** waives that exclusive-purchase platform fee while the Pro subscription is active.
    - Subscription checkout and renewal fees are unchanged for every paid tier, including Pro.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>
