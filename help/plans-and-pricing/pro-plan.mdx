## What Pro Lists Today

The current Pro plan configuration lists:

- Everything in Classic
- Custom beat requests (10 tokens per month)
- No platform fees on exclusive licensing
- Priority Support

<Info>
  The fee benefit applies to one-time exclusive license purchases made while your Pro subscription is active. Pro subscription checkout and renewal still use the normal subscription platform fee.
</Info>
