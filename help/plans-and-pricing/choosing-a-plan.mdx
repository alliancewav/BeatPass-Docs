Start with what you need today. You can compare the available plans on the [pricing page](https://open.beatpass.ca/pricing) without relying on an old price table.

## Match the plan to your next task

| Your goal | What to check |
| --- | --- |
| Listen and organize favorites | Start with a free account |
| License beats for your songs | A plan with downloads, account eligibility, and the standard license limits |
| Send custom beat requests | The included request allowance and your current balance |
| Get help | The support options shown for that plan |
| Buy an exclusive beat | The separate offer price and any applicable active-plan fee benefit |
| Upload and sell your beats | Apply for free producer access; a paid plan is not required |

Producer accounts cannot get new standard catalog licenses, even with a paid subscription. If you already hold a valid license, use [Library → My Licenses](https://open.beatpass.ca/library/licenses) to retrieve it.

## Compare the full cost

Check the currency, billing interval, renewal terms, and total at checkout. The platform fee is shown before confirmation. Do not assume a benefit on an exclusive purchase also changes subscription fees.

## When you are ready

Open [Pricing](https://open.beatpass.ca/pricing), choose the plan that meets your needs, and review checkout before paying. If your account already has a subscription, start in [Billing](https://open.beatpass.ca/billing) and use [Manage your plan](/help/billing/managing-subscription).

Read the [plan comparison](/help/plans-and-pricing/plan-comparison) and [Subscription and Plan Terms](https://open.beatpass.ca/pages/subscription-and-plan-terms) if you need more detail.
