Editorial collections bring together beats selected by BeatPass for a theme or featured listening experience. You can explore them like other collections without changing the original releases.

## Listen and explore

1. Open an editorial collection from a discovery section or link.
2. Play the selection or choose an individual beat.
3. Open the credited producer to hear more of their catalog.
4. Like a beat or add it to your own playlist to revisit it.

## If you are a producer

BeatPass chooses and manages editorial selections. Your producer dashboard does not offer a way to create an editorial collection or guarantee inclusion.

Your beat can appear in an editorial selection and its original collection at the same time. Its ownership, producer credits, and licensing settings stay attached to the original beat. Keep that original entry accurate instead of uploading a duplicate for editorial use.

Editorial inclusion is discovery exposure. It does not promise a particular number of plays, earnings, or future placements.

## Choose the right collection

| Type | Who shapes it |
| --- | --- |
| Producer collection | The release owner |
| Playlist | You and any accepted collaborators |
| Editorial collection | BeatPass |

See [Playlists](/help/playlists) to create your own listening selection.
