Start BeatPass Radio when one beat, producer, or genre has the sound you want to keep exploring. A station mixes related music from across BeatPass; the station name identifies its starting point, not the owner of every beat.

## Start a station

1. Open a beat, producer page, or genre page.
2. Select **Radio** when that action is available, including in **More** where offered.
3. Start the station's playback and use **Next** to explore the recommendations.
4. Like a beat or follow its producer when you find something worth keeping.

Radio starts from beats, producers, and genres. Playlist radio is not offered.

## Keep listening

The queue can continue with more related tracks while a radio session is active. Recently opened stations help you return to a sound you liked. Recommendations and availability can change, so a station is not a fixed playlist.

## Use a beat in your project

Radio is a listening tool. Check each beat's [available license](/help/downloads-and-licensing) before downloading or releasing your own work.
