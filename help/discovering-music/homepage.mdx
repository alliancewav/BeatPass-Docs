Use BeatPass's discovery home to try a few sounds before choosing your next project. Featured releases, producer collections, genres, and recommendations give you several ways into the music.

<DiscoverFigure />

## Start listening

1. Open [Discover](https://open.beatpass.ca/discover).
2. Choose a beat's **Play** action to hear it, or open a collection to browse its tracks.
3. Use **Like** or **Add to playlist** when a beat is worth returning to.
4. Open the producer's name to hear more of their catalog.

## When the page changes

Featured sections and their order can change. Personal recommendations may not appear until you are signed in and have listening history. Use [Search](/help/discovering-music/search) when you want a specific title or producer instead.

## Take your shortlist with you

Find liked beats and saved collections in [Your library](/help/library). A playlist is useful for comparing beats across several producers without losing your favorites.
