Your next song can start with a sound, a producer, or a beat you already love. Listen, keep a shortlist, and come back to the ones that stay with you.

## Choose a starting point

<Columns cols={2}>
  <Card title="Browse for a sound" icon="compass" href="/help/discovering-music/homepage">
    Explore Discover when you want to see where the music takes you.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Find a name or title" icon="magnifying-glass" href="/help/discovering-music/search">
    Search for a beat, producer, collection, playlist, profile, or tag.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Narrow the mood" icon="sliders" href="/help/discovering-music/genres-and-tags">
    Use genres and tags to explore a direction.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Hear more from a producer" icon="user" href="/help/discovering-music/producer-pages">
    Open their catalog and follow the sound you connected with.
  </Card>
</Columns>

For a full release, open [collections](/help/discovering-music/albums-and-collections). To keep a similar sound playing, try [radio](/help/discovering-music/radio).

## Keep the good finds

<Steps>
  <Step title="Listen">
    Open a beat and press **Play**. [Player controls](/help/playing-music/player-controls) explains the buttons on your screen.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save your shortlist">
    Use **Like**, **Follow**, or **Add to playlist**. Find saved music and producers again in [Your library](/help/library).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the license when you are ready">
    Listening, saving, and sharing do not grant release rights. Use [Downloads and licensing](/help/downloads-and-licensing) before using the audio in your own project.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Accordion title="I do not see the same recommendations">
  Browse sections change as music is added. Recommendations may be absent when you are signed out or have little listening history. You can still [search](/help/discovering-music/search) and browse the catalog.
</Accordion>
