Open a producer's BeatPass page to hear their catalog, explore releases, and decide what to save. Your **Follow** action keeps that producer in your library.

## Listen and follow

1. Open a producer's name from a beat, collection, or search result.
2. Select **Play** to start listening, or open a specific beat from the catalog.
3. Select **Follow** to save the producer. **Following** shows the saved state; select it again to unfollow.
4. Return through **Library** → **Following** when you want to hear more.

## Explore the profile

| Area | What to look for |
| --- | --- |
| **Overview** | Featured, popular, and recent beats, collections, biography, and credits |
| **Beats** and **Collections** | Individual tracks and grouped releases |
| **Gallery** | Images and producer information |
| **Videos** | Recent YouTube uploads and Shorts when a channel is linked |
| **Similar artists** | Related producers when suggestions are available |
| **Followers** | People following that producer |

The available tabs can differ by profile. **Achievements** is an owner/admin view. A verified badge indicates producer verification; an **Unclaimed** label means the page has no linked owner yet.

## Send a message

Use **Message** on a claimed producer page. Sign in if asked, then follow the person's user account when the conversation requests it. Following a producer page and following its owner's user account are separate actions.

Messaging is unavailable for an unclaimed page or your own producer profile. You can still use **More** for other available actions, such as sharing or editing a profile you own.
