Use genres to choose a broad sound on BeatPass, then follow tags for a more specific mood or production style. You can open these links from the music you are already exploring.

## Browse by genre

1. Open a genre from a beat, collection, or discovery section.
2. Browse the beats, releases, and producers shown on that page.
3. Use **Radio** when available to continue with related music.

Genre pages can have different sections and filters. Use the controls you see rather than expecting every genre page to look identical.

## Follow a tag

Select a tag on a beat or collection to explore more music with that label. In [Search](/help/discovering-music/search), the **Tags** tab is another way to find a descriptive label.

| Choose a… | When you want… |
| --- | --- |
| Genre | A broad style, such as hip hop |
| Tag | A narrower mood, instrument, or production description |

Like a beat or add it to a playlist before trying another direction, so you can return to your shortlist.
