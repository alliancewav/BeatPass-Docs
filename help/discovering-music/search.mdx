Find a beat, producer, release, or playlist by name using BeatPass Search. Start broad, then choose the kind of result you want.

## Find a result

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open search">
    Choose **Find** on your phone, use the desktop search control, or [open Search](https://open.beatpass.ca/search).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Try a name or a sound">
    Enter a title, producer name, or descriptive word. Suggestions appear after at least three characters. Select one to open it, or submit the search for full results.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the result you need">
    Use **Tracks**, **Artists**, **Albums**, **Playlists**, **Profiles**, or **Tags** when that tab has results. Open a result to listen or explore it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

**Top results** brings different matching types together. Tabs appear when that type has results. Mobile result pages keep a search field available so you can refine the same search.

## Make a search more useful

- Use a producer's name when you want their catalog.
- Try a track title when you already know the beat.
- Open **Tags** for a narrower descriptive label.
- Use [Genres and tags](/help/discovering-music/genres-and-tags) for broad style browsing; there is no separate genre results tab.

<Accordion title="Nothing matches my search">
  Try a shorter phrase or clear the query. A genre has no separate results tab; use [genre browsing](/help/discovering-music/genres-and-tags) for a broad style. Like a good find or add it to a playlist before moving on.
</Accordion>
