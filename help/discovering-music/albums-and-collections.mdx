Listen through a BeatPass collection when you want a grouped release from one or more producers. The cover, track list, and release details help you decide where to start.

## Play a collection

1. Open the collection from Discover, a producer page, search, or your library.
2. Use **Play** to start its tracks, or choose a specific track from the list.
3. Like the collection to save it for another session.
4. Open a producer or tag link to explore more music.

## Read the release details

The page can show the cover, title, credited producers, track count, duration, release date, description, and tags. Comments appear when they are enabled. An empty collection has no playable track list yet.

## Save a listening list

A producer-managed collection is a release. Use a [playlist](/help/playlists) when you want your own selection or order of beats from several releases.

Some collections are [BeatPass editorial selections](/help/discovering-music/editorial-collections). Being included in one does not change a beat's original credits or licensing terms.
