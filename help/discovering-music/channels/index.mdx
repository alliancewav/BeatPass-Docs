Browse BeatPass by the sound you want to hear. Discovery pages can mix playable beats, releases, producers, and playlists, so you can follow a promising find into a wider catalog.

## Browse a section

1. Open [Discover](https://open.beatpass.ca/discover) or a genre link.
2. Select **Play** on a beat to hear it. Open its title or artwork for more details.
3. Move through a horizontal collection to reveal more items. Open **More** when a section offers it.
4. Use any search or filter controls shown on that page to narrow your choices.

Different pages can show different controls. A missing filter does not mean you have lost access to your library.

## Find a more specific sound

- Open a [genre or tag](/help/discovering-music/genres-and-tags) when the style is right.
- Open a [producer page](/help/discovering-music/producer-pages) when you like who made a beat.
- Try [Radio](/help/discovering-music/radio) for related music that keeps playing.

## Keep your place

Like beats you want to revisit or add them to a playlist. Your saved music stays in [Your library](/help/library), even when discovery sections change.
