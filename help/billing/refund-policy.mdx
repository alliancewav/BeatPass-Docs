This guide summarizes the [official Refund Policy](https://open.beatpass.ca/pages/refund-policy). Most BeatPass charges are final because digital files, access, and licenses can be delivered immediately. Nothing in the policy removes rights that cannot be waived under applicable consumer law.

## Bought an exclusive beat by mistake?

Contact support **within 24 hours of purchase**. BeatPass may consider the accidental-purchase exception only when **all four** conditions are met:

1. You submit the request within **24 hours** of purchase.
2. The purchase was made in error, such as an accidental purchase, duplicate charge, or technical malfunction.
3. You have **not downloaded or accessed any beat files**, including audio, stems, or other deliverables.
4. You have **not used, shared, submitted, or relied on the license certificate**, including with a distributor, label, publisher, PRO, Content ID service, platform, or another party.

<Note>
  Automatic certificate generation alone does not rule out consideration. Using or relying on the certificate does. Approval is not guaranteed, even when all conditions are met.
</Note>

If you are asking for this exception, avoid accessing files or using the certificate while support reviews the request. After 24 hours, the accidental-purchase exception normally no longer applies. Other exceptions, including verified rights issues and non-waivable consumer rights, can still apply.

## Other reasons to ask for a review

BeatPass may consider a refund, credit, replacement, documentation support, or another remedy for:

- **Duplicate charges** caused by a technical error.
- **Failed delivery** when payment succeeded and support cannot resolve the missing files or certificate through re-delivery.
- **Unauthorized transactions**. Contact your card issuer as well as BeatPass.
- **Verified rights issues**, including infringement concerns, AI misrepresentation, missing Proof of Rights, or another producer-side licensing problem that leads BeatPass to remove or restrict a beat.

The 24-hour accidental-purchase test does not replace those separate review grounds. For rights issues, BeatPass considers the facts, use of the beat, available replacements, producer payouts, and any claim. A monetary refund is not guaranteed, and BeatPass cannot grant rights the producer did not own or control.

## Subscriptions

Cancelling stops future renewals. It does not automatically refund the current billing period, unused time, or an earlier charge. You normally keep plan access until the paid period ends. A downgrade follows the timing shown in **Billing**; it does not itself create a refund.

Valid licenses issued before cancellation continue under their terms. Keep your certificates for future release or licensing questions.

## Beat request tokens

Tokens are platform allowances, not cash balances.

| What happened? | What to expect |
| --- | --- |
| You submitted a request | The token is consumed; there is no normal cash refund |
| You deleted an open or closed request | Its original token cost returns only up to your current plan cap |
| Your request expired naturally | No automatic token or cash refund |
| You did not use the monthly allowance | Unused tokens reset with the plan cycle and do not carry over |

See [Understanding Tokens](/help/beat-requests/understanding-tokens) for how your balance works.

## Request a review

<Steps>
  <Step title="Contact support">
    Email [support@beatpass.ca](mailto:support@beatpass.ca?subject=Refund%20Request) with **Refund Request - [Your Name]** as the subject, or put that text at the start of **Message** in the [BeatPass contact form](https://open.beatpass.ca/contact). For an accidental exclusive purchase, send the request within 24 hours.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Include the purchase details">
    Include your account email, transaction date, amount, beat title and producer name or plan, and a clear explanation. Add the certificate or purchase reference if available.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add relevant evidence">
    Use email for screenshots or other attachments; the contact form has no attachment field. Include useful errors or charge details, and redact unrelated transactions, full card or bank details, and unnecessary personal information.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Allow time for review">
    BeatPass aims to respond within **5 business days**, where practicable. A response is a review of your case, not a promise that a refund will be approved.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check an approved refund">
    Approved refunds return to the original payment method and usually take **5 to 10 business days**, depending on the bank or payment processor.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## If a refund is approved

The adjustment may be full or partial. For an exclusive purchase, BeatPass may revoke the license, update its record, make the beat available again where appropriate, and reverse or adjust the related producer payout.

For a full refund, BeatPass **may also refund the platform fee**. Fee treatment for partial refunds, credits, replacements, or other remedies depends on the transaction, processor rules, and remedy. The fee is not automatically refunded in every case.

## Chargebacks and bank disputes

For a duplicate charge or delivery problem, contact support promptly and keep your transaction references. For an unauthorized charge, contact your card issuer immediately as well.

A payment dispute can affect account access or producer payouts while it is investigated. The official policy describes how BeatPass handles chargebacks and fraudulent disputes.
