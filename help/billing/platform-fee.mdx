Review the **platform fee** and full total before paying on BeatPass. It is separate from the plan price or producer's listed beat price, and an eligible active plan may waive it for an exclusive purchase.

## Where the fee applies

- Subscription checkout and paid renewals include the subscription platform fee.
- Exclusive checkout shows either its fee or a qualifying active-plan waiver.
- Standard subscription downloads do not add a separate per-download fee.

An exclusive-purchase benefit does not remove subscription checkout or renewal fees. Use the benefit and amount shown for your account at that checkout.

## Read the total

The fee uses a percentage of the base price plus a flat amount. The percentage, flat amount, purchase currency, and applicable benefits can change, so a past example is not a current quote.

For subscriptions, the summary shows the plan price, any applied coupon, fee, and total. A coupon reduces the percentage calculation against the discounted base; the flat fee remains. For exclusive purchases, check the track price, fee or waiver, and total before continuing.

If the amount changes before payment, review the updated quote again rather than relying on an earlier screen.

## Check your receipt later

Open [Billing → Payment history](https://open.beatpass.ca/billing) and use **View Invoice** for the payment. The record reflects the amount charged for that transaction; a later plan change does not rewrite an earlier exclusive receipt.

A full refund may include a fee refund, but fee treatment is not automatic for every adjustment. See [Refund Policy](/help/billing/refund-policy).
