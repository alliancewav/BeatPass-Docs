BeatPass currently calculates a `12% + $3.00 flat` platform fee for eligible paid checkouts. Subscription checkout and renewal use this fee for every paid plan. Exclusive license checkout waives it when the buyer has an active plan with that benefit, currently Pro.

## Where the fee applies

The current fee applies to:

- Subscription checkout and renewal charges
- Exclusive track purchases, unless the buyer's active plan waives the fee

<Note>
  Standard subscription downloads do not trigger a separate per-download platform fee. Pro's exclusive-purchase waiver does not remove the fee from Pro subscription checkout or renewal.
</Note>

## How the calculation works

BeatPass calculates the fee as **(base price × 12%) + $3.00**.

### Example

| Base price | Fee | Total |
| --- | --- | --- |
| $20.00 | $5.40 | $25.40 |
| $50.00 | $9.00 | $59.00 |
| $100.00 | $15.00 | $115.00 |

For an exclusive purchase where the fee applies, the base price is the producer's listed track price. For subscriptions, the base price is the plan price before the platform fee is added.

## What buyers see

### Subscription checkout

The shared checkout summary currently shows:

- Plan price
- Coupon discount, if one is applied
- Platform fee
- Total due today

If a coupon is applied to a subscription, BeatPass reduces the percentage portion against the discounted base price. The flat `$3.00` portion still remains.

### Exclusive track checkout

The exclusive purchase dialog currently shows:

- Track price
- Platform fee with the current percentage and flat-fee formula, or a waived message when an active plan benefit applies
- Total amount in the selected purchase currency

The fee decision is checked again when payment starts. That final checkout amount is stored with the purchase so a later plan change does not rewrite the receipt.

## How invoices and payment history relate

- Subscription invoices can include the platform fee as its own line item
- Payment history gives you the invoice entry and a link to the invoice view when that action is available
- For exclusive purchases, the printable invoice uses the fee stored with that payment. Fee-exempt purchases show the producer price as the total and do not add a platform-fee line.

## Why the fee is added on top

BeatPass keeps the base subscription price or producer's listed track price separate from the platform fee. The producer amount is not reduced, including when an eligible subscriber's exclusive-purchase fee is waived.

## Common questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Is the current fee fixed?" icon="percent" defaultOpen>
    The live BeatPass setting is currently `12% + $3.00 flat`. If BeatPass changes that setting later, trust the amount shown in checkout at the time you pay.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does the fee show before I pay?" icon="receipt">
    Yes. Subscription checkout shows the fee before payment. Exclusive checkout shows either the fee or the active plan waiver before payment.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does the fee come out of the producer's listed track price?" icon="dollar-sign">
    No. The exclusive purchase flow keeps the producer amount equal to the listed track price. The buyer pays the fee when it applies; an eligible active plan such as Pro can waive it.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does Pro remove the subscription platform fee?" icon="crown">
    No. Pro waives the platform fee only on eligible one-time exclusive license purchases. The Pro subscription checkout and renewals still include the subscription platform fee.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
