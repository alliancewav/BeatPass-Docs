Use **Payment method** on BeatPass Billing to update the card used for subscription charges. When you first start a paid plan, you normally enter a payment method during checkout.

## Update a saved card

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Billing">
    Open your profile avatar menu and select **Billing**, or go directly to [Billing](https://open.beatpass.ca/billing).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Update">
    In **Payment method**, check the displayed card brand and last four digits, then select **Update**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Finish with the payment form">
    Complete the available update form and follow any authentication requested by the payment provider. Return to Billing to check the saved method.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The panel may display the expiry date when available. Your full card number is not shown. If your subscription uses a different payment provider, follow the provider action offered in that panel.

## No saved method is shown

Without a paid subscription, Billing can show **View Plans** rather than a saved-card panel. You do not need to buy a plan simply to browse or listen. Add payment information when starting a purchase you intend to make.

Only use payment options offered for the current checkout. A saved subscription card does not mean every one-time purchase uses the same payment flow.

## A payment failed

Check the card expiry and the provider's error. If the bank declined the payment, contact the card issuer or try another offered method. After updating, check your subscription status in Billing before assuming access has returned.

For a charge you do not recognize, contact your card issuer and [BeatPass support](/help/contact-support). Never send support full card or bank details.
