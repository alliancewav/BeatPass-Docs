Choose a higher-priced plan when its extra features suit what you want to do next. Check the full price and when the change will begin before confirming.


## How to Upgrade

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Billing Page">
    Click your **profile avatar** in the top navigation bar, then select **Billing** from the menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Change Plan">
    In the Current plan section, click the **Change plan** button.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Your New Plan">
    Browse available plans and select the one you want to upgrade to.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose when it should apply">
    When **Apply immediately** is available, select it for an immediate change. Leave it unselected to use the renewal date shown on the page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review your agreement">
    Enter the name and address for your private agreement, then choose **Review agreement**. Read the full French agreement before choosing your agreement language. Check **Your payment adjustment** for the amount due now, any adjustment, and the next payment.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm Upgrade">
    Accept the reviewed agreement, then click **Confirm Plan Change**. Complete any PayPal approval or payment confirmation shown.
  </Step>
</Steps>


## What Happens When You Upgrade

### If You Apply It Immediately

- Your features change after the payment provider confirms the change. A pending response means the change still needs confirmation.
- **Due now** can be zero even when a **Plan adjustment** is included in the next invoice. Read both amounts.
- **Next payment** shows the reviewed amount and date. **Estimated next payment** is an estimate.
- PayPal changes need your approval in PayPal. The new price starts at the next billing cycle; feature timing follows the reviewed offer.

### If You Apply It On Renewal Date

- BeatPass keeps your current plan active until your renewal date
- The new plan takes over on that date
- No immediate upgrade charge is required


## If an immediate change is unavailable

If BeatPass does not have a usable payment method for an immediate charge, the confirmation page schedules the upgrade for your renewal date instead.


## Compare the plans

Check the available prices, billing intervals, and request allowances on the [pricing page](https://open.beatpass.ca/pricing). Read the full total and effective date on the confirmation screen before changing your plan.

A paid plan does not unlock new standard catalog licenses for producer accounts. Producer access itself remains free.


## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="My upgrade failed">
    Check that your payment method is valid and has sufficient funds. The card may have been declined due to:
    - Insufficient balance
    - Expired card
    - Card issuer security block
    
    If the page says the change is awaiting confirmation, use **Check status** before trying again. Update your payment method when the provider reports a failed payment.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I don't see the features after upgrading">
    Check the effective date first: a scheduled change starts at renewal. If an immediate upgrade succeeded but Billing still shows the old plan, contact support with the payment reference.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I was charged more than expected">
    Compare **Due now**, **Plan adjustment**, and the next payment in the offer you reviewed. They can refer to different billing dates. Keep your agreement copy and invoice; contact support if the recorded amounts differ.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I only see the renewal-date option">
    That usually means BeatPass cannot complete an immediate charge right now, so the upgrade is being scheduled instead.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
