Cancelling your subscription stops the next renewal while keeping the current paid period active until its end date.


## How to Cancel

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Billing Page">
    Click your **profile avatar** in the top navigation bar, then select **Billing** from the menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Cancel Plan">
    In the Current plan section, click the **Cancel plan** button.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review What Happens">
    The cancellation page shows your **Access until** date and confirms there is **No immediate charge or refund**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm Cancellation">
    Click **Cancel plan** to complete the cancellation.
  </Step>
</Steps>


## Check your access end date

After cancellation, **Current plan** shows **Access ending**, the **Access ends** date, and **Renew plan**. You keep your paid features until that date; future renewals stop. A charge that already processed is separate.

Cancellation itself creates **No immediate charge or refund**. Valid licenses issued earlier continue under their original terms. For a charge review, see [Refund Policy](/help/billing/refund-policy).

## Changed your mind?

Before access ends, select **Renew plan**, then **Confirm Renewal**. There is no immediate payment; your original renewal schedule resumes. See [Resuming Subscription](/help/billing/managing-subscription/resuming-subscription).

If you want a lower price while keeping a paid plan, you can [downgrade](/help/billing/managing-subscription/downgrading-plan) instead.

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="I cancelled but was still charged">
    Check the charge date versus your cancellation date. If you cancelled after the billing date, the charge may have already processed. Contact support if you believe there's an error.
  </Accordion>
  
  <Accordion title="I can't find the cancel option">
    Click your **profile avatar** → **Billing**. The **Cancel plan** button appears in the Current plan section. If you don't see it, make sure you're logged into the correct account and have an active subscription.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I undo the cancellation?">
    Yes. Before the end date shown on Billing, click **Renew plan** and then **Confirm Renewal**.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
