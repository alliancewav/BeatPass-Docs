The **Payment history** section on Billing shows your past payment records, including subscription payments and track purchases.


## Find your payment history

To access your invoice history:

1. Click your **profile avatar** in the top navigation bar
2. Select **Billing** from the menu
3. Scroll down to the **Payment history** section
4. Browse your payment history


## Read a payment record

**Payment history** includes subscription payments and individual track purchases. Each entry shows its **Date**, **Amount**, **Description**, and payment status: **Paid**, **Unpaid**, **Refunded**, or **Partially refunded**.

Use the plan or track description to identify the charge. The record belongs to the account that made the payment.

## Open and save an invoice

1. Find the payment in **Payment history**. Select **Load more** if you need an older record.
2. When available, select the open-in-new icon (**View Invoice**) next to it.
3. The invoice opens in a new tab. Use your browser's print or save tools to keep a copy for expenses, tax, or business records.

## Keep your agreement copy

An invoice records a payment. Your agreement copy records the offer and terms you accepted.

After accepting a new subscription or paid plan change, use **Download your agreement** when offered. BeatPass also attempts to email it. If the email is unavailable, download the copy from the page and contact support if you need help. You can save or print the downloaded HTML file in your browser.

A saved offer or agreement copy does not mean a payment has succeeded. Check the payment confirmation and **Payment history** separately. Existing licenses keep their accepted terms.

## Check the amount

### For Subscriptions

When you open a subscription invoice, BeatPass can show separate line items for your plan and the platform fee shown for that transaction.

### For Beat Purchases

When you open a track-purchase invoice, it uses the amounts captured for that payment. If a platform fee was charged, it appears separately. If an active plan benefit waived the fee, the track price equals the total and no fee line is added.


## If a payment is unpaid

If a payment failed:

- The list shows **Unpaid**
- Your subscription may not renew until resolved
- Update your payment method and try again


## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="I can't find an invoice">
    Check that you're logged into the correct account. Invoices are only visible to the account that made the payment. If you recently made a purchase, it may take a few moments to appear.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The amount doesn't match what I expected">
    For subscriptions, remember the total charge includes the platform fee on top of the plan price. For exclusive purchases, compare the stored track price, any fee shown, and the total. An eligible active-plan benefit can waive an exclusive-purchase fee; use the amounts recorded for that purchase.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I need an invoice for tax purposes">
    Open the invoice in a new tab when the open icon is available, then save or print it from your browser.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I get a refund for an invoice?">
    Refund policies vary. Contact support if you believe you're entitled to a refund. See the [refund policy](/help/billing/refund-policy) for more details.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
