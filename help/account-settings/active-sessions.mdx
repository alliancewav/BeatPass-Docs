Check **Active sessions** when you want to sign out an old browser or review account access. Open [Account Settings](https://open.beatpass.ca/account-settings#sessions) to see your recent sessions.

## Sign out other browsers

<Steps>
  <Step title="Review the list">
    In **Active sessions**, look for your browser and platform. **This device** marks the session you are using now.
  </Step>
  <Step title="End other sessions">
    Select **Logout other sessions** and enter your current password when prompted. Your current browser stays signed in.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the result">
    Wait for **Logged out other sessions.** If an error appears, retry before assuming the other sessions have ended.
  </Step>
</Steps>

This action ends other browser sessions. [API tokens](/help/account-settings/developer-api) are managed separately. If you use Google and do not know your password, [reset or create it by email](/help/account-settings/password/forgot-password) first.

## Understand the list

Each row can show browser, platform, approximate city and country, IP address, and last activity. A mobile network or VPN can make the location look unfamiliar. Missing details may appear as **Unknown**.

BeatPass displays up to **30 recent sessions**. A session falling outside that list does not by itself prove it was deleted or signed out.

## Something looks unfamiliar

Sign out other browsers, [change your password](/help/account-settings/password/changing-password), and review your account details. Consider [two-factor authentication](/help/account-settings/two-factor-authentication). If access or account details changed without your permission, [contact support](/help/contact-support).
