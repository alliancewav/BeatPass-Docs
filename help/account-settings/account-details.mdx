Use **Account details** to update your avatar and check the legal name used on certificates and producer agreements. The panel is titled **Update name and profile image**. Your legal name does not change your public handle. Open [Account details](https://open.beatpass.ca/account-settings#account-details) to see the action available to your account.

<AccountFigure />

## Update your avatar or name

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open account settings">
    Open the **Account Settings** page from your profile avatar menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go to Account details">
    Open the **Account details** section.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Edit your fields">
    Update **First name** and **Last name** if they are editable. For your avatar, use the **Profile image** control.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save your changes">
    Click **Save** for editable name changes. Your profile-image control handles its own upload. If the name fields are locked, follow the name guidance below.
  </Step>
</Steps>


<Tip>
  A clear square image usually works best for avatars.
</Tip>


## Complete your name for licensing

### Customers

 use your own legal first and last name as shown on official identification. Choose **Review legal name**, or open [Library → My Licenses](https://open.beatpass.ca/library/licenses). The page is titled **Your licenses**. Review the name carefully before selecting **Confirm and lock name**. Later corrections require support. Eligible certificates complete automatically once the required identities are saved. Follow any remaining producer-credit review or support action shown on the card.

### Producers

Your legal name synchronizes from your connected payout details. Open [Finances](https://open.beatpass.ca/producer-dashboard/finances) and follow **Payout setup** if setup or review is required. Once the payout identity is ready, the synchronized name is locked. A complete producer identity is needed for the certificates you grant and before signing an exclusive offer.

A locked name may come from customer confirmation or payout synchronization. [Contact support](/help/contact-support) if it needs correction. Existing signed certificates retain their original names and terms. If a legal-name check is unavailable, choose **Try again**; do not assume that locked fields mean your account details were deleted.

## Looking for another field?

**Account details** does not include username, email, website, or social-link fields. Use [Password](/help/account-settings/password) for password changes.


## Common Questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="My avatar isn't updating">
    The profile-image control uploads independently of the name form. Wait for the upload to finish, then refresh if the old image still appears. If an error appears, try the image again.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do I need to fill out both name fields?">
    Both legal names must be complete and confirmed through the action shown for your account. Customers confirm in **Your licenses**; producers use their payout identity. Complete your own step first. A certificate waiting on another party stays locked until their required details are complete. A missing identity does not itself revoke the original license grant.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Where do I change my password?">
    Password changes are handled in the [Password](/help/account-settings/password) section, not in **Account details**.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
