Use **Social login** to connect an available sign-in provider to your BeatPass account. Only enabled providers appear; use the options shown in your account rather than assuming a provider is supported.

## Connect a provider

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Social login">
    Open [Account Settings](https://open.beatpass.ca/account-settings#social-login), then select **Social login**. The panel is titled **Manage social login**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enable the account">
    Select **Enable** beside the provider, such as **Google account**. Complete the provider's sign-in window.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the connection">
    The row shows the linked account instead of **Disabled** when the connection succeeds. If the popup is blocked, allow popups for BeatPass and retry.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If an account already exists with the same email, BeatPass may ask for its existing password before linking the provider. Sign in to that account first if needed.

## Disconnect safely

Make sure you can sign in another way before selecting **Disable**. If you have only used Google, [create a password through email recovery](/help/account-settings/password/forgot-password) first and check that it works.

Disabling the connection changes your BeatPass sign-in method; it does not delete your Google account. You can also review connected-app access in the provider's own account settings.

## What information is used

The provider can share account information such as your email, name, and profile image for sign-in. Your provider password is not entered into BeatPass. Review the [Privacy Policy](/help/legal/privacy-policy) for account information handling.
