Set your language, country, and timezone in BeatPass so the interface and dates fit how you work. In [Account Settings](https://open.beatpass.ca/account-settings#location-and-language), select **Location and language**. The panel is titled **Date, time and language**.

## Save your preferences

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose your language">
    Select an available **Language**. User-created titles, descriptions, and messages are not automatically translated.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check your region and time">
    Select **Country** and **Timezone**. Use the country search if needed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save and check">
    Select **Save**. If dates still look wrong, refresh and check the saved timezone. Some interface text may remain in English when a translation is unavailable.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Your saved country can prefill **Governing law** when you create new exclusive terms. Review that field before publishing. Changing your country does not rewrite saved terms on an existing beat.

For a missing language or a preference that will not save, [contact support](/help/contact-support) with the language or timezone and what you see.
