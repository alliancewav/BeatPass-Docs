Deleting your BeatPass account is permanent. Once the deletion is confirmed, your current user account is removed right away and you are signed out.

---

## Before You Delete

### Consider These Points

Before deleting your account, think about:

- **Active subscription** — Deleting the account also removes the subscription attached to it
- **Saved access** — Your login sessions and connected sign-in methods are removed with the account
- **Files and settings** — Account-owned data tied directly to your user is deleted

<Warning>
  Account deletion is **permanent and irreversible**.
</Warning>

---

## What BeatPass Removes

The current account deletion flow removes your user account and related items tied directly to it, including:

| Data Type | What Happens |
|-----------|--------------|
| **User account** | Deleted permanently |
| **Linked social accounts** | Removed |
| **Notifications** | Removed |
| **API access tokens** | Removed |
| **Active sessions** | Ended |
| **Account-owned files and exports** | Removed |
| **Subscription records tied to the account** | Cancelled and deleted |

---

## Records That May Be Retained

Deleting your login does not erase every commercial or compliance record. BeatPass may retain or de-identify payout, transaction, license, accounting, tax, fraud-prevention, dispute, and audit records where they are needed to support existing licenses, complete or document payments, comply with law, or protect the platform. Stripe and Wise may also retain information under their own legal and privacy obligations.

These retained records are not a recoverable BeatPass user account and do not allow you to sign back in. For a privacy request about retained information, contact the BeatPass Privacy Officer through [contact@beatpass.ca](mailto:contact@beatpass.ca). See the [official Privacy Policy](https://open.beatpass.ca/pages/privacy-policy).

## What Happens to Subscriptions

If you have an active subscription:

- It is cancelled as part of account deletion
- Future renewals stop with the deleted account

<Note>
  If you just want to cancel your subscription without deleting your account, you can do so from the [Billing page](/help/billing).
</Note>

## How to Delete Your Account

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Account Settings">
    Open **Account Settings** from the user menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find the Danger Zone">
    Scroll to the bottom to find the **Danger zone** section.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start deletion">
    Click **Delete account**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm Your Decision">
    Review the confirmation dialog. It states: *"Your account will be deleted immediately and permanently. Once deleted, accounts can not be restored."*
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete Deletion">
    Click **Delete**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

---

## After Deletion

### Immediate Effects

- You're logged out immediately
- You cannot log back in with those credentials
- Your existing sessions are ended

---

## Alternatives to Deletion

If you're not sure about permanent deletion, consider these alternatives:

### Cancel subscription only

If cost is the issue:
- Cancel your subscription but keep the account
- Keep access to your account settings and history

### Keep the account and secure it instead

- Change your password
- Enable two-factor authentication
- Use **Logout other sessions** from [Active Sessions](/help/account-settings/active-sessions)

---

## Re-registering After Deletion

If you delete your account and later want to return:

- You can create a **new account**
- Your old account data **cannot be restored**
- You start fresh as a new user

<Info>
  Deleted accounts cannot be recovered. If you think you might want to return, consider keeping your account rather than deleting it.
</Info>

## Common Questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can I recover my account after deletion?">
    No. Account deletion is permanent and irreversible. Once deleted, your user account cannot be recovered. Some commercial, licensing, payout, accounting, tax, fraud-prevention, and audit records may be retained as described above.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I delete my account without canceling my subscription first?">
    Yes. Deleting your account automatically cancels any active subscriptions.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How long does deletion take?">
    The current deletion flow removes the account immediately after you confirm it.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
