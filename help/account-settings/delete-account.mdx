Delete your BeatPass account only when you are ready to give up access to it. Confirmation removes the account immediately and signs you out. The deleted account cannot be restored.

## Before deleting

Save any invoices, certificates, or other records you may need. If you only want to stop renewal charges, [cancel the subscription](/help/billing/managing-subscription/cancelling-subscription) and keep the account instead.

Account deletion cancels active subscriptions as part of removing the account. It does not automatically refund an earlier charge. Producers with catalog, license, or payout questions should [contact support](/help/contact-support) before deleting so they can understand what happens to their records and ongoing obligations.

## Delete the account

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Delete account">
    Open [Account Settings](https://open.beatpass.ca/account-settings#delete-account) and select **Delete account** in the page sections. The panel is titled **Danger zone**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the confirmation">
    Select **Delete account**. The dialog explains that deletion is immediate, permanent, and cannot be restored.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm only when ready">
    Select **Delete** to remove the account. You are signed out. If the action fails, keep the error message and contact support; do not assume deletion completed.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Records that may remain

Deleting your login does not erase every licensing, payment, or compliance record. BeatPass may retain or de-identify records needed for existing licenses, payouts, accounting, tax, fraud prevention, disputes, security, or legal duties. Stripe and Wise control their own retention too.

These records do not restore the deleted account. For a request about retained information, contact the [BeatPass Privacy Officer](/help/legal/privacy-governance). The [official Privacy Policy](https://open.beatpass.ca/pages/privacy-policy) explains retention and deletion limits.

If you return later, you need a new account; your old account is not recovered by registering again.
