Use **Password** in BeatPass Account Settings to update a password you know, or email recovery to reset one you have forgotten. Google-only accounts can use email recovery to create a password too.

## Choose your next step

New passwords need at least **8 characters** and matching confirmation. Choose a unique password, and keep it in a password manager if you use one.

## Protect your sign-in

Change a password you believe was exposed. Review [Active sessions](/help/account-settings/active-sessions) and consider [two-factor authentication](/help/account-settings/two-factor-authentication). Keep access to your account email and save recovery codes somewhere you can reach if you lose your phone.

Never send your password or a reset link to another person, including someone claiming to offer support.
