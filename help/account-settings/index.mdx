## Open account settings

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your account">
    Use your **profile avatar** menu, or [open Account Settings](https://open.beatpass.ca/account-settings) directly.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the section you need">
    Scroll to the relevant section. Some options, such as **Social login**, depend on what is available to your account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save and check">
    Follow that section's save or confirmation action before leaving. If a field is locked, use its guidance rather than creating a second account.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Keep your account secure

Use a unique password, enable two-factor authentication, and keep access to your sign-in email. New passwords need at least eight characters; a longer password you do not use elsewhere is a better choice.

Keep recovery codes somewhere you can reach if you lose your phone. Review [active sessions](/help/account-settings/active-sessions) if a device looks unfamiliar.

Email changes are not offered in the standard account settings form. Confirmed or payout-synchronized legal names may be locked. Follow [Account details](/help/account-settings/account-details) if a name needs attention.

## Need Help?

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="I cannot sign in">
    Use [password recovery](/help/account-settings/password/forgot-password) to reset or create a password by email.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="My settings look different">
    Some sections only appear for eligible accounts. **Social login** lists enabled providers. Integration access is explained separately in [API & Developers](/developers/overview).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Someone else may be using my account">
    [Sign out other sessions](/help/account-settings/active-sessions), change your password, and [contact support](/help/contact-support) if you need help regaining control.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
