Found the sound for your next song? Check the beat's license, request the audio, and keep its certificate with your release records. A download and an exclusive purchase are different paths.

  <Card title="Standard download" icon="download" href="/help/downloads-and-licensing/how-downloads-work">
    Use eligible customer download access for a standard non-exclusive license tied to one project.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Exclusive purchase" icon="crown" href="/help/downloads-and-licensing/exclusive-licenses/purchasing-exclusive">
    Review that beat's offer and complete a separate one-time card checkout when available.

<Note>
  Producer access is free, but current producer accounts cannot acquire new standard licenses or make new exclusive catalog purchases for other producers' beats, including with a paid plan. Producers can retrieve their own audio and audio covered by an existing valid certificate. See [How Downloads Work](/help/downloads-and-licensing/how-downloads-work) for the access rules.
</Note>

## Check the terms for your license

This page is a guide. Read the [Terms of Service](https://open.beatpass.ca/pages/terms-of-service) and the applicable [Non-Exclusive License Agreement](https://open.beatpass.ca/pages/non-exclusive-license-agreement) or [Exclusive License Agreement](https://open.beatpass.ca/pages/exclusive-license-agreement), together with your transaction terms and certificate. The Terms control conflicts unless a signed written license says otherwise.

The standard non-exclusive agreement covers one project, with limits of **100,000 physical or digital units**, **500,000 streams**, and **one music video no longer than five minutes**. Follow the caps and parameters attached to your download. Performance royalties and synchronization income default to **50% for the producer and 50% for the licensee**, unless the transaction terms, certificate, or a separate agreement provide otherwise. An income split does not itself grant permission for a sync use.

Use the terms that apply to your issued license. This guide does not change earlier grants. Exclusive permissions and deliverables depend on the specific purchase.


<Info>
  An eligible download can generate a license certificate automatically. Repeat downloads may use your existing valid certificate. Find your license on the track page or in **Your licenses**.
</Info>



## Fast comparison

| Topic | Non-exclusive | Exclusive |
| --- | --- | --- |
| How you get it | Download an eligible track | Select **Buy Exclusive** on an eligible track |
| Other artists can still use the beat | Yes | No new licenses to new buyers; earlier valid non-exclusive licenses continue under their terms |
| Attribution required | Yes | Check the certificate terms |
| Content ID registration | No | Only if the purchase terms allow it |
| Large live performances | No, capped at 50 attendees | Only if the purchase terms allow it |
| Film/TV/broadcast/commercial/video games | Not allowed | Only if the purchase terms allow it |
| Transferable | No | Not by default |

<Note>
  Read the exclusive preview and certificate with the checkout terms, governing Terms, applicable agreement and any signed addendum. Rights and deliverables depend on the particular purchase.
</Note>

## From beat to licensed audio

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose a track">
    On the track page, use **Download** for standard access or **Buy Exclusive** when an instant exclusive offer is available.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check your access">
    New standard licenses require eligible download access. A later exclusive sale stops new licenses for new buyers; earlier valid non-exclusive grants continue under their terms. Re-download access is checked separately, and an existing valid certificate may permit retrieval. Contact support if you hold a valid license but cannot retrieve the file.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the result">
    **Download requested** means your browser has been asked to save the audio. Check its download list, then open **My licenses** in the dialog to reach **Your licenses**. A qualifying download creates or retrieves the applicable certificate; repeating it does not grant a new project. Exclusive purchases use separate secure card checkout.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find your license">
    Open [Library → My Licenses](https://open.beatpass.ca/library/licenses), titled **Your licenses**, and follow any identity action before opening the certificate.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify when needed">
    Every license can be checked on the public verification page using its unique link.
  </Step>
</Steps>

An exclusive purchase prepares the original uploaded WAV for download. Other promised files are defined by that offer's preview and certificate; the producer delivers them separately.

For provenance, expand **BeatPass ID** on the track. It brings together licensing, Sample-Safe status, available AI disclosure, promised files, and fingerprint information. A missing current track fingerprint is separate from a problem with a stored certificate. If a certificate cannot open or print because its record is incomplete or fails its checks, contact support.
