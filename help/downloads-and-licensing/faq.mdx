Find a quick answer about BeatPass download access, your certificate, or the rights needed for your release. Each answer links to the workflow when you need more detail.

## Access and downloads

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What do I need to download a track?" icon="download" defaultOpen>
    New standard licenses require a signed-in customer account with an active paid plan that includes downloads, current payments, and an eligible beat. Current producer accounts cannot acquire new standard catalog licenses even with a paid plan. Owner audio and existing valid certificates are checked separately.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I download my own tracks?" icon="user">
    Yes. Track owners can download their own tracks without needing a standard generated license certificate for that step.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why is the track blocked even though I can see it?" icon="triangle-exclamation">
    Check the action shown: the beat may no longer offer new standard licenses, your account may not be eligible, payment may need attention, or certificate identity may be incomplete. An existing valid license can still allow retrieval after a later exclusive sale. See [How Downloads Work](/help/downloads-and-licensing/how-downloads-work).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What file do I get from a standard download?" icon="file-audio">
    You receive the attached downloadable audio, rather than a guaranteed WAV/MP3 bundle or stems. **Download requested** confirms the browser save request. Check your device for the file and **Your licenses** for an issued or reused certificate.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Certificates and verification

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Where do I find my certificates?" icon="file-lines" defaultOpen>
    Go to **Your licenses**. You can search by track, artist, licensee, or license ID.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why can't I find my certificate after downloading?" icon="fingerprint">
    Open **Your licenses** and follow any action for your legal name, producer payout identity, or the certificate record. If it is waiting for another party, they must complete their step. Contact support if the record is still missing or asks for review.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I verify whether a certificate is real?" icon="shield-halved">
    Yes. Every certificate includes a public verification link that anyone can use to check its current recorded status. An incomplete or invalid result needs attention before you share it as validated proof.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What does the public verification page show?" icon="circle-info">
    It shows the result, beat, certificate ID, license type, masked licensed party, issue date, expiry when applicable, and current status. See [verification results](/help/downloads-and-licensing/verifying-licenses) for what each result means.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## License rules

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What does a standard non-exclusive license let me do?" icon="users" defaultOpen>
    The current standard covers one original musical work, up to 100,000 distributed units, 500,000 streams, one music video up to five minutes, and live events up to 50 attendees. Producer credit and income-sharing terms apply. Read [Standard license terms](/help/legal/license-terms/non-exclusive) and your issued certificate; earlier grants keep their applicable terms.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What is not allowed under the current standard non-exclusive terms?" icon="circle-xmark">
    You cannot release the beat by itself, use it in film, TV, broadcast radio, commercials, or video games, perform at larger events, resell or share the beat, or register it in Content ID.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do I have to credit the producer?" icon="signature">
    Yes for the current standard non-exclusive license. The certificate wording uses **Produced by [Producer Name]**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What does perpetual mean?" icon="clock">
    The grant has no automatic time limit, subject to its applicable terms, breach provisions, and valid underlying rights. A technical certificate or identity problem does not itself establish that the legal grant expired.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Exclusive purchases

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What happens when I buy exclusive?" icon="crown" defaultOpen>
    BeatPass completes the secure payment, generates the exclusive license, marks the track as sold, prepares the original WAV download, and stores the license in **Your licenses**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I still buy exclusive if other users previously downloaded the beat?" icon="users">
    An eligible customer account can buy an available exclusive offer even when other people hold earlier licenses. Those valid licenses remain in force; the exclusive sale stops new standard licenses. In the current app, producer accounts cannot start new exclusive catalog purchases, even with a paid plan. Their own files and existing valid licenses remain subject to the separate retrieval rules.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What is included with exclusive?" icon="file-lines">
    The exact deliverables come from the license preview and the final certificate for that track. Do not rely on older generic promises about automatic file bundles.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I transfer an exclusive license to someone else?" icon="arrows-left-right">
    Check your purchase terms. Transfer is not included in the default configuration and may require a separate written agreement.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Producers and privacy

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can producers see who licensed or downloaded their tracks?" icon="music" defaultOpen>
    Producers can review license and download-related activity through their tools, including granted licenses shown in **Your licenses** for owned tracks.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does the public verification page expose my full email?" icon="user-secret">
    No. The public page shows a masked email only.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
