Open a certificate's public verification link to check the current BeatPass record. You do not need to sign in to check it. Use the result together with the full license document, which defines the rights and obligations for the release.

<LicenseVerificationFigure />

## Check a certificate

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the complete verification link">
    Use the link supplied on the certificate, or open a card's **Details** in [Your licenses](https://open.beatpass.ca/library/licenses) and select **Verify**. Keep the complete certificate ID in the link.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Compare the record">
    On **Verify a BeatPass license**, compare the **Beat**, **License type**, masked **Licensed party**, **Issued on**, and **Certificate ID** with the document you received.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the result">
    **License record confirmed** with **Active license** means the current record passed the check. Review the full license terms before deciding whether a specific use is covered.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The page can also show **Expiry**, **License status**, and **View licenses**. Public verification does not disclose the holder's full account email or legal name.

## If the result needs attention

| Result | Next step |
| --- | --- |
| **Certificate identity is incomplete** | The holder or producer needs to complete account details or a review. Eligible certificates complete automatically when missing details are saved. This result does not itself revoke the original grant. |
| **Original certificate preserved** | A new license was issued separately. Use **Check the new certificate** when offered to inspect its current status and terms. The original remains history. |
| **License is not currently valid** | The record does not currently confirm active permission. Contact the holder or BeatPass support with its ID. |
| **No matching license record** | Check the full link or ID with the person who supplied it. A missing record does not confirm permission. |
| **Too many verification attempts** | Wait a few minutes before checking again. |
| **Verification could not be completed** | Select **Try again**. A failed check is not a completed verification. |

## Share proof for a release

Send the verification link, certificate document, beat title, release or project name, and any claim reference to the person reviewing your release. A screenshot alone cannot establish the current status.

A confirmed record does not guarantee sample clearance, AI-free audio, acceptance by a distributor, or the absence of future rights claims. If details look wrong or a review remains unresolved, [contact support](/help/contact-support). See [license certificates](/help/downloads-and-licensing/license-certificates) for legal-name and download help.
