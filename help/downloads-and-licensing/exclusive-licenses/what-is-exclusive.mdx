An exclusive BeatPass purchase stops future licensing of that beat to new buyers. It does not erase valid non-exclusive licenses issued before the sale, and it does not transfer ownership beyond the rights in the purchase terms.

## Decide whether the offer fits your release

Open the track's **Preview exact license terms**, or **View License Terms** in checkout. Read:

- The release, streaming, live performance, broadcast, sync, and Content ID permissions you need.
- The producer/buyer shares, any royalty obligation, territory, and transfer restriction.
- The files promised and who delivers them.

Exclusive rights are configured for the individual beat. The label **Exclusive** alone does not promise unlimited use or a standard set of percentages and files.

## What stays the same

The producer keeps ownership except for the rights expressly granted. Earlier valid licensees can continue under their own terms. You cannot relist the raw beat as your product, and AI training, datasets, sample packs, and competing libraries require separate written permission.

Keep the final certificate and read it with the [Exclusive License Agreement](/help/legal/exclusive-license-agreement), [AI and Rights Addendum](/help/legal/exclusive-license-ai-and-rights-addendum), and any signed agreement for the purchase. This guide does not change an earlier grant.

## Next step

Use [Purchasing exclusive](/help/downloads-and-licensing/exclusive-licenses/purchasing-exclusive) when you are ready. For private offers or rights not shown in the preview, speak with the producer before paying. A rights issue is not solved merely by buying the beat exclusively.
