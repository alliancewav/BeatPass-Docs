Choose an exclusive license when the beat's offer includes the rights you need and you want BeatPass to stop issuing new licenses to future buyers. Exclusive purchases use separate one-time checkout; they are not included in subscription downloads.

Producer accounts cannot start new catalog purchases, including exclusive licenses. Existing valid licenses and purchases remain separate from this restriction.

On an eligible beat, use **Preview exact license terms** or **View License Terms** in checkout. Check the rights, producer/buyer shares and royalty obligations, file list, currency, and full total. Benefits or fees shown for an active plan apply to the offer currently being checked.

## After a successful purchase

BeatPass records the purchase, issues its exclusive certificate, and stops new licensing of the beat. Earlier valid non-exclusive licenses remain in force under their terms.

The success flow prepares the original uploaded WAV for download. Check your browser's download list to confirm saving completed. The producer separately delivers stems or other promised files; use the offer's file list rather than assuming a bundle.

Find the record in [Your licenses](https://open.beatpass.ca/library/licenses). Read the certificate with the governing agreements before using rights such as Content ID, sync, broadcast, or large-event performance.

## If something goes wrong

For a missing file or certificate, keep the purchase reference and [contact support](/help/contact-support). For an accidental purchase, the [Refund Policy](/help/billing/refund-policy) has a **24-hour** exception requiring unused files and an unused certificate. Rights issues have a separate review path.

Exclusive licensing does not include AI training or dataset rights without separate written permission. Read the [Exclusive AI and Rights Addendum](/help/legal/exclusive-license-ai-and-rights-addendum) for those boundaries.
