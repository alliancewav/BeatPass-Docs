Buy an exclusive BeatPass license by reviewing the exact offer, confirming your legal name, and completing one-time card checkout. Keep the purchase record with your release files afterwards.

<Info>
  Exclusive purchases use secure card checkout. Supported purchase currencies are USD, EUR, GBP, and CAD.
</Info>

## Before you start

You need:

- A signed-in customer account with complete, confirmed legal identity; follow [Account details](/help/account-settings/account-details). Producer accounts cannot start new catalog purchases, even with a paid subscription; existing valid purchases and licenses remain separate.
- A track that is still eligible for exclusive purchase
- Producers with complete legal names, a signed exclusive offer, and payment setup completed for that track
- A supported payment card

### Common reasons purchase is blocked

- The track is already sold
- The track is not configured for exclusive purchase
- You already purchased it
- You are trying to buy your own track
- You are using a producer account
- The producer does not have the required payment setup
- The buyer or any producer is missing a legal first or last name
- The producer has not signed the current exclusive offer

## Buy the license

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the track page">
    Find a track with a **Buy Exclusive** button.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the price and terms">
    Open the purchase dialog and use **View License Terms** to read the exclusive terms for that track.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the price breakdown">
    BeatPass shows the producer price, either the platform fee or an active plan waiver, and the total amount in the selected currency.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete secure checkout">
    Accept the displayed exclusive terms and electronic signature using your legal account name, then continue to the secure payment form. Review changed terms again before paying.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for success confirmation">
    On success, BeatPass generates the exclusive license, marks the track as sold, and shows the success flow.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find the license later">
    Your exclusive license is stored in **Your licenses**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Signed offer and checkout record

The producer signs the exclusive offer from Backstage using their legal account name and confirms authority to license the beat for all rights holders. Changing the offered terms requires a new producer signature. A reusable preset does not carry another track’s authorization.

Checkout records the buyer’s acceptance time, legal name, producer identities, producer authorization, and exact license terms. Certificate issuance uses that accepted snapshot even if the producer later edits the offer. Incomplete identity can require action before a usable certificate or licensed download is available. Complete your legal identity in [Account details](/help/account-settings/account-details), then return to **Your licenses**; eligible certificate records can complete automatically when the identities match. Producers use their connected payout identity. An identity or viewing issue does not erase the original purchase and grant.

## After payment

After a successful payment:

- The exclusive license is created
- The track is marked as sold
- BeatPass prepares the original uploaded WAV for download
- The purchase is recorded in the selected currency
- The certificate becomes available in **Library** → **My Licenses**

## Check the total and promised files

The producer sets the track price. Checkout shows any platform fee or eligible active-plan waiver, the full total, and the currency before you pay. Secure card checkout supports USD, EUR, GBP, and CAD. An exclusive-purchase benefit does not remove subscription checkout or renewal fees.

Use **View License Terms** to check the file list. The producer separately delivers stems or other files promised in the offer.

## What to expect if payment fails

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Card was declined" icon="credit-card" defaultOpen>
    No license is created and the track stays in its prior state. You can try again with a different card or after fixing the payment issue.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Track became unavailable during checkout" icon="triangle-exclamation">
    Exclusive sales are first-come, first-served. If another user completed the purchase first, the track becomes unavailable.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Payment succeeded but I do not see the license yet" icon="file-lines">
    Check **Library** → **My Licenses** first. If it still does not appear, use the support path with the purchase details.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Refund and billing issues

If an exclusive purchase was made by mistake, contact support **within 24 hours**. The [refund exception](/help/billing/refund-policy) requires an erroneous purchase, a request within 24 hours, no access to beat files, and no use, sharing, submission, or reliance on its certificate. Automatic certificate generation alone does not disqualify review. Approval is not guaranteed.

BeatPass may also review remedies if a beat is later restricted because of a verified rights issue, AI misrepresentation, Proof of Rights failure, or AI policy breach. Remedies can include support, credit, refund review, certificate-status context, or another commercially reasonable response under the applicable policies.

<Warning>
  Exclusive purchase does not include AI training, dataset creation, model development, sample-pack creation, or competing library rights unless separately agreed in writing by all required parties.
</Warning>
