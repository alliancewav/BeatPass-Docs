Open **BeatPass ID** on a track page to understand its licensing choices and producer declarations before you choose a license. Its audio fingerprint helps connect the license record to the uploaded audio; it is not a guarantee of copyright clearance.

## Read the track's BeatPass ID

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open a track">
    Find a beat on BeatPass and expand **BeatPass ID** on its page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the declarations">
    Check **Licensing model**, **Offer status**, **Sample declaration**, and **Creation declaration**. Read producer notes or AI assistance details when shown.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check files and terms">
    Review **Promised buyer files** for an exclusive offer and use **Preview exact license terms** when available. The listed file package belongs to that offer.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Understand the audio fingerprint

**Verified audio hash** displays the recorded fingerprint when available, or **Not available**. A certificate can also include the saved fingerprint as evidence of the audio it covers.

A fingerprint identifies audio in BeatPass's record. It does not prove that every sample is cleared, that the beat is AI-free, or that a third-party distributor will accept a release. Producer declarations and rights obligations still apply.

Producers see processing checks when uploading audio. Follow the upload result before publishing; a **Verified Unique** result is a platform check, not a legal clearance certificate.

## Find your license proof

A qualifying download issues a certificate or reuses your existing valid certificate. Open [Your licenses](https://open.beatpass.ca/library/licenses) for the document and verification link. **Download requested** confirms a browser save request, so also check that the file arrived on your device.

Use [Audio Recon](/help/licensing-for-producers/audio-recon) for the separate producer similarity-review workflow, or [license verification](/help/downloads-and-licensing/verifying-licenses) to check a specific certificate.
