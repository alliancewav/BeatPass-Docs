Once you have a licensed beat, the next step is releasing your finished song correctly. This page covers the BeatPass license rules to keep in mind before you distribute.

<Note>
  Keep your certificate accessible in **My Licenses**. It is the fastest way to prove your rights if a platform or distributor asks questions.
</Note>

## Check the terms for your license

This page is a guide. Read the [Terms of Service](https://open.beatpass.ca/pages/terms-of-service) and the applicable [Non-Exclusive License Agreement](https://open.beatpass.ca/pages/non-exclusive-license-agreement) or [Exclusive License Agreement](https://open.beatpass.ca/pages/exclusive-license-agreement), together with your transaction terms and certificate. The Terms control conflicts unless a signed written license says otherwise.

The standard non-exclusive agreement covers one project, with limits of **100,000 physical or digital units**, **500,000 streams**, and **one music video no longer than five minutes**. Follow the caps and parameters attached to your download. Performance royalties and synchronization income default to **50% for the producer and 50% for the licensee**, unless the transaction terms, certificate, or a separate agreement provide otherwise. An income split does not itself grant permission for a sync use.

Use the terms that apply to your issued license. This guide does not change earlier grants. Exclusive permissions and deliverables depend on the specific purchase.

## Before You Release

Make sure you have:

- The downloaded beat on your device
- A finished song created from that beat
- Access to the correct license certificate
- The correct producer credit if you are using a standard non-exclusive license

## Release Checklist

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your finished song">
    A standard non-exclusive license covers one original musical work you create with the beat. Release your finished song, not the standalone instrumental.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the license terms">
    Confirm the license type and read the governing Terms, applicable agreement and your transaction terms with the certificate. Check the unit, stream and video limits, producer credit, and any income-sharing obligations before release.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Credit the producer when required">
    Standard non-exclusive licenses require attribution in this format: **Produced by [Producer Name]**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload through your distributor">
    Use the distributor you already trust and keep the certificate ready in case they request proof of license.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Keep the verification link handy">
    If a platform questions the release, share the certificate or the public verification URL tied to that license UUID.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What You Can Release Under Each License

| Capability | Non-exclusive | Exclusive |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Release and monetise a finished song | Within the applicable limits and income-sharing terms | Check the purchase terms |
| Number of projects | One original musical work per valid license | Check the purchase terms |
| Units and streams | Standard agreement: 100,000 physical or digital units and 500,000 streams | Check the purchase terms |
| Music video | Standard agreement: one video no longer than five minutes | Check the purchase terms |
| Register the beat in Content ID | No | Only if the purchase terms allow it |
| Film, TV, commercials, broadcast, or video games | Separate permission required outside the standard grant | Only if the purchase terms allow it |
| Perform at events over 50 attendees | Outside BeatPass's intended standard live-event policy | Check the purchase terms |

<Note>
  Read your certificate with the governing Terms, applicable agreement and transaction terms. This summary does not change an earlier grant.
</Note>

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Releasing the beat by itself" icon="circle-xmark" defaultOpen>
    Standard licenses are for songs you create with the beat. They do not let you release or redistribute the raw instrumental.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Skipping producer credit" icon="signature">
    Attribution is part of the standard non-exclusive terms. Treat it as required, not optional.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Registering Content ID on a non-exclusive beat" icon="shield-halved">
    The standard non-exclusive license does not allow Content ID registration for the beat.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Using one license for multiple songs" icon="copy">
    The standard non-exclusive license covers one original musical work. Re-downloading the same beat may reuse the existing certificate and does not itself grant another project. Contact support to confirm the separate permission needed before using it in a different song.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## If a Platform Questions Your Release

Open **My Licenses**, find the right certificate, and share:

- The license certificate (printable or PDF)
- The license UUID
- The public verification URL
