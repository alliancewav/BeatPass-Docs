A BeatPass license certificate records your right to use a track under a specific license. It combines track details, your license identity, the usage terms, and a public verification link.

BeatPass issues certificates based on producer warranties, disclosures, and Proof of Rights. A certificate documents the license record, but it does not guarantee that no third-party claim, AI-origin dispute, or rights-verification issue can ever arise.

## Check the terms for your license

This page is a guide. Read the [Terms of Service](https://open.beatpass.ca/pages/terms-of-service) and the applicable [Non-Exclusive License Agreement](https://open.beatpass.ca/pages/non-exclusive-license-agreement) or [Exclusive License Agreement](https://open.beatpass.ca/pages/exclusive-license-agreement), together with your transaction terms and certificate. The Terms control conflicts unless a signed written license says otherwise.

The standard non-exclusive agreement covers one project, with limits of **100,000 physical or digital units**, **500,000 streams**, and **one music video no longer than five minutes**. Follow the caps and parameters attached to your download. Performance royalties and synchronization income default to **50% for the producer and 50% for the licensee**, unless the transaction terms, certificate, or a separate agreement provide otherwise. An income split does not itself grant permission for a sync use.

Use the terms that apply to your issued license. This guide does not change earlier grants. Exclusive permissions and deliverables depend on the specific purchase.

## How to Access a Certificate

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open My Licenses">
    Navigate to **My Licenses**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find the license">
    Search by track, artist, licensee, or license ID.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the certificate">
    Select **Download** on the license card to open the printable certificate page for that license UUID.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Other Ways to Reach the Certificate

- The **Download Complete** modal after a successful track download
- A direct printable URL tied to the track and license UUID

<Note>
  An eligible non-owner download can generate a license certificate automatically. A repeat download may return the existing valid certificate rather than create another project license.
</Note>

## What the Certificate Shows

| Section | What it includes |
| --- | --- |
| **License identity** | Certificate UUID and license type (Non-Exclusive or Exclusive) |
| **Track information** | Track name, credited artists, duration, release date, genre, and track ID |
| **Audio Fingerprint Details** | Fingerprint status, fingerprint date, analysed duration, and fingerprint hash when available |
| **Licensed User Verification** | Licensed user name and masked email |
| **Usage terms** | Rights and restrictions for the specific license type |
| **Verification link** | Public URL at `/verify-license/{uuid}` |

## Names and License Cards

The **Licensed to** line uses the account holder's first and last name when available, with a username fallback. Certificates also identify available names for the accounts managing the beat's producer profiles. Update your own name in [Editing your profile](/help/profiles/user-profiles/editing-profile); it does not replace your public handle.

In [My Licenses](https://open.beatpass.ca/library/licenses), green cards identify non-exclusive licenses and blue cards identify exclusive licenses. Invalid or inactive records use red and explain their status in text. Read the status and verification result before relying on a certificate.

## Certificate vs receipt vs download history

A payment receipt proves that a transaction happened. Download history shows that an account accessed a file.

A BeatPass license certificate records the license identity, track details, applicable usage terms, licensed-user verification, fingerprint details when available, and a public verification link. When a distributor, platform, collaborator, label, or rights team asks for proof, the certificate is the document designed for that moment.

## AI and Rights-Issue Context

BeatPass certificates do not allow AI training, dataset creation, model development, sample-pack creation, or competing library use unless separate written permission is granted by all required parties.

If BeatPass later removes, disables, or materially restricts a beat because of a verified rights issue, AI misrepresentation, failure of Proof of Rights, breach of the AI Audio Policy, or another producer-side licensing problem, BeatPass may provide support, replacement credit, account credit, refund review, documentation support, certificate-status context, or another commercially reasonable remedy under the applicable policies.

BeatPass cannot grant rights the producer did not own or control.

## What a certificate does not promise

A certificate documents the license record BeatPass issued. It does not promise that no third-party rights issue, AI-origin dispute, producer-side breach, or platform review can ever arise.

If a verified issue appears later, BeatPass may provide commercially reasonable support under the applicable policies. BeatPass cannot grant rights the producer did not own or control.

## Standard Non-Exclusive Terms

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What you can do" icon="circle-check" defaultOpen>
    Create one original musical work with the beat and credit the producer. Follow the applicable limits: the standard agreement allows 100,000 physical or digital units, 500,000 streams, and one music video no longer than five minutes. BeatPass's intended live-event policy remains up to 50 attendees per event. Performance royalties and synchronization income default to 50/50 between producer and licensee unless the transaction terms, certificate, or a separate agreement provide otherwise.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What you cannot do" icon="circle-xmark">
    Release the beat by itself, use it in film/TV/broadcast radio/commercials/video games, perform at larger paid events, claim ownership of the beat, resell or share the beat or stems, or register the beat in Content ID.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Attribution" icon="signature">
    Standard non-exclusive terms require credit in this format: **Produced by [Producer Name]**.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Common questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can I send my certificate to a distributor?" icon="paper-plane" defaultOpen>
    Yes. If a distributor, label, platform, or rights team asks for proof, send the certificate or its public verification link.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does my certificate still matter if I cancel?" icon="infinity">
    Yes, for licenses that remain valid under the applicable terms. Cancellation affects future access, not the existence of an already issued certificate.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Is a screenshot enough?" icon="camera">
    Use the certificate and public verification link instead. A screenshot cannot independently confirm the certificate's current status.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Exclusive Certificate Terms

Your exclusive certificate records the terms for that purchase. Check it with the preview, checkout terms, governing Terms, applicable agreement and any signed addendum.

Confirm the rights granted, license duration, files, producer credit, Content ID permission, transfer restrictions, and any publishing or royalty obligations. These depend on the purchase. Do not assume a generic default percentage applies to your license.

## Printing and Sharing

The certificate opens as a printable BeatPass page. In practice, you can:

- Print it or save it as a PDF from your browser
- Share the public verification URL when a platform, distributor, or rights team needs confirmation

## Status and Priority Rules

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Active vs revoked" icon="shield-halved" defaultOpen>
    The certificate and public verification page show the current recorded status. Technical integrity, missing-data or expiry checks can affect certificate display or printing. A certificate check failure alone does not establish that a perpetual legal grant has expired. Keep the license record and contact support to clarify the issue.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Rights issue or review status" icon="triangle-exclamation">
    Future certificate states may include under review, disputed, rights issue notice, or revoked for licensee breach. A review or rights-issue status does not automatically mean the buyer acted wrongly; it means BeatPass has identified a license, rights, compliance, or verification issue that may require support.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Exclusive vs non-exclusive priority" icon="crown">
    An exclusive purchase stops future licensing to new buyers while earlier valid non-exclusive licenses continue under their applicable terms. Interface display priority does not cancel those earlier rights. Exclusive licenses are colour-coded blue; non-exclusive licenses are green.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Producer view" icon="user">
    If you own tracks, **My Licenses** also shows licenses granted to other users for your catalog.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
