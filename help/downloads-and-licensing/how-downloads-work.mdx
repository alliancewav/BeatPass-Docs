Found the beat for your next song? Open its download dialog to save the audio, then find your certificate separately in [Library → My Licenses](https://open.beatpass.ca/library/licenses), on the **Your licenses** page.

<Note>
  New standard catalog licenses require an eligible customer account with an active paid plan that includes downloads. Current producer accounts cannot acquire new standard licenses for other producers' beats, including with a paid plan. Exclusive checkout is covered separately in [Purchasing Exclusive](/help/downloads-and-licensing/exclusive-licenses/purchasing-exclusive).
</Note>

<DownloadFigure />

## Before you download

For a **new standard license**, sign in to an eligible customer account with a paid plan that includes downloads and current payments. The beat must offer standard licensing, remain unsold, and have every credited producer ready for subscription payouts. Required certificate identities and access checks must also be complete.

If you already have rights to the audio, use the matching route:

<Columns cols={2}>
  <Card title="Your own beat" icon="music">
    Retrieve your source audio when downloads are available. Self-downloads do not issue customer certificates.
  </Card>
  <Card title="A beat you already licensed" icon="file-contract">
    A valid earlier standard or exclusive grant is checked separately, including after cancellation, becoming a producer, or a later exclusive sale. Identity and access checks still apply.
  </Card>
</Columns>

Exclusive-only or sold tracks do not offer new standard licenses. Existing valid licenses and owner retrieval are checked separately.

## Save the audio and find your certificate

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click Download">
    On the beat page, review **Read standard license terms**, then select the download action. BeatPass opens **Download track**, with audio, license status, and BeatPass ID together.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the download">
    Wait for the license check, then select **Download audio**. BeatPass checks access again before asking your browser to save the file.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check your browser downloads">
    **Download requested** means your browser has been asked to save the producer's uploaded audio. Check its download list or your device's Downloads folder to confirm the file arrived.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find the certificate separately">
    An authorized customer download can issue a standard certificate or reuse an existing valid one. Owner downloads do not issue customer certificates. Select **My licenses** in the dialog to open **Your licenses**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review your issued terms">
    Open the certificate when it is ready. Re-downloading the same beat does not grant another project. Contact support before using it for a different song.
  </Step>
</Steps>



## Return to your licenses

Open [Library → My Licenses](https://open.beatpass.ca/library/licenses) to view the records tied to your account. The page is titled **Your licenses**.

From that page you can:

- Search by track name, artist name, licensee name, or license ID
- Open the printable certificate view
- Use **Verify** to open the public verification page
- View licenses granted to other users if you are a track owner



## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Download button is not visible" icon="eye-slash" defaultOpen>
    Sign in and check the beat's licensing choices. **View plans** means an eligible customer account needs download access for a new license. A producer account cannot unlock new standard catalog licenses by subscribing.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I see an overdue subscription message" icon="credit-card">
    Resolve the payment issue in Billing before acquiring new standard downloads. Previously acquired valid certificates are checked separately.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I own the track but have no license" icon="user">
    Owners can download their own tracks freely. No license certificate is generated for self-downloads.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The track downloaded but I need my certificate" icon="file-lines">
    Open **Your licenses** and follow its next action. Customers may need to confirm a legal name; producers may need payout identity review. See [Account details](/help/account-settings/account-details). If another party needs to act, the affected certificate stays locked.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<Note>
  Standard downloads provide the attached audio file, rather than a guaranteed WAV/MP3 bundle or stems. The browser save request does not prove that the file finished saving. If it failed, check browser download permissions and retry.
</Note>
