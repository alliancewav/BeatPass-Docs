Complete your beat's musical details to earn metadata XP and make it easier to browse. Open the beat from [My Beats](https://open.beatpass.ca/producer-dashboard/tracks), then check your rewards in [Progress](https://open.beatpass.ca/producer-dashboard/progress).


## Track rewards

| Action | XP |
|--------|------------|
| Track upload | 100 XP, once per track |
| First counted play on a track | 1 XP, once per track |
| Metadata completion | 50 XP, once per track |
| Collaboration | 25 XP, once per track |

## What Metadata-Complete Means

The metadata XP check looks for:

- **BPM**
- **Key**
- At least one **genre**

When you save a track that meets this check, eligible credited producer accounts can receive **50 XP once for that track**. Editing the same fields again does not earn another award. The full upload form has additional requirements, including scale, artwork, and rights declarations.


## How to Improve Metadata Completion

The easiest ways to hit metadata-complete status are:

1. Check that BPM and key match the audio.
2. Add at least one accurate genre.
3. Save the track and open **Progress** to review recent XP activity.

Keep descriptions, scale, and tags accurate too. If an award is missing, first check whether that track already received it; include the track link when asking support to investigate.


## Best Practices

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Treat metadata as catalog quality, not just XP">
    The biggest benefit of strong metadata is better discovery and clearer buyer understanding.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Review older tracks">
    Updating older beats is often the fastest way to improve your catalog-wide completeness.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Focus on accuracy first">
    Check musical details in your project before saving. Use only tags that describe the beat.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
