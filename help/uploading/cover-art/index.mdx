## Where Cover Art Appears

BeatPass uses track and collection artwork across the product, including:

- Track pages
- Search and browse results
- Producer profiles
- Collection pages

## Prepare the image

Use an image under **2 MB**. Square artwork works well with the circular crops used around BeatPass. Check that it stays clear as a small thumbnail.


## Artwork Priority

For existing content, BeatPass generally prefers:

1. Track-level cover art
2. Collection cover art if the track belongs to one
3. Fallback artwork where applicable

Confirm the preview before saving. If an image is rejected, check its file type and size, or choose a matched preset.
