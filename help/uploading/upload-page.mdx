Publish a beat with clear artwork, credits, and licensing choices. Start from [Upload beats](https://open.beatpass.ca/backstage/upload), keep your WAV ready, and work through the form in order.

<ProducerOnly />



## Accessing the Upload Page

From **Producer Dashboard** or **My Beats**, select **Upload beat**. The app navigation can also offer **Upload beats**. The page heading is **Upload your beats**.

## Upload Modes

Use the normal upload for a standalone beat. To group multiple files, turn on **Create Collection** before choosing them. The collection needs its own cover art, and every beat still needs its own audio file.

## Supported Audio Formats

Export your audio as **WAV**. MP3, FLAC, AAC, OGG, and other formats are not accepted.

If you upload an unsupported format, a dialog titled **Unsupported File Format** appears with an **Upload a Different File** button.

## Adding Files

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose the WAV">
    Drag it into the upload area or select **Choose files to upload**. After acceptance, the track form opens and audio fingerprinting begins.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Follow the open section">
    **Cover Art & Style** opens first. Complete it to make the next section available. The preceding section stays open for review; up to two sections remain open at a time.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review before saving">
    Complete the details below and wait for **Verified Unique**. Then follow [Saving Your Upload](#saving-your-upload) and check the saved beat in **My Beats**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Track Form Sections

The form is organized into five sections:

### Cover Art & Style

Upload cover art for your beat. You can use a custom image or select from **AI Cover Presets** matched to your genres. The form links directly to the [AI Cover Art Presets guide](/help/uploading/cover-art/templates) for more detail.

### Track Details

| Field | Required | Notes |
|-------|----------|-------|
| **Track Name** | Yes | Enter the final catalog title; file names and embedded audio titles are not copied into this field |
| **Artists** | Yes | Your producer profile is filled in; add agreed co-producer credits here |
| **Description** | No | Describe the beat's style, mood, or intended use |
| **Collection/Album** | No | Assign the beat to an existing or new collection |
| **Tags** | No | Add searchable tags (custom values allowed) |

### Music Properties

| Field | Required | Notes |
|-------|----------|-------|
| **Key** | Yes | Select from C through B, including sharps and flats |
| **Scale** | Yes | Major or Minor |
| **BPM** | Yes | Whole number from 60 to 200 |

### Protection & Verification

Choose your beat's sample clearance status:

<Columns cols={2}>
  <Card title="Cleared" icon="shield">
    The beat is 100% original or all samples are fully cleared.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Needs clearance" icon="triangle-exclamation">
    The beat contains samples that still require outside clearance.
  </Card>
</Columns>

If you choose **Needs clearance**, an additional notes field appears and buyers are warned accordingly.

This section also shows the audio fingerprinting status as the duplicate check runs.

### AI Use Declaration

BeatPass policy requires every beat to be human-made and not fully AI-generated. AI can assist production, but it cannot replace the producer's role in making the beat.

Choose one declaration before publishing:

| Choice | Use it when |
|--------|-------------|
| **Human-made** | The beat contains no AI-generated audio or material AI-generated source element |
| **AI-assisted** | AI supported a primarily human-made production and you remain the primary creator |

**AI-assisted** also requires the tool or service name, a brief explanation of how it affected the final audio, and confirmation that you can commercially use and license all AI-related material. Keep records that support your declaration in case BeatPass requests Proof of Rights.

<Warning>
  Sample-Safe status does not replace AI disclosure or AI rights verification. A beat marked **Needs clearance** can still violate the AI policy if the AI source or commercial-use rights cannot be verified.
</Warning>

Look for these separate declarations in the form. The examples use a saved demo beat; they are not a completed new upload.

### Licensing Options

This is the final step in the guided form. Choose how you want customers to license the beat. You can save and organize your catalog before payout setup is complete; customer downloads and purchases still depend on the payout readiness shown for each route.

If you want to offer exclusive licensing, expand the section and choose a model:

| Option | Description |
|--------|------------|
| **Standard Licensing** | Subscription licensing when every credited producer is eligible |
| **Standard + Exclusive** | Offer both standard licensing and exclusive purchase |
| **Exclusive Only** | Offer the beat only as an exclusive opportunity |

Read any payout notice below your choice. Saving the beat does not by itself make that licensing route available to customers. Follow **Set up payouts** when offered, then check [Finances](/help/earnings/index).

<Note>
  **Exclusive Only** tracks earn from direct sales and do not contribute to subscription revenue sharing. See the [Contribution System](/help/earnings/contribution-system) before choosing this model.
</Note>

When exclusive licensing is enabled, additional fields appear:

- **Exclusive price** and **currency** (USD, EUR, GBP, or CAD) for an **Available** offer
- **Price & availability**: **Available** lists a checkout price, subject to payout readiness; **Private** removes the price and directs buyers to chat
- **Exclusive contact email** (auto-filled with your email)
- **Files delivered to the buyer**: the original WAV plus stems and any other files you guarantee

**Sold** is not a publishing choice. BeatPass applies it automatically when an exclusive certificate is issued. After that, the audio and licensing settings are locked and only the cover art can be changed.

The form includes links to the licensing, exclusive terms, contribution, cover art, Sample-Safe, and AI policy guides. A compact **Publish with confidence** panel below the form keeps the main guides together without adding more fields.

## Audio Fingerprint Status

During upload, BeatPass shows these fingerprint states:

| Status | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **Pending** | Upload present but duplicate check hasn't started |
| **Checking** | Duplicate detection is starting |
| **Analyzing** | Audio fingerprint is being analyzed |
| **Verified Unique** | No duplicate found |
| **Check Failed** | Verification did not finish; upload the audio file again before publishing |

<Warning>
  A beat cannot be published unless the fingerprint check reaches **Verified Unique**. If the check fails, upload the audio file again. Contact support if verification repeatedly fails on a valid WAV file.
</Warning>

If a matching beat is detected, a modal shows the original track details and similarity score.

## Saving Your Upload

When the required fields are complete, click **Save Track**. The form is locked while BeatPass publishes the beat, so wait for the confirmation before leaving the page. After saving, open the beat from **My Beats** and check its artwork, credits, and licensing display. If saving fails, read the missing-field message and check the upload and verification status before trying again.

For collection uploads:

- The collection itself needs its own cover art
- Each track still needs its own audio file

If the beat includes an exclusive offer, BeatPass asks you to confirm that the license terms and promised deliverables are accurate before publishing. **WAV (Uncompressed)** is always included. You must also select **Stems (Separated Tracks)** and guarantee that you can provide them if the beat sells.

## Upload Minutes

Producer access is free. If your account has an upload allowance, the page shows the minutes remaining. Follow the limit message if an upload is blocked, or contact support if the allowance looks wrong. A paid customer subscription is not a producer upload upgrade.

## Tips

  <Accordion title="Export as WAV before uploading" icon="file-audio">
    Convert your final masters to WAV before uploading. All other formats are blocked.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Pick genres early" icon="music">
    Genres affect discovery and drive the AI Cover Preset artwork suggestions.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Watch the fingerprint status" icon="fingerprint">
    A beat cannot be confirmed unique until the duplicate check finishes. If a duplicate is found, resolve it before saving.
