Credit the people who made the beat with you. Agree on credits, permissions, and money before publishing, then add collaborators in the track form's **Artists** field.


## How Collaborations Work

For collaborative beats, BeatPass currently does the following:

- Shows the beat on each credited producer profile
- Splits subscription contribution value equally across credited producers
- Lets the uploader and accounts that own a credited producer profile access track management, subject to licensing locks

<Note>
  A producer credit can give the account that owns that profile access to track management. Add credits only with agreement from your collaborators. A collaborator cannot use this to make an unrelated profile the primary credit, and sold beats keep their artwork-only edit limit.
</Note>


## Adding Collaborators

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the track form">
    Upload a new beat or edit an existing one.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Use the Artists field">
    Add the credited producers in the **Artists** picker.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the track">
    Once saved, the collaboration appears on the credited producer profiles.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Attribution Best Practice

The cleanest collaboration setup is when every credited producer already has a BeatPass profile. That keeps attribution, profile visibility, and producer-side data aligned.


## How Subscription Earnings Are Split

BeatPass currently splits contribution value equally among credited producers.

| Producers Credited | Each Producer Gets |
|-------------------|-------------------|
| 1 | 100% |
| 2 | 50% each |
| 3 | 33.3% each |
| 4 | 25% each |

This applies to BeatPass subscription contribution, not to private off-platform agreements between collaborators.

## Exclusive Sales on Collaborative Beats

BeatPass does not automatically split exclusive-sale proceeds across collaborators just because multiple producers are credited.

The exclusive payout route follows the beat's **primary credited producer**. Adding other credits does not automatically divide that payment. Confirm who receives the payout and who will deliver the promised files before listing an exclusive offer.

If collaborators want a different revenue split, they need to arrange that outside BeatPass.


## When to Use Collaboration Credits

Use collaboration credits when:

- Two or more producers worked on the beat
- You want the release to appear on multiple producer profiles
- You are comfortable sharing BeatPass subscription contribution equally

Do **not** use collaboration credits casually if you do not want the track appearing on another producer's profile or sharing BeatPass-side contribution value.


## Common Questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can I remove a collaborator later?">
    On an unsold beat you manage, update the agreed credits in **Artists** and save. Credits affect profile visibility, management access, and future contribution. Sold beats have locked metadata; contact support if a locked credit needs correction.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Do collaborators approve the credit inside BeatPass?">
    There is no separate credit-approval step in this form. Coordinate with your collaborators before publishing; an accepted producer credit can give that profile's owner management access.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Does collaboration also affect XP?">
    An eligible credited producer account can receive **25 XP once per collaborative track**. Upload, metadata, and first-counted-play awards have their own once-per-track rules.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Does BeatPass automatically split exclusive-sale money across collaborators?">
    No. The payout route follows the primary credited producer. Agree separately on any split with collaborators and keep a record of that agreement.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
