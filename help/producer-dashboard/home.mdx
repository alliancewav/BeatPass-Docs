Pick up where you left off in your catalog, then check how listeners are responding. [Dashboard](https://open.beatpass.ca/producer-dashboard) opens with **Recent beats**, followed by your **Performance snapshot**.

## How to Get Here

Click your **profile avatar** in the top bar, then select **Producer Dashboard**. Choose **Dashboard** in the producer navigation to return here.

## What You See

  </Card>
  <Card title="Performance snapshot" icon="chart-bar">
    Your 30-day play comparison, level progress, catalog totals, and audience totals.
  </Card>

## Recent beats

Browse up to eight of your newest beats. Swipe the row on a phone, or use **Previous** and **Next** when there are more cards than fit on screen. Choose **View all** to open **My Beats** with the full catalog and its editing tools.

If you have no beats, choose **Upload beat**. If the catalog cannot load or refresh, use **Try again**; a failed refresh keeps the previous beats visible.

## Performance Snapshot

**30-day play activity** compares your latest and previous play counts. **Your progress** shows your level, cumulative XP, next-level progress, and your latest unlocked achievement when available; select it to open **Progress**.

The **Your catalog** and **Audience** blocks keep the remaining totals together:

| Card | What it tracks |
| --- | --- |
| **Tracks** | Total tracks for your current producer profile |
| **Total Plays** | Lifetime plays across your catalog |
| **Collections** | Collections in your producer catalog |
| **Downloads** | Track download count |
| **Followers** | Follower total |

### How the Play Trend Works

The trend counts plays recorded on BeatPass for tracks connected to your current producer profile. It compares two adjacent, non-overlapping rolling periods:

- **Current:** the latest 30 days
- **Previous:** the 30 days immediately before the current period

<Accordion title="How Play change is calculated" icon="calculator">
  **Play change** compares the difference with the busier of the two periods, keeping the result between −100% and +100%. For example, 237 recent plays and 42 previous plays gives +82.3%.

  Equal counts give 0%. Plays in only the current period give +100%; plays in only the previous period give −100%. Both source counts remain visible beside the result.
</Accordion>

| State | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Growing** | More than +5%, once enough play data exists |
| **Steady** | From −5% through +5%, inclusive |
| **Needs attention** | Below −5% |
| **Building data** | Fewer than 10 combined plays across both periods |
| **Play trend unavailable** | The trend could not be calculated or loaded |

<Note>
  This signal measures BeatPass play activity. It does not measure imported lifetime plays, contribution points, payouts, Pro Page traffic, or catalog value.
</Note>

## View Your Producer Profile

On desktop, choose **View Producer Profile** in the top navigation to see your public page. Use **My Beats** to work on your catalog, or **Pro Page** to prepare your shareable storefront.

<Tip>
  Use the Home page to decide where to investigate next. The play counts explain the trend; open My Beats when you want to work on the catalog behind it.
</Tip>

## When Data Is Missing

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="You are new to the dashboard" icon="wand-magic-sparkles" defaultOpen>
    New producers see **0** for successfully loaded totals until tracks, plays, downloads, or followers begin to accumulate.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="A metric request fails" icon="triangle-exclamation">
    Analytics and progress load independently. BeatPass keeps any successful group visible, marks only unavailable values with a dash, and offers a retry instead of showing realistic-looking zeros.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The play trend is building data" icon="chart-line">
    BeatPass waits for at least 10 combined plays across the two 30-day periods before assigning a directional state.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="No linked artist profile" icon="user">
    The dashboard shows a not-found state when your account does not have a linked artist profile. Complete your [Producer Program application](/help/producer-program) first.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
