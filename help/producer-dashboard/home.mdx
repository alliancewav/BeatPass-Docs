The Home page is the landing view when you open the Producer Dashboard. It keeps your Pro Page guidance, producer tools, and useful catalog signals in one focused flow.

<ProducerOnly />

## How to Get Here

Click your **profile avatar** in the top bar, then select **Backstage**. The sidebar opens on **Dashboard** by default.

## Pro Page

The **Pro Page** module appears above **Producer tools**. It explains the focused storefront, shows your upload, verification, and BEATPAY requirements, and provides sharing actions after access is ready.

See [Your Producer Pro Page](/help/producer-dashboard/pro-page) for the complete unlock and campaign guide.

## Producer Tools

The soft-panel rows help you choose what to work on next. Each row includes an icon, a plain-language description, and a visible destination indicator.

- Tools follow the live primary sidebar order
- The current **Dashboard** link is omitted because you are already on that page
- Internal destinations use normal same-tab navigation
- External destinations show an external-link indicator and follow their configured tab behavior
- Secondary shortcuts remain in their existing menu and are not mixed into this section
- If tools cannot be loaded, BeatPass shows producer-facing unavailable guidance

## Performance Snapshot

The featured **30-day play activity** panel appears first. It shows the latest count, the previous count, and the relative play change without hiding the source numbers. Below it, six compact cards surface the most useful totals for your current producer profile.

| Card | What it tracks |
| --- | --- |
| **Tracks** | Total tracks for your current producer profile |
| **Total Plays** | Lifetime plays across your catalog |
| **Albums** | Total album count |
| **Downloads** | Track download count |
| **Followers** | Follower total |
| **Level** | Current level and XP |

### How the Play Trend Works

The trend counts plays recorded on BeatPass for tracks connected to your current producer profile. It compares two adjacent, non-overlapping rolling periods:

- **Current:** the latest 30 days
- **Previous:** the 30 days immediately before the current period

The **Play change** value divides the difference between the two periods by the busier period. This keeps the result naturally between **−100% and +100%** instead of calculating a large percentage and cutting it off. For example, 237 recent plays compared with 42 previous plays is **+82.3%**, not a capped +100%.

Equal counts produce **0%**. Current activity with no previous plays produces **+100%**, while previous activity with no current plays produces **−100%**. The value is rounded to one decimal when needed, and the two play counts remain visible beside it.

| State | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Growing** | More than +5%, once enough play data exists |
| **Steady** | From −5% through +5%, inclusive |
| **Needs attention** | Below −5% |
| **Building data** | Fewer than 10 combined plays across both periods |
| **Play trend unavailable** | The trend could not be calculated or loaded |

<Note>
  This signal measures BeatPass play activity. It does not measure imported lifetime plays, contribution points, payouts, Pro Page traffic, or catalog value.
</Note>

## View Your Producer Profile

When your account has a producer profile, **View Producer Profile** appears with the right-side account actions in the floating top navigation on desktop. It opens your public producer profile in the same tab. The former profile tile is no longer part of Performance snapshot, and no mobile replacement is shown.

<Tip>
  Use the Home page to decide where to investigate next. The play counts explain the trend; open My Beats when you want to work on the catalog behind it.
</Tip>

## When Data Is Missing

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="You are new to the dashboard" icon="sparkles" defaultOpen>
    New producers see **0** for successfully loaded totals until tracks, plays, downloads, or followers begin to accumulate.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="A metric request fails" icon="triangle-exclamation">
    Analytics and progress load independently. BeatPass keeps any successful group visible, marks only unavailable values with a dash, and offers a retry instead of showing realistic-looking zeros.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The play trend is building data" icon="chart-line">
    BeatPass waits for at least 10 combined plays across the two 30-day periods before assigning a directional state.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="No linked artist profile" icon="user">
    The dashboard shows a not-found state when your account does not have a linked artist profile. Complete your [Producer Program application](/help/producer-program) first.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
