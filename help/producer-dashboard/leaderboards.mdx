The **Leaderboards** page helps you understand where you stand using separate, clearly labeled measures. **Earning share** appears first because it connects directly to the contribution information used in **Finances**.

<ProducerOnly />

## Open Leaderboards

Click your **profile avatar** → **Backstage**, then select **Leaderboards** in the sidebar.

The page always follows this order:

1. **Earning share**
2. **Producer rankings**

If you manage more than one artist profile, use **Artist profile** above the sections to choose which artist you want to review. **Progress** remains account-wide when you switch artists.

## Earning Share

**Earning share** compares each ranked artist's current contribution share. It uses the same current contribution formula as **Finances**, but it never shows anyone's private earnings amount.

Each row shows:

- Rank
- Producer name and profile link
- Earning share
- Number of contributing tracks

An artist needs at least one track on BeatPass to join producer rankings. An eligible artist without a current contribution share is not ranked in this view.

<Tip>
  Select **View your earnings** to open [Finances](/help/producer-dashboard/finances) and review your own currency-specific totals and payout stages.
</Tip>

## Producer Rankings

Use **Ranking** to compare one measure at a time:

| Ranking | What it measures | Scope |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Progress** | Cumulative XP earned by the producer account | All time |
| **Audience plays** | Plays from listeners who are not part of the artist team | 7 days, 30 days, or all time |
| **Releases** | Tracks added to BeatPass during the selected period | 7 days, 30 days, or all time |
| **Collaborations** | Collaborative tracks released during the selected period | 7 days, 30 days, or all time |

On narrow screens, **Ranking** and **Period** use native selection fields. On wider screens, they appear as grouped controls.

### Time Periods

**7 days** and **30 days** are exact rolling periods ending when the ranking snapshot updates. **All time** has no artificial start date. **Progress** is cumulative, so it always uses all-time XP. Each artist profile appears once through its canonical producer account, so additional team access cannot create a duplicate producer row.

### Audience Plays

Known plays from people who manage the ranked artist are excluded. Anonymous listener plays can still count. This keeps the ranking focused on audience response instead of artist-team activity.

### Releases and Collaborations

Tracks published on BeatPass count. A collaboration is a track credited to at least two artist profiles. These rankings count released tracks, not an opaque combined score.

## How Positions Work

- Equal results share the same rank
- The next rank skips the positions occupied by a tie
- BeatPass shows the top 20 producers for the selected view
- If you are outside the top 20, **Your position** shows your row with a nearby producer above and below when available
- The participant total describes producers who are ranked in the current view, not every eligible artist on BeatPass

Select **Read the Leaderboards guide** to open this guide from the dashboard.

## Update and Error States

Rankings are prepared in the background and normally refresh about every five minutes. This avoids running platform-wide calculations while producers open the page.

- **Showing the most recent available ranking.** means the latest usable snapshot is being shown
- **Ranking update failed. Showing the last available result.** keeps previously loaded rankings visible after a background update fails
- **Producer rankings are temporarily unavailable** appears only when no usable result is available
- A genuine no-activity state is explained separately and is never replaced with a realistic-looking zero

## Use Rankings Constructively

Each view points to a real part of your producer work:

- Review [Finances](/help/producer-dashboard/finances) for your private earning details
- Review [Progress](/help/producer-dashboard/progress) for XP sources and achievements
- Review [Activity](/help/producer-dashboard/activity) for audience response and downloads
- Use [My Beats](/help/producer-dashboard/tracks) to maintain releases and collaboration credits

Rankings are a comparison tool, not a promise of promotion, visibility, or future earnings.
