Give people one link to your sound. Your **Pro Page** brings your identity, beats, credits, and available licensing choices together for visitors arriving from a bio, campaign, or conversation.

<ProducerOnly />

Every producer profile owner can open [Pro Page](https://open.beatpass.ca/producer-dashboard/pro-page). The public storefront becomes available after its unlock checklist is complete. Producer access is free.

## Pro Page vs Producer Profile

<Columns cols={2}>
  <Card title="Producer profile" icon="user" href="/help/profiles/producer-profiles">
    Your full marketplace presence: catalog, followers, profile tabs, and discovery.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Pro Page" icon="gem" href="https://open.beatpass.ca/producer-dashboard/pro-page">
    A focused storefront for traffic you send from social posts, campaigns, or direct messages.

The same profile and catalog information feeds both pages. Your Pro Page adds a focused place to listen and explore; your producer profile remains available.

## Find Your Pro Page Status

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your producer workspace">
    Select your **profile avatar** → **Producer Dashboard** → **Pro Page**. On desktop, it is in **Audience**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the current status">
    Follow the action beside an incomplete checklist item. The workspace uses your own producer profile automatically.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the public result">
    Once access is ready, use **Open page** before sharing the link.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Unlock Checklist

Complete all three items in **Your unlock checklist**:

<Columns cols={3}>
  <Card title="Playable beats" icon="music">Reach the beat target displayed for your profile, with you as the primary producer.</Card>
  <Card title="Verified profile" icon="circle-check">Complete the producer identity review and keep verification active.</Card>
  <Card title="Active BEATPAY" icon="building-columns">Complete and maintain bank payout setup.</Card>
</Columns>

The beat target is a permanent unlock milestone. Verification and BEATPAY must remain active to keep the page public. The profile must also have an attached owner, and each owner's account must stay in good standing. Managers or collaborators cannot keep it published alone.

### What counts toward the beat milestone

A beat counts when you are its **primary producer** and its audio is saved and playable. A collaboration primarily owned by another producer does not count for you. A sold beat or one with no current buying option can still count toward this milestone; that does not make it available for a new license.

Use the displayed target and saved-catalog count. Follow [upload help](/help/uploading), [verification help](/help/producer-program/application-types/get-verified), or [BEATPAY setup](/help/earnings/beatpay) for an unfinished item.

## When Your Page Is Ready

<ProPageFigure />

The ready card gives you a public link and these actions:

- **Open page** to review it
- **Copy link** or **Share** to send it, with device sharing when supported
- **Copy tagged link** to label a campaign for Instagram, TikTok, X, or direct messages
- **View performance** to open the **Pro Page** tab in Artist Insights

If the card says BeatPass is preparing the page after all items are complete, wait for that state to finish. You do not need to change the catalog again.

## Keep Your Page Live

If verification or BEATPAY becomes inactive, an owner connection is removed, or an owner account loses good standing, the public page is temporarily hidden. Follow the requirement highlighted in the dashboard. Completing it restores the existing page; unlocked access and past traffic remain recorded.

Falling below the beat target after unlocking does not hide the page. If a temporary service interruption prevents a readiness check, use **Try again** rather than replacing bank details that were already complete.

## Choose the Featured Beat

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your catalog">
    Select **Choose featured beat** from Pro Page, or open **My Beats**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the first beat visitors hear">
    Search or filter, then select **Feature on Pro Page** on the beat you want to lead with.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Change or remove the choice">
    Feature another beat to replace it. **Remove featured beat** lets BeatPass choose an available beat again.
  </Step>
</Steps>

A featured beat needs your primary producer credit, playable audio, and an active licensing or purchase option. AI-assisted beats also need commercial-rights confirmation. A beat sold exclusively cannot be featured for new customers. **My Beats** explains an unavailable choice; if a saved choice later becomes unavailable, BeatPass uses another eligible beat.

### How the Automatic Choice Works

<Accordion title="What happens if I do not choose a beat?">
  BeatPass considers playable, available beats primarily credited to you and not sold exclusively. It balances plays, saves, listening completion, recency, complete details, and current buying choices. No single number decides the result.

  Your own featured choice takes priority while it remains eligible. An automatic choice can change as your catalog and audience activity change.
</Accordion>

## Choose Which Optional Sections Appear

Use **Page sections** to show or hide **Links**, **Collections**, **Collaborations**, **Credits**, and **Photos**. Your identity, catalog, player, licensing, and an available featured beat remain part of the storefront.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check the section's content">
    **Not available yet** means there is no usable public content. Its switch is disabled and visitors see no empty placeholder. Use the management link to add the content.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose what to show">
    Switch an available section on or off. Each change saves immediately, with no separate Save button. These choices belong to the producer profile and are shared by its owners; managers and collaborators cannot change them.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the saved result">
    Open the public page. If saving fails, the previous choice stays active and **Try again** is offered. A failed refresh also keeps the saved choices visible.
  </Step>
</Steps>

A hidden section stays hidden if its content disappears and returns, until an owner shows it again. These controls change visibility, rather than adding themes, custom code, or a page builder.

## Make the Page Work for Your Campaign

Choose a clear profile image, cover, short bio, location, and genres. Keep links, credits, photos, artwork, and buying options current. Feature the beat you want people to hear first.

Give a post one reason to click, such as a particular beat or collection. Test playback and available actions on a phone and computer before sharing widely.

## What Visitors See

Visitors see your identity, available links, and featured beat, then can search, filter, play, and review available licensing in the catalog. Back and Forward retain the selected catalog view and beat. Opening a collection goes to that collection's page.

Only usable public content appears. Collections need public beats; empty links, collaborations, credits, and photos are omitted, as are sections you chose to hide. When a desktop QR code is shown, visitors can use it to continue on their phone.

## Understand Pro Page Traffic

Open **View performance**. If you start from **Insights** in the desktop navigation, choose the **Pro Page** tab on your own primary producer report. The **Overview** and **Audience** tabs cover marketplace listening.

<ProPageInsightsFigure />

| Result | What it tells you |
| --- | --- |
| **Engaged visits** | Visitors who stayed active after opening the link |
| **30-second listens** | Plays that reached at least 30 seconds |
| **License actions started** | Standard or exclusive licensing actions selected on the Pro Page |
| **Completed licenses and sales** | Matching downloads or purchases confirmed by BeatPass |

Select **What counts?** for definitions and the dates used by the report. **Where visits came from**, **Licensing activity**, memberships started, and **Exclusive earnings** help you follow the path from a shared link to an outcome. Earnings stay separate by currency.

<Accordion title="Why are some traffic numbers missing or different?">
  A new page can have no activity or only a partial date range. Regular marketplace listening remains in the other Artist Insights sections.

  Detailed activity has a retention limit; daily totals remain after older details expire. A later visit, listen, or another licensing action can add activity. Repeated actions can count more than once. A started action is separate from a completed license or sale.
</Accordion>
