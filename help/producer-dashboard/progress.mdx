The **Progress** page explains how your producer account earns XP and how close you are to the next level. It also keeps available and unlocked achievements in one collection.

<ProducerOnly />

## Open Progress

Click your **profile avatar** in the top navigation bar, select **Backstage**, then select **Progress** in the sidebar.

The page is organized as **Progress**, **Earn XP**, and **Achievements**.

## Progress

**Your progress** shows:

- Your current level number and producer title
- Your cumulative XP
- A linear bar showing progress within the current level
- The exact XP threshold for the next level
- Up to five of your most recent XP awards, with the source and date

A new account starts at Level 1 with 0 XP. This is a valid starting state. At Level 100, the page shows **Maximum level reached** instead of a next-level remainder.

Select **How progress works** for a short explanation of account scope, one-time track awards, achievement rewards, and update timing.

## Earn XP

**Earn XP** uses the current reward amounts supplied by BeatPass. Each row explains when the award can be earned and links to a relevant producer action when one is available.

| Action | Current award | When it is counted |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| **Upload a track** | +100 XP | Once for each eligible track |
| **First counted play** | +1 XP | Once when an eligible track receives its first counted play |
| **Complete track details** | +50 XP | Once when an eligible track has complete metadata |
| **Collaborate on a track** | +25 XP | Once for each eligible collaborative track credited to your account |
| **Unlock achievements** | Varies | Once for each achievement |

Audience plays are shown as information. Do not create artificial plays to pursue XP.

## Achievements

The **Achievements** summary features **Collection progress** with your unlocked count and percentage, plus the XP actually awarded by achievements and your latest unlock date.

Use **Status** to choose **Available**, **Unlocked**, or **All**. Use **Category** to narrow the collection. The default view is **Available**, and eight achievements appear per page. **Previous** and **Next** appear only when the filtered collection needs more than one page.

Achievement cards use a distinct rarity color so **Common**, **Rare**, **Epic**, and **Legendary** rewards are easy to recognize as a collection. The color does not change the unlock rule or XP amount.

Each achievement separates two values:

- An available achievement shows the XP it can award
- An unlocked achievement shows the XP actually added to your account

A real 0 XP unlock is shown as **No XP bonus**. It still counts as unlocked.

Select **View Producer Leaderboards** to compare producer activity. Rankings remain separate from your personal Progress view.

## Updates and unavailable states

Progress listens for eligible uploads, completed track details, collaborations, counted plays, level changes, and achievement unlocks. When the live connection is available, the page refreshes as soon as the award is committed. It also checks again every 30 seconds as a fallback.

Larger XP awards and level changes show a short on-screen message. Achievement unlocks also create a notification you can return to later. Small awards still update the XP total without interrupting your work. Managers do not earn XP for activity on a producer profile they only manage; awards belong to the profile owners credited through that producer identity.

If a refresh fails, previously loaded information stays visible with **Try again**. If the first request fails, BeatPass shows that Progress is temporarily unavailable; it does not treat missing information as 0 XP. Level and achievement information can remain available while supplementary **Recent XP** or **Earn XP** details are temporarily unavailable.
