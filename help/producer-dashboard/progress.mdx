The **Progress** page explains how your producer account earns <Tooltip tip="Points that increase your producer level and unlock progress rewards.">XP</Tooltip> and how close you are to the next level. It also keeps available and unlocked achievements in one collection.

<ProducerOnly />

## Open Progress

Click your **profile avatar** in the top navigation bar, select **Producer Dashboard**, then select **Progress** in the sidebar.

The page follows **Progress** → **Achievements** → **Earn XP**. Use the section links to jump to what you need.

## Progress

<ProgressFigure />

The level card shows:

- Your current level number and producer title
- Your cumulative XP
- A ring showing progress within the current level
- The exact XP threshold for the next level

Select **Recent XP** for up to five recent awards, with their source and date. **Earn XP** jumps to the earning actions below.

A new account starts at Level 1 with 0 XP. This is a valid starting state. At Level 100, the page shows **Maximum level reached** instead of a next-level remainder. You can still earn XP and unlock achievements.

Select **How progress works** for a short explanation of account scope, one-time track awards, achievement rewards, and update timing.

## Earn XP

**Earn XP** uses the current reward amounts supplied by BeatPass. Each row explains when the award can be earned and links to a relevant producer action when one is available.

| Action | Current award | When it is counted |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| **Upload a track** | +100 XP | Once for each eligible track |
| **First counted play** | +1 XP | Once when an eligible track receives its first counted play |
| **Complete track details** | +50 XP | Once when an eligible track has BPM, key, and at least one genre |
| **Collaborate on a track** | +25 XP | Once for each eligible collaborative track credited to your account |
| **Unlock achievements** | Varies | Once for each achievement |

Audience plays are shown as information. Do not create artificial plays to pursue XP.

## Achievements

**Your achievements** shows how many you have unlocked, how many remain to explore, a collection progress meter, and XP from achievements. Individual unlocked cards show their recorded dates when available.

The collection opens on **All**. Browse the **Unlocked** row, then **Explore achievements** below. Use **Search achievements**, open **Category** to choose a category, and select **All**, **Unlocked**, or **Not unlocked** to narrow it. **Clear filters** starts again.

Uncollected achievements appear six per page. Use **Previous** and **Next** below that list. The unlocked row scrolls separately with **Previous achievement** and **Next achievement** when needed.

Locked artwork is muted and marked with a lock; open it to read the requirement. Rarity pills identify **Common**, **Rare**, **Epic**, and **Legendary** achievements. That assigned rarity is separate from the measured community unlock rate. Scroll through a card's details for its reward, public producer examples, and your unlock history when available. Goal and suggested-task buttons appear at the bottom when offered.

Each achievement separates two values:

- **Not unlocked** cards show potential XP when the goal has an XP reward
- **Unlocked** cards show recorded XP awarded at the time

A recorded 0 XP unlock says **No XP bonus** and still counts. If the historical award is missing, the card says **Unlocked** without inventing a number.

### Keep a goal in view

<AchievementGoalFigure />

Select **Choose a goal** when it appears, or open a locked achievement yourself. Read its requirement, then select **Choose this goal** at the bottom when offered. **Your next goal** keeps its artwork, requirement, and reward above the collection.

Use **View goal** to reopen it. **Change goal**, when offered, opens another achievement to review before you choose it. **Remove goal** clears your choice. A completed choice says **Goal unlocked** until you change or remove it.

The choice is remembered for this account on this device when browser storage is available; otherwise it lasts **For this visit**. Choosing a goal does not unlock it or award XP. Historical goals such as **Early Adopter** explain their original requirements instead of offering this action.

Select **Leaderboards** below the collection to compare producer activity. Rankings remain separate from your personal Progress view.

## Updates and unavailable states

Eligible uploads, completed track details, collaborations, counted plays, level changes, and achievement unlocks can update **Progress** right away. The page also checks every 30 seconds so the totals stay current if a live update is delayed.

Larger XP awards and level changes show a short message. Achievement unlocks also create a notification you can revisit. Small awards update your total without interrupting your work. XP belongs to producer profile owners; managers do not earn it for profiles they only manage.

If a refresh fails, previously loaded information stays visible with **Try again**. If the first request fails, BeatPass shows that Progress is temporarily unavailable; it does not treat missing information as 0 XP. Level and achievement information can remain available while supplementary **Recent XP** or **Earn XP** details are temporarily unavailable.
