The **Finances** page helps you track every earning, see what is waiting, and manage how you get paid.

<ProducerOnly />



## Open Finances

Open [Finances](https://open.beatpass.ca/producer-dashboard/finances), or choose your **profile avatar** → **Producer Dashboard** → **Finances**.

The page uses your own producer profile. Use the section links to jump to the earnings, setup, activity, or currency information you need.

## Page Order

The page always keeps the same order:

1. **Earnings**, with **Payout setup** below the amounts in the same card
2. **Finance activity**
3. **Currency context**, which you can expand when available

If payout setup needs action, a short notice links to **Payout setup** without moving the section.

## Earnings

**Earnings** keeps every currency separate. Use **Earnings currency** when the artist has earnings in more than one currency.

| Amount | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Total earned** | Successful, non-refunded track sales and eligible subscription earnings, including money still moving through payout processing |
| **Pending** | Earned money waiting to enter payout processing |
| **Processing** | Money moving through the payout provider |
| **Delayed** | Earned money with a payout issue; open the earning for details |
| **Sent** | Money sent by the payout provider; bank arrival can happen later |

<PayoutBankArrival />

A real zero remains visible as zero. If BeatPass cannot load earnings, the section says they are unavailable instead of replacing missing information with zero.

## Payout Setup

**Payout setup** sits below the amounts in **Earnings** and shows the route available to your account. Bank setup and payout readiness are related, but subscription earnings and exclusive sales can have different availability during a rollout. Follow the readiness shown for the earning type rather than assuming one active destination enables every sale or payout.

| What you see | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Set up BEATPAY** | Add a bank payout destination for eligible earnings |
| **Upgrade to BEATPAY** | Legacy Stripe is still active and BEATPAY setup is available |
| **BEATPAY setup is under review** | Submitted bank details are being reviewed; no new action is requested |
| **BEATPAY is ready** | Eligible earnings use the saved BEATPAY destination |
| **Use a different bank account** | Replace the current BEATPAY destination |

If no payout destination is active yet, **Payout setup** shows **Setup required**
and asks you to choose a bank account currency that the receiving account can
accept. Select **Set up BEATPAY** to continue through the guided bank details
form.

BeatPass does not offer new or resumed Stripe onboarding from this page.

### Legacy Stripe

**Legacy Stripe** appears only while your producer profile has an active or draining legacy account.

- Before BEATPAY becomes active, Stripe can remain the current payout route.
- After BEATPAY becomes active, Stripe may remain visible while previously routed payouts finish.
- The Stripe balance is read-only and separate from **Earnings**.
- Retired or incomplete Stripe setup does not create a setup action.

Transaction history can still identify **Legacy Stripe** after the setup card is gone.

See [BEATPAY Bank Payouts](/help/earnings/beatpay) for bank setup and [Payout Schedule](/help/earnings/payout-schedule) for status and timing definitions.

## Finance Activity

<FinancesFigure />

**Finance activity** is below Earnings and records individual track sales and subscription earnings.

The two summary actions show the current number of:

- **Pending** earnings
- **Delayed** earnings

Use the separate filters to narrow the list:

| Filter | Options |
| --- | --- |
| **Status** | **All activity**, **Pending**, **Processing**, **Delayed**, **Sent**, **Reversed** |
| **Earning type** | **All types**, **Track sales**, **Subscription earnings** |

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose the records you need">
    Select **Status** and **Earning type**. Use **Delayed** to investigate an earning with an issue, or choose **All activity** to see the full list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the earning">
    Review the original-currency amount, provider, status explanation, and **Reference ID**. Keep that reference if you need support.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Continue through history">
    The list shows 20 records at a time. Use **Previous** and **Next** and check the displayed range.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Each row can show:

- The track or subscription earning period
- The earning type, date, and payout provider
- The **Reference ID** and, when shown, the payout group reference
- The amount in its original currency
- **Pending**, **Processing**, **Delayed**, **Sent**, or **Reversed**, with an explanation when one is available

BeatPass refreshes records while payouts are pending or processing and checks again when you return to the page. A reversed customer payment appears as **Reversed**. A payout issue can appear as **Delayed**; read the earning for the reason and any action requested.

Failed payment attempts that never became earnings do not appear. A payout failure or hold on a real earning remains visible under **Delayed**.

## Currency Context

Expand **Currency context** below Finance activity to see the exchange-rate information available for your payout setup.

Use **Comparison currency** to choose a currency, then select **7D**, **30D**, or **90D**. The graph shows:

- The recent exchange-rate line
- **Current**, **Low**, and **High**
- The first and last dates in the selected range
- A date and rate when you point to the graph or use the Left and Right Arrow keys

On a touch screen, tap or drag across the graph to inspect a date. You can still
scroll the page vertically while using the graph.

<Info>
  Historical exchange-rate context is not a payout quote. The final rate is set when a payout is created.
</Info>

An unavailable request, a period without enough history, and a same-currency selection are shown as different states.

## Loading and Errors

**Finance activity**, **Earnings**, **Currency context**, **Payout setup**, and a legacy Stripe balance load independently. If one area is unavailable, the other sections remain usable.

During a page change, the current finance activity stays visible until the next page is ready. If that request fails, use **Try again** without losing the other sections.
