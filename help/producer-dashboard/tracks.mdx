The **My Beats** page keeps catalog work in one place. Find a beat, play it, review its details, update offers, and connect with collaborators.

<ProducerOnly />



## How to Get Here

Open [My Beats](https://open.beatpass.ca/producer-dashboard/tracks), or choose your **profile avatar** → **Producer Dashboard** → **My Beats**.

The workspace uses your own producer profile. Use **Your catalog**, **Impact**, and **Collaborations** to jump to a section.

## Beat Catalog

**Your catalog** is the main working section. Start with **Search beats**, choose a catalog view, then narrow the **License type** or sort order. Use **Items per page**, **Previous**, and **Next** below the catalog to browse more results.

Each beat shows:

- Artwork and beat name
- BPM, key, scale, and duration
- Collection name or **Single**
- Licensing choice
- **Plays** (all-time audience plays)
- Last update date
- Featured-beat, **Insights**, and **Edit** actions

<Note>
  **Audience plays** counts BeatPass listening activity from people outside your producer team. A real zero stays visible. **Unavailable** means BeatPass could not load that play information.
</Note>

### Filters

- **Needs review**: Beats whose artwork cannot be displayed or whose genre, BPM/key/scale, or licensing information needs review
- **Singles**: Beats that are not in a collection
- **In collections**: beats assigned to a collection
- **Collaborations**: Beats credited to more than one producer

Choose **All beats**, **Needs review**, **Singles**, **In collections**, or **Collaborations** above the search field. Choose **All licenses** to include every licensing choice.

### Licensing Choices

- **Standard**
- **Standard and exclusive**
- **Exclusive only**

Use the sort menu for **Newest first**, **Recently updated**, **Oldest updates**, **Most played**, or **Least played**.

### Actions and Selection

Open the beat's **More** actions menu to find **Edit**, **Insights**, and **View track**. On a phone, these appear in a sheet at the bottom of the screen. Desktop also provides direct **Edit** and **Insights** shortcuts.

<BeatCatalogFigure />

- **Upload beat** opens the beat upload flow.
- The featured-beat area lets you choose an eligible beat for the first spot on your Pro Page.
- **Remove from featured beat** in the beat's actions, or **Remove featured beat** in the featured area, clears your choice so BeatPass can select an available beat.
- **Insights** opens detailed performance information for one beat.
- **Edit** opens the beat editor.
- Choose **Select**, then select beats or use **Select all on this page**. Choose **Edit** for shared license or price changes, or use the delete action and read its confirmation before removing beats. **Done** leaves selection mode.

Collection names open the connected collection. Use **Items per page** to show 10, 20, or 50 beats. Use **Previous** and **Next** below the list to move between pages. The range text shows which beats are currently visible, and **Updating** indicates a background refresh without hiding the current rows.

### Featured Beat

You can save one featured beat for your producer profile. Choosing another replaces the previous choice.

The **Feature on Pro Page** action is available when you are the primary producer, the audio is playable, and at least one active licensing or purchase path exists. For an AI-assisted beat, you must also confirm that you hold the commercial rights. An exclusively sold beat cannot be featured for new customers. When the action is unavailable, its explanation points to the field or offer that needs attention.

The Pro Page uses your featured beat while it remains available. Otherwise, BeatPass chooses from your playable beats with an active buying choice. It balances listener response, freshness, and complete track details instead of choosing only the newest or most-played beat. See [Producer Pro Page](/help/producer-dashboard/pro-page#how-the-automatic-choice-works) for details.

## Edit Several Beats Together

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select your beats">
    Choose **Select**, then select beats on the current page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Edit">
    Enable **Change license availability**, **Set an exclusive price**, or both. Fields you leave off keep their current values.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review changes">
    Check the beats, licensing choice, price per beat, and currency. The same price applies to each selected beat, rather than to the selection as a bundle.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Apply the change">
    Use the **Apply** action shown for your selection. If some beats need attention, follow their explanations, adjust the changes, and review again.
  </Step>
</Steps>

A bulk price change does not complete an unsigned exclusive offer or make an unavailable offer ready for checkout. Review [Setting prices](/help/licensing-for-producers/exclusive-licenses/setting-prices) for signing and delivery requirements.

## Review Details

The upload flow requires cover art, a playable audio file, a producer credit, genre information, BPM, key, scale, rights declarations, and a valid licensing choice before a new beat can be published. **My Beats** does not repeat audio or producer credits as separate warnings. It does flag artwork that can no longer be displayed, which can happen to an older or imported beat after upload.

For older or imported catalog entries, **Needs review** identifies editable information that can affect discovery or licensing:

- **Artwork**: Neither the beat artwork nor its collection artwork can be displayed
- **Genres**: No genre is attached to the beat
- **BPM, key, or scale**: One or more musical details is missing
- **Licensing**: The licensing choice or required exclusive-offer details are incomplete

Rows that need attention state the exact details beside the beat metadata. Use **Edit** to correct them.

## Understand Your Impact

Choose **Impact** to reach **Understand your impact**, then **How it works** for the explanation. **Trending** follows recent BeatPass listening compared with the previous week. **Contribution** uses eligible beats' recency, counted plays, and producer credits to help calculate a share of subscription earnings.

Contribution points are neither cash nor XP. A calculation can differ from the public play total, and no Trending badge does not mean your beat is declining. See [Contribution System](/help/earnings/contribution-system) for the earning rules.

## Collaborations

**Current collaborators** lists producers already credited with you and shows their collaborative-beat counts. Verified profiles keep their blue check beside the producer name. Use **Chat** when the collaborator has one connected producer account, or use **View collaborative beats** to apply the matching catalog view.

**Artists with shared genres** compares the genres saved on producer profiles. Suggestions exclude you and your current collaborators. Each suggestion shows the full number of genres in common and at most two genre names. Use the listed genres to decide which profile to explore.

When a suggested artist has a connected producer account, select **Chat** to open a conversation. Select the artist name to review the profile first.

If your producer profile has no genres, use **Add genres to artist profile** before using this section for suggestions.
