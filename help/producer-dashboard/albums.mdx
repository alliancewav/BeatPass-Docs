The **My Collections** page helps you organize beats into releases and correct missing release details. Open a release, add the right beats, and check its artwork and date before sharing.

<CollectionCatalogFigure />

## How to Get Here

Open [My Collections](https://open.beatpass.ca/producer-dashboard/albums), or choose your **profile avatar** → **Producer Dashboard** → **My Collections**.

The workspace uses your own producer profile. Choose grid or list view to browse your releases in the way that suits you.

## Collections

**Your collections** is the main working section. Use **Search collections**, choose a catalog view, and switch between grid and list. **Items per page**, **Previous**, and **Next** help you browse more releases.

Each collection shows:

- Artwork and collection name
- Release date or **Release date not set**
- Track count, including a real zero
- **Plays** (all-time audience plays across the collection's beats)
- Actions to manage the collection, view it, or open **Insights**

<Note>
  **Audience plays** counts BeatPass listening activity from people outside your producer team. A real zero stays visible. **Unavailable** means BeatPass could not load that play information.
</Note>

### Filters

- **Needs review**: Collections whose artwork cannot be displayed, release date is not set, or beat list is empty
- **Upcoming**: Collections with a future release date
- **Released**: Collections whose release date is today or earlier
- **Empty**: Collections with no beats

Choose **All collections**, **Released**, **Upcoming**, **Needs review**, or **Empty** above the search field.

Use the sort menu to browse by update, name, release date, or plays.

### Actions and Selection

- **Create collection** opens the collection editor.
- **Insights** opens detailed performance information for one collection.
- **Manage collection** or **Manage** opens the collection editor; the label depends on the chosen view.
- Choose **Select**, then select collections or use **Select all on this page**. Use the delete action only after reading its confirmation, including its effect on tracks. **Done** leaves selection mode.

Use **Items per page** to show 10, 20, or 50 collections. Use **Previous** and **Next** below the list to move between pages. The range text shows which collections are currently visible, and **Updating** indicates a background refresh without hiding the current rows.

## Update Several Release Dates

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select collections">
    Choose **Select**, then select the collections on the current page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Edit">
    In **Edit release dates**, choose the new date for the selected collections.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review release dates">
    Check the affected collections and new date before applying the change. Follow any validation message if a collection needs attention.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the saved dates">
    After the save succeeds, review the dates in your catalog. Use **Done** to leave selection mode when needed.
  </Step>
</Steps>

A release date is planning information. It does not schedule or hide a collection, or change its beats' licenses and prices.

## Release Details

**Release details** provides planning guidance and leads back to filtered collections. It does not represent a separate publication gate.

| Issue | What to Review |
| --- | --- |
| **Artwork unavailable** | The collection artwork cannot be displayed |
| **Release date not set** | No release date is set |
| **No beats added** | The collection does not contain any beats |

Select **Artwork unavailable**, **Release date not set**, or **No beats added** to apply the matching **Needs review** filter and return to **Collections**. New collections require artwork, but artwork on an older or imported collection can stop loading later. When current collections have working artwork, a release date, and at least one beat, the section shows **Release details are complete**.

If a future release exists, **Next upcoming release** links to the closest collection by release date. Upcoming and released labels always use the collection's release date, not the date it was created.

## Collections on Your Pro Page

A collection appears on the public Pro Page only when it has public beats that visitors can play and explore. If no usable collection exists, the public page omits the Collections section.

Visitors can open a collection from your Pro Page to hear that release. Show or hide the available Collections section from your Pro Page workspace.

Use collections for a real listening path, such as a beat tape, album, campaign, or focused sound.
