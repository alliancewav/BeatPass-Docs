Pick up your producer work in one place: find a beat, check your audience, or follow an earning. Your **Producer Dashboard** uses your own producer profile; producer access is free.

<ProducerOnly />

<DashboardFigure />

## Open the Dashboard

Open your **profile avatar** menu and choose **Producer Dashboard**, or [go straight to the dashboard](https://open.beatpass.ca/producer-dashboard). On a phone, swipe the producer page links sideways to find more.

The desktop menu groups work into **Catalog**, **Audience**, **Business**, and **Tools**, with **Dashboard** at the top. Use the page name when you are following a guide.

## How the Dashboard Works

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="My producer profile is missing">
    Check the account you are signed in to, then review your [Producer Program application](/help/producer-program). A paid customer subscription does not create producer access.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I have not added anything yet">
    New accounts show genuine empty or setup states. Start with an upload, a payout setup action, or the task shown on that page. Example screenshots use invented records to show what a populated workspace looks like.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="A page cannot refresh">
    **Progress**, **Leaderboards**, and **Finances** keep successfully loaded information visible when a later request is unavailable. Use the page's retry action and read the message before repeating a save or setup step.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
