## Open the Dashboard

Click your **profile avatar** in the top navigation bar, then select **Backstage**. Use the sidebar to navigate between dashboard pages. Producer profile owners can open **Pro Page** immediately after **My Collections**; the page shows whether the selected profile is locked, ready, live, or needs attention.

<Note>
  If your account does not have a linked artist profile, the dashboard shows a "No Artist Profile" state instead of redirecting. Complete your [Producer Program application](/help/producer-program) first.
</Note>

## How the Dashboard Works

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Artist context" icon="user" defaultOpen>
    Most pages load data for your linked artist profile. **Pro Page**, **My Beats**, **My Collections**, **Finances**, and **Leaderboards** can show an artist switcher when your account manages more than one artist. Pro Page, My Beats, and My Collections keep that choice in the URL. The **Progress** ranking remains account-wide.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Overview first, editing second" icon="table-columns">
    The dashboard is designed for quick stats and navigation. Uploading, editing tracks, and full insights live in the backstage editing tools.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Empty states" icon="circle-info">
    If you do not have uploads, downloads, albums, achievements, or payout setup yet, you will see empty or setup states instead of placeholder data.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Loading and update behavior" icon="arrows-rotate">
    Dashboard pages update in the background. **Progress**, **Leaderboards**, and **Finances** keep successfully loaded information visible when a later request is unavailable.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
