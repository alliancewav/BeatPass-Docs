BeatPass 4.2.1 helps you move from finding a beat to preparing your next release. Listening, producer tools and license follow-up have clearer controls and more useful next steps.

## What's New

- **Keep listening your way.** Playback controls fit around your queue, and mobile lyrics and timed waveform comments are easier to use. Library search, playlist privacy and navigation are more consistent.
- **Take control of your catalog.** In **My Beats**, select the beats you want to manage and review shared licensing or price changes before applying them. **My Collections** previews release-date changes. Featured-beat controls connect your producer profile and Pro Page.
- **Find your next goal.** Explore achievement artwork, search your collection and choose a goal in **Progress**. Your chosen goal stays on this device when browser storage is available. **Leaderboards** explain the ranking, period and snapshot behind your position.
- **Read the report you need.** **Insights** separates **Overview** and **Audience**, with a dedicated **Pro Page** tab for your own primary producer profile. Clearer listening metrics and **What counts?** explanations help you interpret the results.
- **Send the beat that fits.** Beat requests and your saved requests share a more focused layout. Producers can **Review & send** selected beats or use **Upload & send**. If an uploaded beat is saved but not sent, the next step is explained.
- **Follow your license through.** License cards explain missing details, producer review and available certificate actions. Confirm legal identity through the action your account offers, and review exclusive terms and promised files before payment. Existing grants keep their accepted terms.
- **Know what you’re agreeing to.** Policies have separate English and French pages. Read the full agreement and choose its language before creating an account, subscribing or confirming a paid plan change. Review what is due now, any adjustment, and the next payment before confirming. Keep your downloadable agreement copy.
- **Recover without starting over.** Checkout distinguishes completed, pending and failed payments. Check a pending plan change’s status before trying again. Payout setup, conversations, notifications and producer videos have clearer loading, recovery and saved-state feedback.

## Why It Matters

- Find the right screen and understand what an action changes.
- Keep catalog edits, payment progress and license records easier to follow.
- Use the current report and its definitions before drawing conclusions.

## Try It

1. Open your **profile avatar**, then **My library**, or choose **Library** on your phone.
2. Producers can open **Producer Dashboard** for **My Beats** and **Progress**. Use **Insights** on desktop, or **View performance** on a ready Pro Page.
3. Follow the visible status and next action when a license or payment needs attention.

[Explore the Help Center](/help), [review your certificates](/help/downloads-and-licensing/license-certificates), or [read Insights](/help/analytics/getting-started).
