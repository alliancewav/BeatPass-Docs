BeatPass 4.2.1 helps you move from finding a beat to preparing your next release. Listening, producer tools and license follow-up have clearer controls and more useful next steps.

## What's New

- **Keep listening your way.** Playback controls fit around your queue, and mobile lyrics and timed waveform comments are easier to use. Library search, playlist privacy and navigation are more consistent.
- **Take control of your catalog.** In **My Beats**, select the beats you want to manage and review shared licensing or price changes before applying them. **My Collections** previews release-date changes. Featured-beat controls connect your producer profile and Pro Page.
- **Give your work the right credits.** Spotify credits have their own cover-led cards and lists, distinct from playable BeatPass Collections. In Backstage, search and filter a paged portfolio, select projects and review bulk role additions or credit removal. Album edits stay limited to the songs and releases you have already credited. Shared song and album credits connect registered producers through their public profiles, with each producer’s own roles shown.
- **Explore collections without losing your place.** Artist-profile Collection lists bring playback, release details and an expandable track list together. Grid and list pagination keep the same page size as you continue browsing.
- **Keep your Pro Page in balance.** Beats and Collections use consistently sized carousel cards that fit the available space on phones, tablets and desktop screens. Beat details brings listening, musical facts and the selected license action together; exact terms remain available before purchase.
- **Find your next goal.** Explore achievement artwork, search your collection and choose a goal in **Progress**. Your chosen goal stays on this device when browser storage is available. **Leaderboards** explain the ranking, period and snapshot behind your position.
- **Read the report you need.** **Insights** separates **Overview** and **Audience**, with a dedicated **Pro Page** tab for your own primary producer profile. Clearer listening metrics and **What counts?** explanations help you interpret the results.
- **Send the beat that fits.** Beat requests and your saved requests share a more focused layout. Producers can **Review & send** selected beats or use **Upload & send**. If an uploaded beat is saved but not sent, the next step is explained.
- **Follow your license through.** License cards explain missing details, producer review and available certificate actions. Confirm legal identity through the action your account offers, and review exclusive terms and promised files before payment. Existing grants keep their accepted terms.
- **Know what you’re agreeing to.** Policies have separate English and French pages. Read the full agreement and choose its language when creating an account. Your previously issued agreement copies remain available.
- **Join without an extra agreement step.** Email and new social signup use the configured site-policy checkbox instead of a full private-agreement review and copy popup. Your selected plan stays with you through sign-in and email verification. Existing social accounts keep their normal sign-in path, and verified-account checks protect new social registrations. Ordinary signup also lets you correct a prefilled email.
- **Get your pass with fewer steps.** Subscription checkout opens payment without asking for a separate contract name, postal address or another agreement checkbox. Plan changes also skip those fields, while keeping a clear review of adjustments, renewal and timing before confirmation. Exclusive beat purchases keep their own license and buyer-detail steps.
- **Keep your pass in view.** Checkout keeps the selected pass beside payment on desktop when it fits, and groups payment help and policies into one compact disclosure. After confirmed payment, return to your track or request, browse beats, and find your licenses and included Beat Requests without another setup form. Producer accounts get links to their workspace instead. An empty license library keeps the focus on finding music, with name confirmation appearing when licenses or an explicit recovery link need it.
- **Recover without starting over.** Checkout distinguishes completed, pending and failed payments. Check a pending plan change’s status before trying again. Payout setup, conversations, notifications and producer videos have clearer loading, recovery and saved-state feedback.

## Why It Matters

- Find the right screen and understand what an action changes.
- Keep catalog edits, payment progress and license records easier to follow.
- Use the current report and its definitions before drawing conclusions.

## Try It

1. Open your **profile avatar**, then **My library**, or choose **Library** on your phone.
2. Producers can open **Producer Dashboard** for **My Beats** and **Progress**. Use **Insights** on desktop, or **View performance** on a ready Pro Page.
3. Follow the visible status and next action when a license or payment needs attention.
