# A Smoother Beat Upload & Showcase

## Summary

BeatPass 3.4.0 makes publishing a beat clearer from the first WAV drop to the final confirmation. It also gives every beat page a stronger producer showcase, clearer licensing information, and more useful license-certificate access.

---

## What's New

- **Guided publishing** — The upload form now opens one focused section at a time, shows section progress, keeps your completed work available for review, and locks safely while **Publishing your track**
- **Creation declaration** — Every new upload records whether the beat is **Human-made** or **AI-assisted**. AI-assisted beats also record the tool, its role, and your commercial-use confirmation
- **More inspiring cover art** — **Cover Art & Style** offers a faster, touch-friendly preset browser with genre matching, pagination, animated circular previews, and a clear path to upload your own artwork
- **Clearer exclusive setup** — **Licensing Options** brings the model, price, availability, financial terms, buyer permissions, and legal details into one guided flow
- **Buyer-file guarantee** — Selecting **Standard + Exclusive** or **Exclusive Only** includes WAV and stems automatically, then asks you to confirm the files you promise to deliver after a sale
- **BEATPAY-ready exclusive offers** — Eligible producers can publish exclusive beats while their standard licensing availability is managed separately in **Finances**
- **Better beat pages** — Licensing actions are easier to scan, **BeatPass ID** brings license terms and track verification together, and the producer section now highlights your bio, verified badge, location, social links, and follow action
- **Stronger producer profiles** — Professional credits feel more like career highlights, while **New Beats** stays readable across mobile and smaller desktop screens
- **Improved certificates** — Producers can download certificates for their beats, and buyers can confirm their legal name once to refresh all of their existing certificates

---

## How to Use

1. Open **Backstage**, choose **Upload**, and drop your WAV file.
2. Complete each section, including **Protection & Verification** and your creation declaration.
3. If you offer an exclusive license, review **Licensing Options** and confirm the promised buyer files.
4. Select **Save Track**. BeatPass keeps the form locked until your confirmation page is ready.
