# Album & Track Management Fixes

<Info>
  **Hotfix Release** — Critical bug fixes for album and track management released immediately following the December 2025 Production Launch.
</Info>

---

## Summary

This hotfix addresses several issues discovered during the production launch, primarily affecting album creation and track management workflows.

---

## Bug Fixes

### New Tracks Appearing on All Channels

**Issue:** Newly uploaded tracks weren't appearing on certain channel pages (like Discover) even though they showed up on others (like the New Beats channel).

**Fix:** Newly uploaded tracks now appear immediately across all channel pages, including Discover.

---

### Album Creation Errors

**Issue:** Creating an album with existing tracks could fail with an error.

**Fix:** Albums can now be created with existing tracks reliably.

---

### Duplicate Track Creation

**Issue:** Adding existing tracks to an album created duplicate copies instead of linking the tracks you already had.

**Fix:** Existing tracks are now correctly linked to the album instead of being duplicated.

---

### Track Metadata Loss

**Issue:** Adding existing tracks to an album removed their associated artists, tags, and genres.

**Fix:** Tracks now keep their artists, tags, and genres when added to an album.

---

### Album Metadata Preservation

**Issue:** Updating an album could unintentionally clear its artists, tags, or genres if they weren't included in the update.

**Fix:** Albums now keep their artists, tags, and genres during updates unless you change them yourself.

---

### Notification Crash Prevention

**Issue:** Creating an album without artists could cause an error when notifying followers.

**Fix:** Albums without artists now save correctly, and followers are notified only when there's an artist to notify.

---

## Verification

After these fixes:

- New tracks appear immediately on all channel pages, including Discover
- Albums can be created with existing tracks without errors
- Existing tracks keep their artists, tags, and genres when added to albums
- Albums keep their details during updates
- Album creation works reliably, even in unusual situations
