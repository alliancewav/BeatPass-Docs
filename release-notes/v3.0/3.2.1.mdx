# BEATPAY Rollout & Producer Dashboard Refresh

## Summary

BeatPass 3.2.1 starts the gradual **BEATPAY** bank payout rollout, powered by Wise, for eligible producers. If BEATPAY is available to you, **Finances** shows a new **Set up BEATPAY** option. If you do not see that option yet, nothing changes: keep using your current payout setup.

The Producer Dashboard also feels cleaner across desktop and mobile. Navigation is easier to scan, **Finances** explains payout readiness more clearly, and progress, activity, catalog, and leaderboard views are easier to use.

<Note>
  BEATPAY is rolling out slowly. Existing Stripe Connect payouts continue to work while BeatPass expands access.
</Note>

---

## BEATPAY Bank Payouts

- **Guided bank payout setup** - Eligible producers can start **Set up BEATPAY** from **Finances** and complete bank payout setup from the Producer Dashboard
- **Powered by Wise** - BEATPAY uses Wise for bank payouts while BeatPass shows only the payout status and masked account details you need to recognize the setup
- **Current payouts continue** - Existing Stripe Connect payout setups keep working during the rollout
- **Clear setup steps** - The BEATPAY flow walks through **Payout currency**, **Continue to bank details**, **Save bank details**, and **Done**
- **Currency context** - Eligible producers can review exchange-rate movement and estimated arrival timing before payout money moves
- **Payout updates** - Producers can receive **Payout Updates** when a BEATPAY payout is sent or needs attention

---

## Producer Dashboard Refresh

- **Easier dashboard navigation** - Producer pages now use a cleaner desktop sidebar and mobile navigation chips for **Dashboard**, **Finances**, **Activity**, **Progress**, **Leaderboards**, **My Beats**, and **My Collections**
- **Helpful section shortcuts** - Pages can show quick section chips such as **Status**, **Overview**, **Transactions**, **Rates**, **Earnings**, **Level**, **Actions**, **Achievements**, and **Leaderboard**
- **Clearer Finances status** - **Finances** now separates **Active** and **Setup Required** states, shows current subscription share when available, and highlights BEATPAY setup for eligible producers
- **Smoother progress and rankings** - **Progress** has fresher sync details and a manual **Refresh** action, while **Leaderboards** switch categories with less loading flash
- **Better activity and catalog summaries** - Downloads, tracks, albums, and dashboard stats now use more consistent cards and recent activity views
- **Player no longer covers dashboard pages** - Account settings and Producer Dashboard pages have more breathing room when the web player is active

---

## Other Improvements

- **More accurate trend signals** - Track trend badges, trending producer ranking, and album popularity bars now better separate recent BeatPass momentum from lifetime plays
- **More reliable billing totals** - Subscription renewals, coupon checkout totals, and <Tooltip tip="A fee added on top of eligible paid checkouts to support secure billing and platform operations. It is shown before you confirm payment.">platform fee</Tooltip> records stay aligned with the amount charged
- **Stronger account and checkout protections** - Sign-in, payment confirmations, comments, recovery-code visibility, trusted links, and uploads received behind-the-scenes safety improvements
- **More predictable uploads and browsing** - Upload duplicate checks are clearer, collection browsing is more stable, and empty albums or playlists no longer look playable
- **Contribution payouts stay familiar** - Contribution and payout formulas are unchanged in this release

---

## What This Means For You

- **If you see Set up BEATPAY** - Follow the setup shown in **Finances** to prepare bank payouts
- **If you do not see BEATPAY yet** - Keep using your current payout setup; no action is needed
- **If you already use Stripe Connect** - Existing Stripe Connect payouts continue during the rollout
- **If you manage your catalog daily** - The Producer Dashboard should feel easier to move through, especially on mobile
