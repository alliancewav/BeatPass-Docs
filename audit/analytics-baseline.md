# Mintlify analytics review

Period: 2026-05-13 through 2026-08-11 (90 days)

Source: aggregate Mintlify page, search, feedback, and flow analytics for the `beatpass` deployment, reviewed on 2026-08-11.

This public artifact records the method and conclusions only. Raw traffic counts, search terms, feedback entries, flows, and visitor data are intentionally not committed.

## Architecture conclusions

- The Help hub is the primary documentation destination and remains the main task router.
- Developer overview and authentication reference traffic support a compact developer entry path with authentication near the top.
- License certificates, Getting Started, account deletion, BeatPay, producer Pro Page guidance, plans, beat requests, license verification, and producer requirements are independently valuable destinations and remain available.
- None of the thin notification, channel, plan-tier, payment-method, leaderboard, or profile-element leaves selected for consolidation appeared among the most-viewed pages.
- Search traffic supported retaining explicit BeatPay, producer-program, payout, analytics, and non-exclusive-license terminology in headings, descriptions, or glossary content.
- Feedback volume was not sufficient to rank consolidation candidates. Thin-page length, duplication, workflow distinctness, search value, and redirect preservation therefore remain the fallback decision rules.

## Review method

The review included:

1. Popular pages for human and agent traffic.
2. Search queries, click-through destinations, and zero-result searches.
3. Session entry points and next-page flow data.
4. Detailed feedback totals and source categories.
5. A comparison between high-value destinations and the proposed redirect map.

Re-evaluate after the new architecture has collected 90 days of traffic. Compare redirect landings, search click-through, zero-result topics, task-hub entry flows, and page feedback before further consolidation.
